Heng Sheng Supermarket

Tanki Leendert 192-A, Noord, Aruba

Open: Daily 8am–9pm

Phone: +297 587 0188

Directions

Heng Sheng is a neighbourhood supermarket on Tanki Leendert in Noord, best known for its Asian groceries such as fresh dumplings, sauces and specialty snacks, alongside everyday basics. Its wine shelf is modest but useful, with around 30 wines listed in OB Sommelier across red, white and rosé. A handy stop if you are cooking an Asian-style dinner and want a bottle to go with it.
