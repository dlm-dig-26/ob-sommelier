Cmart Aruba

Cumana 38, Oranjestad

Open: Mon–Sat 7:00–21:00 · Sun 9:00–18:00

Phone: +297 597 9847

Directions

Cmart is a large one-stop store at Cumana 38 in Oranjestad, a few minutes from the airport, combining a full supermarket with home goods, clothing and appliances. Aruba's tourism site counts it among the island's big supermarkets. Its wine range in OB Sommelier is sizeable, with around 70 bottles listed, all red at the moment. A practical first stop for supplies after landing.
