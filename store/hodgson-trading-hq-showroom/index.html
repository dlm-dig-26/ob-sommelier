Pedro Gallegostraat 4 (My Computer building, near Dakota), Oranjestad

Open: Mon–Fri 9am–6pm · Sat 9am–4pm · Sun closed

Phone: +297 587 1318

Directions

Hodgson Trading is a wine importer with a showroom at Pedro Gallegostraat 4 in Oranjestad, in the My Computer building near Dakota. Unlike a supermarket, the showroom is all about wine: around 100 bottles are listed in OB Sommelier, covering red, white, rosé and sparkling, with a strong selection from Argentina. Plan your visit for a weekday or Saturday, as the showroom is closed on Sunday.
