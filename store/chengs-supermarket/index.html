Chengs' Supermarket

Bubali 111, Noord

Open: Mon, Wed–Sat 7:30am–9pm · Tue 7am–9pm · Sun 8:30am–9pm

Directions

Chengs' is a supermarket at Bubali 111 in Noord, close to the hotel area, with groceries, snacks and everyday convenience items. For wine it carries one of the larger supermarket ranges in OB Sommelier, with well over 100 wines listed: red, white, rosé, sparkling and dessert wines. A practical choice if you are staying near the beaches and want a bottle without driving into Oranjestad.
