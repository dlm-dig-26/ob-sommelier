Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026. Pilot version for Aruba.

The short version

You can use OB Sommelier without an account. We don’t collect your name, email or phone number when you search. Your location stays on your phone, except for one short check when you report whether a wine is on the shelf, and even then we don’t store it.

What we collect when you search

  • What you searched for (the text, or the text read from a label photo), which wine it matched, and whether a store nearby had it. We use this to grow the catalog and to tell stores which wines people are looking for. It is not linked to your name or device.
  • Label photos are read on your phone. The photo itself is never uploaded.
  • Your location is used on your phone to measure distances to stores. It is not sent to us when you search.

When you tap “Found it” or “Not there”

  • Your phone sends its location once, so we can check you are at the store (within 250 m). We only keep the result of the check, not the location.
  • We store the report (found or not there), which store and wine it was for, the time, and a random device code created by the app on your phone. The code lets us limit how many reports one phone can make. It does not identify you, and you can reset it by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Stored on your phone only

Your age confirmation, chosen search radius, whether you allowed location, the random device code, and when you last saw a promotion. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes all of it.

Stores and contributors

  • Store owners sign in with an email link. We keep the email to let you sign in. Your store’s name, address, location, phone and wine prices are shown publicly in the app.
  • Invited contributors sign in with an email link. Shelf photos you upload are stored privately, read to record which wines a store carries, and used to resolve mistakes. Ask us to delete your photos or account at any time.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the app’s data (servers in the United States) and, like any web service, keeps short-term technical logs that include IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the app’s fonts and code libraries, so they see your IP address like any website you visit.
  • Google Maps opens only when you tap a store for directions.
  • TheMealDB is contacted only when you tap “Recipe ideas”.

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use advertising networks or tracking cookies. Promotions in the app are for wine stores and are always labelled “Sponsored”.

Your choices

You can use the app with location turned off (distances are then measured from Oranjestad). Store owners and contributors can ask for their account and data to be deleted. Questions or requests: dlm.digid@gmail.com.

Terms of use

  • 18 and over. OB Sommelier is about alcoholic drinks and is only for people of legal drinking age (18 in Aruba). Please drink responsibly.
  • Information only. We don’t sell wine. Prices, stock and “seen on the shelf” information come from stores, shoppers and shelf photos, and can be wrong or out of date. Check with the store before you go. A sighting marked “last known” may no longer be in stock.
  • Pairing suggestions are general guidance, not professional advice.
  • Be honest with reports. Only report what you actually saw in the store. We may ignore or remove reports, listings or photos that look wrong or abusive.
  • Stores are responsible for the prices and information they publish. Sponsored placements are labelled and never change which wine is shown as available or cheapest.
  • No guarantee. The app is provided as it is during the pilot and may change or be unavailable at times. We are not responsible for losses from relying on its information.
  • Changes. We may update these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version.
