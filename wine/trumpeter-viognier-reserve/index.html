What to eat with it

  1. Pisca hasa. A medium-bodied, aromatic white has enough weight for fried fish and its tangy creole sauce.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets with lime, salt and garlic, dust with flour and fry. Top with a sauce of onion, sweet pepper, tomato and a little vinegar.
  2. Sopi di pisca. Soft stone fruit and floral notes sit nicely with a light, herby fish soup.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, garlic, celery, tomato, potato and a little hot pepper in stock for 20 minutes. Finish with lime juice.
  3. Mild chicken curry. Viognier's ripe fruit and rounder acidity soften gentle spice and coconut cream.
    Recipe idea: Cook onion, garlic and ginger, add curry powder and chicken, pour in coconut milk and simmer 25 minutes. Serve with rice.
  4. Roast pork with apricot glaze. Apricot and peach notes typical of Viognier echo the fruity glaze on juicy pork.
    Recipe idea: Roast a seasoned pork loin at 190 C, brushing with warmed apricot jam mixed with mustard for the last 20 minutes.
  5. Grilled shrimp with mango salsa. Tropical fruit in the salsa matches the wine's aromatic style, and the shrimp stays light.
    Recipe idea: Grill skewered shrimp brushed with oil and lime. Serve with diced mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice and a little chili.
