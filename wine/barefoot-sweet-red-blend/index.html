About this wine

Barefoot began in 1965, when Davis Bynum made its first wine in a garage he had turned into a winery in California. In 1986 the company became Barefoot Cellars and created its footprint label, a nod to the old custom of crushing grapes by foot. Barefoot joined E. & J. Gallo Winery in 2005 and is based in Modesto, California. The Sweet Red Blend is a non-vintage California red made from grapes such as Zinfandel, Pinot Noir, Barbera, Grenache and Petite Sirah, in a soft, fruity and sweet style.

On the shelf: Look for the Barefoot name and the bare footprint logo that appears on every bottle.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Gentle sweetness and soft tannins suit the salty cheese and the sweet-savoury filling with raisins and olives.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or slices of Gouda with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, then bake 30 minutes until melted.
  2. Pastechi. Juicy red fruit and a touch of sweetness balance salty fried pastry with spiced meat.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with spiced ground beef or cheese, fold, seal with a fork and fry until golden; serve warm.
  3. Barbecue ribs. Sweet fruit matches a sticky, smoky barbecue glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, brown sugar and salt, bake covered 2 hours at 150°C, then brush with barbecue sauce and grill until sticky.
  4. Sweet chili chicken. Sweetness tames the chili heat while the fruit stands up to the glaze.
    Recipe idea: Fry bite-size chicken pieces, toss in sweet chili sauce with garlic and lime, and serve with rice and spring onion.
  5. Dark chocolate brownies. A sweet, berry-driven red that echoes chocolate desserts instead of clashing with them.
    Recipe idea: Melt dark chocolate with butter, whisk in sugar, eggs and a little flour, bake 25 minutes at 180°C and cool before slicing.
