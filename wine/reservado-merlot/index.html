About this wine

Reservado is an everyday range from Concha y Toro, a Chilean wine company that dates its history to 1883. This Merlot comes from the Central Valley, from alluvial and colluvial soils, and winemaker Marcelo Gangas ages it briefly in stainless steel and concrete tanks rather than oak, keeping the focus on fresh, ripe fruit. The 2022 vintage won a bronze medal at The Global Merlot Masters in 2023.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Fresh red fruit and soft tannins sit well with chicken in tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a pinch of cumin, then simmer until tender and saucy. Serve with white rice.
  2. Pastechi with beef. An easy, fruity red suits savoury fried pastries.
    Recipe idea: Fill small pastry half-moons with seasoned cheese, tuna or ground beef, seal the edges with a fork and fry or bake until golden.
  3. Pasta with tomato sauce. Fresh acidity matches tomatoes and the fruit keeps it friendly.
    Recipe idea: Simmer canned tomatoes with garlic, olive oil, basil and salt for 20 minutes and toss with spaghetti and parmesan.
  4. Pork chops. A medium-bodied Merlot flatters juicy pork without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Season pork chops with salt, pepper and thyme, pan-sear 4 minutes a side and finish with butter and garlic.
  5. Chicken quesadillas. Unoaked, fruity red works with cheese and mild spice.
    Recipe idea: Fill tortillas with shredded chicken, cheese, peppers and onion, fold and toast in a dry pan until crisp and melted.
