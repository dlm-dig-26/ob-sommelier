About this wine

Partridge is the entry-level line of Viña Las Perdices, the family winery in Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo, founded in 2004 by the sons of Don Juan Munoz-Lopez, who came from Andalucía in the early 1950s to grow grapes. The line was first made in 2009 for export and in 2018 was also launched in Argentina, with a Malbec, a Cabernet Sauvignon and this Chardonnay, all from the family's own vineyards in Agrelo. The idea is a young, honest wine that lets the grape speak.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Baked gouda filled with spiced chicken needs a round white with enough fruit and acidity to refresh the cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed gouda or cheese slices with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, raisins and capers, then bake until the cheese melts.
  2. Grilled red snapper. Lemon and apple notes suit simply grilled fish, and the body holds up to a little char.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, and grill 6 to 8 minutes per side. Serve with a lime wedge.
  3. Lemon butter scallops. The citrus in the wine echoes the lemon, and its soft texture matches the butter.
    Recipe idea: Sear dry scallops in a hot pan 2 minutes per side, then add butter, lemon juice and parsley and spoon the sauce over.
  4. Roast herb chicken. A dry, medium-bodied Chardonnay is a classic partner for roast chicken with herbs.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, garlic, thyme and rosemary, roast at 200 °C for about an hour and rest before carving.
  5. Creamy mushroom pasta. A mild cream sauce with mushrooms goes well with the soft apple and beeswax notes.
    Recipe idea: Sauté mushrooms with garlic, add cream and parmesan, simmer briefly and toss with tagliatelle and black pepper.
