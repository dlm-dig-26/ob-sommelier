About this wine

Brothers Pablo and Héctor Durigutti started Proyecto Las Compuertas in 2007, when they bought an old Malbec vineyard close to their winery in Las Compuertas, the coolest corner of Luján de Cuyo in Mendoza. The project sets out to protect the winemaking heritage of the area and support local growers. The winery calls Blanco de Finca the first white wine of the Las Compuertas appellation. The 2023 vintage is 90 percent Marsanne and 10 percent Semillon from organically farmed vines on their Finca Victoria, planted in 2019. The grapes are picked by hand, fermented with native yeasts in unlined cement eggs and aged 12 months on fine lees.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Citrus notes and fresh acidity match lime-cured fish, and the creamy texture softens the raw onion.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili and cilantro; rest 15 minutes and serve cold.
  2. Grilled red snapper. Pear, citrus and a light touch of spice suit the sweet, delicate flesh of grilled snapper.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with garlic, lime and salt, grill 6 to 8 minutes a side and serve with lime wedges.
  3. Keshi yena with chicken. Medium body and a lees-rich texture handle melted Gouda, while the acidity keeps each bite fresh.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives and raisins, top with cheese and bake 30 minutes.
  4. Herb roast chicken. Floral, stone-fruit flavors and a creamy texture suit golden roast chicken.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, thyme, lemon and garlic, then roast at 200 °C for about 1 hour 15 minutes.
  5. Shrimp risotto. A rounded texture and fresh acidity balance a creamy rice dish with sweet shellfish.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio rice in butter, add hot stock little by little, stir in shrimp near the end and finish with lemon zest and parmesan.
