About this wine

Barefoot Cellars is a California wine brand based in Modesto. Fruitscato is its range of sweet Moscato-style wines with added natural fruit flavours, and Strawberry is one of nine flavours in the line. It is light and lightly fizzy, with ripe strawberry up front, and at 7.5% alcohol it is lower in alcohol than most table wines. It has a screw cap and is best served well chilled. Barefoot itself suggests it with spicy or savoury food such as barbecue or Asian dishes.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Spicy shrimp curry. Sweetness and low alcohol calm chili heat while strawberry lifts the coconut.
    Recipe idea: Cook shrimp in coconut milk with red curry paste, garlic, ginger and a little lime, and serve with rice and fresh cilantro.
  2. Pastechi. Light bubbles and sweet fruit contrast nicely with salty, spiced fillings.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with spiced ground beef, cheese or tuna, fold into half moons, seal the edges and deep-fry until golden.
  3. Cala. A chilled, sweet sparkle cools the pepper in these crisp bean fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, rub off the skins, blend with onion, hot pepper and salt, whisk until airy and fry spoonfuls until golden.
  4. Tropical fruit salad. Strawberry and watermelon notes blend right into fresh tropical fruit.
    Recipe idea: Cut mango, pineapple, papaya and strawberries into pieces, toss with lime juice and a little mint, and chill before serving.
  5. Coconut flan. A sweet wine for a sweet dessert: its bubbles lighten the creamy flan.
    Recipe idea: Blend eggs, condensed milk, coconut milk and grated coconut, pour into a caramel-lined mould and bake in a water bath at 170°C for 50 minutes.
