About this wine

Rose Gold is a sparkling rosé from Bottega, part of its Il Vino dei Poeti range, made entirely from Pinot Noir from northern Italy. The grapes get a short cold soak on the skins for colour, are fermented in steel, then given a second fermentation in pressure tanks by the Charmat method. The result is a light, fresh, pale pink sparkler with fine bubbles.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Crisp brut bubbles and red berry notes lift lime-cured fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice and leave 15 minutes, then mix in red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt. Serve cold.
  2. Pastechi. Fine bubbles clean up fried pastry and salty cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill small pastry half-moons with seasoned cheese, tuna or ground beef, seal the edges with a fork and fry or bake until golden.
  3. Grilled shrimp. Light and dry, it matches sweet, garlicky shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Toss shrimp with olive oil, garlic, paprika and salt, grill 2 minutes a side and finish with lime and parsley.
  4. Sushi. Delicate fruit and high acidity suit raw fish and rice.
    Recipe idea: Press seasoned sushi rice onto nori, add fresh tuna or salmon and cucumber, roll tightly and slice. Serve with soy sauce.
  5. Strawberry salad with goat cheese. Wild strawberry notes echo the fruit in a fresh salad.
    Recipe idea: Toss mixed greens with sliced strawberries, crumbled goat cheese, toasted nuts and a light balsamic dressing.
