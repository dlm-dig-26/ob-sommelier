About this wine

This Reserva Pinot Noir is part of the Casillero del Diablo range from Concha y Toro, a Chilean wine company that traces its history to 1883. Winemaker Sebastián Rodríguez sources grapes from leading Chilean wine valleys on alluvial soils and ages the wine in oak barrels, which adds a light toasty note. The result is an elegant, fresh red with soft tannins. The range is named after the old legend of a devil guarding the Concha y Toro cellar.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. A light Pinot with fresh acidity is gentle enough for grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole red snapper with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, grill over medium heat for about 8 minutes per side and finish with a squeeze of lime.
  2. Galinja stoba. Red berry fruit and soft tannins suit chicken in a mild tomato stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a pinch of cumin, then simmer until tender and saucy. Serve with white rice.
  3. Pork tenderloin. Lean pork and fresh red fruit are a classic easy match.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork tenderloin with garlic, thyme and olive oil, sear on all sides and roast at 200C for 15 minutes. Rest and slice.
  4. Roast chicken with herbs. Light body and lively acidity keep roast chicken juicy and bright.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, lemon, garlic and herbs and roast at 200C for about 75 minutes until the juices run clear.
  5. Tuna steak. A delicate red with cherry notes matches meaty, seared tuna.
    Recipe idea: Coat tuna steaks with sesame seeds and pepper, sear 1 minute a side in a very hot pan and serve sliced with soy and lime.
