About this wine

Ménage à Trois is a California brand from Trinchero Family Estates. Silk was launched in 2015 as a softer companion to the darker Midnight blend. It brings together Pinot Noir, Malbec and Petite Sirah from the Central Coast, North Coast and Lodi-Delta, with a hint of French and American oak. The result is silky and smooth, with cherry, ripe raspberry, dried violets, rose petals and gentle spice.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled salmon. Pinot Noir in the blend and soft tannins make this red friendly to rich fish.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with olive oil, salt, pepper and a little honey, grill skin-side down 6 minutes, flip and cook 2 minutes more.
  2. Galinja stoba. Red cherry and gentle spice suit chicken in a lightly sweet tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then simmer with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic, a little sugar and capers for about 45 minutes.
  3. Carni stoba. Supple and fruity, it carries a homestyle beef stew without heaviness.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms bring out the floral, savoury side of the wine.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio rice in butter with onion, add sautéed mushrooms and hot stock ladle by ladle for 18 minutes, finish with Parmesan.
  5. Roast chicken. A light, silky red that will not overpower herb-roasted chicken.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, thyme and lemon, roast at 200°C for about 1 hour 15 minutes until the juices run clear.
