About this wine

J. Lohr is a family-owned California winery that farms estate vineyards in Monterey, Paso Robles and St. Helena, and began planting in Paso Robles in 1986. Seven Oaks Cabernet Sauvignon followed with its first vintage in 1987 and is named after a stand of oak trees that still grows on the property. The grapes come from the winery's own Paso Robles vineyards, and the wine spends 12 months in oak barrels. The 2023 vintage is mostly Cabernet Sauvignon with small parts of Petite Sirah, Petit Verdot, Merlot and Cabernet Franc, and every bottle is Certified California Sustainable.

On the shelf: The label was redesigned in 2026 to highlight its Paso Robles roots, so older and newer label designs may both be on shelves.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled rib-eye steak. Ripe black fruit and toasty oak are a natural match for charred, well-marbled beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a rib-eye with salt and pepper, grill 4 to 5 minutes per side over high heat and rest 5 minutes. Finish with garlic butter.
  2. Keshi yena. Baked cheese with spiced meat filling has the richness to meet a full Cabernet.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken or beef cooked with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, cover with cheese and bake 30 minutes at 180 C.
  3. Carni stoba. The wine's body and vanilla notes suit a deep, slow-cooked beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a glass of red. Simmer covered 2 hours and serve with rice or funchi.
  4. Braised short ribs. Soft, plush tannins and dark cherry fruit carry the richness of slow-braised beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown short ribs, add onion, carrot, garlic, tomato paste and red wine. Cover and braise at 160 C for 3 hours until falling off the bone.
  5. Aged cheddar burger. A juicy Cabernet with toasty notes loves sharp cheese and grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Grill seasoned beef patties 4 minutes per side, top with aged cheddar to melt and serve on toasted buns with caramelised onions.
