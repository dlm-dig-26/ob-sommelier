About this wine

Rutini Wines traces its roots to Felipe Rutini, an Italian from the Marche region who settled in Mendoza and founded Bodega La Rural in Maipú in 1885. The family later planted vineyards in the cooler, higher Uco Valley, where the company now also runs a winery in Tupungato. This Malbec belongs to the Single Vineyard range, which picks out individual high-altitude parcels in the Uco Valley. The grapes come from one vineyard in Paraje Altamira, at over 1,100 metres, and the wine is aged in French oak barrels under head winemaker Mariano Di Paola. Expect deep plum and blackberry fruit with violet and a silky, polished structure.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe dark fruit and rounded tannins stand up to slow-braised goat with cumin and pepper.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a little stock; simmer covered 2 hours until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Plush fruit and gentle spice echo a rich Aruban beef stew without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Sear cubed beef, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, capers and olives, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours. Add a few potatoes at the end.
  3. Grilled ribeye steak. Classic Argentine match: the tannins meet the char and fat, the fruit keeps every bite juicy.
    Recipe idea: Rub ribeye with salt, pepper and garlic; grill over high heat 4 minutes a side, rest 5 minutes and spoon over chimichurri of parsley, oregano, garlic, vinegar and oil.
  4. Lamb chops with rosemary. Violet and dark berry notes flatter rosemary and the sweet richness of lamb.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chops with rosemary, garlic, lemon zest and olive oil for an hour; sear 3 minutes a side and rest briefly before serving.
  5. Pastechi with spiced beef. A savoury spiced filling and crisp pastry suit a fruity, smooth Malbec served slightly cool.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with ground beef cooked with onion, sweet pepper, raisins and a pinch of nutmeg; fold, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
