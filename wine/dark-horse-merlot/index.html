About this wine

Dark Horse is a California wine brand from E. & J. Gallo, based in Modesto. Head winemaker Beth Liston and her team buy fruit from many growers and blend it, fine-tuning details such as fermentation temperature and oak. For the 2022 Merlot, a warmer than usual season led to an early harvest, and the wine was fermented warm and then aged. It is medium bodied, with dark cherry, blackberry and a touch of violet, silky tannins, and a finish of toasted almond, mocha and a hint of cinnamon.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Plum and black cherry with soft tannins suit tender, slow-braised beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown cubed beef, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato and a splash of red wine, then simmer slowly until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Galinja stoba. A medium-bodied red with silky tannins that does not overwhelm stewed chicken.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and a little soy sauce, then simmer until the sauce thickens and the meat is tender.
  3. Roast chicken. Ripe dark fruit and a touch of toasted oak flatter crisp, savory skin.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, thyme, salt and pepper and roast at 200 C for about 75 minutes, until the juices run clear.
  4. Stuffed mushrooms. Earthy mushrooms bring out the mocha and toasty notes in the wine.
    Recipe idea: Fill mushroom caps with chopped stems, garlic, breadcrumbs, parmesan and parsley, drizzle with olive oil and bake until golden.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. Soft tannins and juicy fruit match a rich meat and tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown ground beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 30 minutes. Toss with spaghetti and parmesan.
