About this wine

Finca Las Moras is a winery rooted in San Juan, Argentina, with vineyards in the Tulum, Zonda and Pedernal valleys. Its Barrel Select range is hand-harvested with bunch and berry selection and aged for 12 months in French and American oak. The producer currently lists the range's Cabernet as a Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah blend, while critics have also reviewed a straight Cabernet Sauvignon Barrel Select, so check the label. Expect a deep colour, jammy black fruit, toasty notes from the barrels and a velvety texture.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled ribeye. Generous fruit and toasty oak are made for charred, juicy beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  2. Carni stoba. Jammy black fruit and smooth tannins suit a rich, slow-cooked beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Simmer beef cubes with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, capers and olives for about 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Stobá di kabritu. A warm-climate red with body stands up to the strong flavour of goat.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  4. Barbecue ribs. Vanilla and cedar from the oak echo the smoky, sweet glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, garlic and brown sugar, bake covered at 150°C for 2 hours, then glaze and grill.
  5. Grilled burgers. A ripe, round red makes an easy match for a cheeseburger from the grill.
    Recipe idea: Shape seasoned minced beef into patties, grill 4 minutes per side and serve on buns with tomato, onion and cheese.
