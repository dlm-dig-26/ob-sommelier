About this wine

Yellow Tail is an Australian wine brand from Casella Family Brands. A UK retailer describes it as created by, and still owned by, the sixth generation of the Casella family, and still made in the family's home town, with Alan Kennett as winemaker. The grapes for this Cabernet Sauvignon come from vineyards across South Eastern Australia. It is made in a ripe, fruit-forward style for drinking young, and the producer describes red fruit, blackcurrant, blackberry and a sweet mocha note. The bottle is closed with a screw cap.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe blackcurrant and a full body handle the spice and richness of goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic and cumin, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper.
  2. Carni stoba. Soft, rounded tannins and mocha notes suit slow-cooked beef in tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper and a splash of vinegar, and simmer until tender.
  3. Grilled steak. A juicy Cabernet with dark fruit is made for charred red meat.
    Recipe idea: Rub a steak with salt, pepper and olive oil, grill over high heat and rest for five minutes before slicing.
  4. Lasagna. The fruit matches the tomato and the body holds up to meat and cheese layers.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with beef ragu, béchamel and mozzarella, top with Parmesan and bake until golden.
  5. Barbecue burgers. Sweet mocha and ripe berries love smoky grilled beef and barbecue sauce.
    Recipe idea: Grill beef patties, brush with barbecue sauce, and serve on buns with grilled onions and cheese.
