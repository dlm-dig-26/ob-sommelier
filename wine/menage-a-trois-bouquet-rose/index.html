About this wine

Ménage à Trois is a California wine brand made by the Folie à Deux winery, which has been part of Trinchero Family Estates since 2004. The Bouquet Rosé blends three grapes: Merlot, Syrah and Gewurztraminer. The juice is fermented cold and kept from malolactic fermentation, which preserves its fresh fruit and floral spice. The result is a dry, crisp rosé with raspberry, wild strawberry and lychee, a floral perfume and a silky finish. It comes in a 750 ml screw-cap bottle, easy to open at a picnic or a casual dinner.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Crisp acidity and red berry fruit lift lime-cured fish without covering its delicate taste.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, red onion, chili and cilantro, rest 20 minutes in the fridge and serve cold.
  2. Keshi yena. Soft fruit and fresh acidity cut through melted cheese and balance the savoury, slightly sweet filling.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with tomato, olives, capers and raisins, cover with cheese and bake until golden.
  3. Pastechi. The bright, fruity style refreshes the palate between bites of hot fried pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with spiced ground beef or cheese, fold into half moons, seal the edges and fry until golden.
  4. Grilled shrimp salad. Red berry and citrus notes match sweet shrimp and a light, tangy dressing.
    Recipe idea: Grill garlic shrimp and toss with mixed greens, mango, cucumber and a lime and olive oil dressing.
  5. Spicy chicken stir-fry. A touch of fruit and no tannin calm chili heat instead of making it sharper.
    Recipe idea: Stir-fry chicken strips with sweet peppers, garlic, ginger and a little chili, finish with soy sauce and lime and serve over rice.
