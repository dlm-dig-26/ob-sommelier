About this wine

Robert Mondavi Private Selection is a California range based in Ripon, with a badge on its site showing it was established in 1994. For this Merlot, the producer says the grapes come from vineyards along the Central Coast and in Lodi. An Italian retailer lists the wine as 100% Merlot, fermented in stainless steel and then aged in oak, and the producer notes that French oak adds roundness and depth. Do not confuse it with the Rum Barrel-Aged Merlot, a separate wine in the same range.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Plum and cherry fruit with soft tannins suit tender beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, tomato and sweet pepper, then simmer until tender and serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Galinja stoba. A smooth, medium-bodied red is gentle enough for stewed chicken.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper and a little soy sauce, and simmer until cooked through.
  3. Pastechi di carni. Light spice and ripe fruit match the savoury beef filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef cooked with onion, raisins and spices, fold, seal and fry until golden.
  4. Roast chicken. Mocha and baking spice in the wine pick up the crisp, golden skin.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, thyme and salt, roast at 200 C for about an hour and rest before carving.
  5. Mushroom pasta. Earthy mushrooms bring out the tea leaf and olive notes.
    Recipe idea: Sauté mushrooms with garlic and thyme, add a splash of cream and toss with pasta and grated Parmesan.
