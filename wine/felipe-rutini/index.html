About this wine

Felipe Rutini is the icon wine of Rutini Wines, named after the founder. Felipe Rutini came to Mendoza from the Marche region of Italy and founded the La Rural winery in Coquimbito, Maipú, in 1885. His family later planted some of the first vines in the Uco Valley, and the company now makes its wines at a winery in Tupungato. The Felipe Rutini wine was first made in 1981 and released in 1985 to mark the winery's centenary.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. A full, structured red stands up to rich goat and spice.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Firm tannins and body suit a long-simmered beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled ribeye. A big red with strong tannins is made for fatty grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat for 4 minutes a side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Slow-roasted lamb shoulder. Rich lamb softens the tannins of a powerful blend.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb shoulder with garlic, rosemary and salt, roast covered at 160C for 4 hours until it pulls apart, then crisp uncovered.
  5. Aged gouda. Nutty, firm cheese pairs well with a serious red.
    Recipe idea: Serve slices of aged gouda with walnuts, dried fruit and crackers at room temperature.
