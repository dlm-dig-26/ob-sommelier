About this wine

Ménage à Trois is a California red blend of three grapes: Zinfandel, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon. Each variety is fermented separately and they are blended just before bottling. Zinfandel brings blackberry and raspberry jam, Merlot adds red fruit and a soft mouthfeel, and Cabernet gives firm tannins and structure. The result is forward, silky and smooth, an easygoing red for everyday meals. The wine is produced under the Folie à Deux winery name.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Barbecue chicken. Jammy berry fruit loves a sweet, smoky barbecue glaze.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in barbecue sauce with garlic and a little hot pepper, grill until charred and cooked through, brushing with more sauce.
  2. Pastechi. Juicy fruit against a savoury, spiced beef filling makes an easy match.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with spiced ground beef, cheese or tuna, fold into half moons, seal the edges and deep-fry until golden.
  3. Pepperoni pizza. Ripe fruit and smooth tannins are an easy match for tomato, cheese and pepperoni.
    Recipe idea: Spread pizza dough with tomato sauce, top with mozzarella and pepperoni, and bake in a very hot oven for 10 to 12 minutes.
  4. Carni stoba. Enough fruit and structure for a homestyle beef stew without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  5. Cheeseburger. A smooth, fruit-forward red that keeps up with melted cheese and grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season with salt and pepper, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and top with cheddar, onion and tomato.
