About this wine

Carlo Rossi began in California in 1962 under the name Red Mountain and took its current name in 1975, after Charles Rossi, a well-known salesman for the company who was related to the Gallo family by marriage. The wines are made by E. & J. Gallo Winery and are known for a simple, fruit-forward style. The Moscato is a sweet, light white from California with ripe peach, apricot and tropical fruit, honeysuckle aromas and lively acidity. It comes with a twist-off cap and is best served well chilled.

On the shelf: Twist-off screw cap.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Spicy shrimp creole. Gentle sweetness and a light body cool the chili heat, while peach fruit lifts the shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Sauté onion, celery and peppers, add tomato, garlic and hot sauce, simmer, then add shrimp for 3 minutes; serve with white rice.
  2. Cheese pastechi. Bright, sweet fruit is a nice contrast to salty cheese and crisp fried dough.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with grated Gouda, fold, seal the edges with a fork and fry until puffed and golden.
  3. Coconut flan. Ripe peach and honeysuckle echo a creamy coconut dessert; keep the dessert no sweeter than the wine.
    Recipe idea: Caramelize sugar in a mold, pour in eggs blended with coconut milk and condensed milk, bake in a water bath for 50 minutes and chill.
  4. Tropical fruit salad. Peach and tropical notes mirror mango, pineapple and papaya.
    Recipe idea: Cube mango, pineapple and papaya, add a squeeze of lime and a few mint leaves, and chill before serving.
  5. Thai green curry with chicken. Light sweetness and fresh acidity balance coconut milk and chili.
    Recipe idea: Fry green curry paste, add coconut milk and sliced chicken, simmer with vegetables for 10 minutes and finish with basil.
