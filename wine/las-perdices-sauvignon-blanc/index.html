About this wine

Viña Las Perdices is a family winery in Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo, in the Andean foothills of Mendoza at roughly 1,030 meters above sea level. It was created by Juan Muñoz López, who came from Andalucía in Spain, together with his wife Rosario, and their children work in the business. Local lore says Don Juan tended his vines with three partridges for company, which gave the winery its name, Partridge Vineyard. Their Sauvignon Blanc is a dry, crisp white, with tasters noting passion fruit, citrus, green apple and fresh grassy notes.

Bottle sizes made: 375 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. High acidity and citrus notes match the lime in the fish and keep every bite fresh.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice and add red onion, chili, salt and coriander. Rest 15 minutes and serve cold.
  2. Pisca hasa. Crisp and zesty, it cuts through the fried coating like a squeeze of lime.
    Recipe idea: Season snapper fillets with lime, salt and garlic, dust with flour and fry until golden. Serve with lime wedges and a green salad.
  3. Sopi di pisca. Its grassy, herbal side suits a light fish soup full of fresh herbs.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper and potato in stock. Finish with lime juice and chopped herbs.
  4. Goat cheese salad. A classic match: the acidity balances the tangy, creamy cheese.
    Recipe idea: Toss mixed greens and sliced cucumber with a lemon vinaigrette, then top with crumbled goat cheese and toasted nuts.
  5. Garlic shrimp. Light body and citrus freshness lift buttery garlic shrimp without covering them.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp in olive oil with plenty of garlic and a pinch of chili. Finish with lemon juice and parsley and serve with bread.
