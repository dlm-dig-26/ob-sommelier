About this wine

Trumpeter is a range from Rutini Wines, a winery in Tupungato in the Uco Valley, Mendoza, with Mariano Di Paola listed as winemaker. This wine is 100% Sauvignon Blanc and is fermented in stainless steel, which keeps the focus on fresh fruit. It is pale straw with green-gold tints and shows lemon, grapefruit, fresh herbs and cut grass, with a light body and lively acidity.

Bottle sizes made: 375 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Grapefruit and lime notes and sharp acidity mirror the citrus marinade.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt.
  2. Pisca hasa. Herbal freshness cuts through fried fish like a squeeze of lime.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets or whole fish with lime, garlic and salt, fry in hot oil until golden and serve with a fresh onion and pepper sauce.
  3. Grilled red snapper. Light body and grassy notes suit simply grilled fish with thyme.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic, thyme and salt, and grill 6 to 8 minutes per side until the flesh flakes.
  4. Goat cheese salad. A classic match: tangy goat cheese and zesty Sauvignon Blanc.
    Recipe idea: Toss mixed greens with a lemon vinaigrette, top with crumbled goat cheese, sliced cucumber and toasted nuts.
  5. Garlic shrimp. Crisp acidity balances garlic and olive oil and keeps the dish light.
    Recipe idea: Sauté peeled shrimp in olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 2 to 3 minutes, finish with lemon juice.
