About this wine

Robert Mondavi Private Selection began in 1994 as an everyday range carrying the Robert Mondavi name, and it is based in Ripon, California. Its Cabernet Sauvignon is made mainly from grapes grown on the California Central Coast and in Lodi. After fermentation the wine spends about ten months in a mix of French and American oak, which adds notes of vanilla and toast. The result is a medium-bodied, approachable red with cherry, blackberry and cassis and a touch of pepper and coffee.

Bottle sizes made: 375 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Soft tannins and dark fruit stand up to the rich, spiced goat stew without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a splash of vinegar until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. The plum and coffee notes match slow-cooked beef and its tomato-rich sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, tomato, bell pepper, capers and olives, cover with stock and simmer for about two hours. Serve with white rice.
  3. Grilled steak. Cassis and toasty oak love the char of a grilled steak, and the tannins cut through the fat.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt, pepper and garlic, grill over high heat for 4 to 5 minutes per side and rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Beef burgers. A medium-bodied, fruit-forward red is an easy match for juicy cheeseburgers.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season well, grill until done to taste, top with cheddar and serve with tomato, onion and pickles.
  5. Lasagna. Ripe fruit and moderate acidity hold up to tomato sauce, beef and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with a beef and tomato ragù, ricotta and mozzarella, finish with parmesan and bake at 180 C for about 45 minutes.
