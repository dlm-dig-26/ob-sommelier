About this wine

Rutini Wines traces its roots to 1885 and Felipe Rutini, an Italian immigrant who brought Old World winemaking traditions to Argentina. Today the winery is in Tupungato, in the Uco Valley, where its vineyards sit between 1,000 and 1,200 metres above sea level. The Colección Cabernet Sauvignon is made from Uco Valley grapes. Cool nights at this altitude let the grapes ripen slowly and keep their fresh acidity, giving a structured Cabernet with dark fruit.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Firm tannins and dark fruit stand up to this rich, slow-cooked goat stew with cumin and pepper.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and Scotch bonnet, then simmer 2 hours with a splash of the wine until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. The wine's structure matches tender braised beef and its savoury, tomato-rich sauce.
    Recipe idea: Sear beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste and stock, and simmer until soft; add potatoes for the last 30 minutes.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Charred, fatty steak softens the tannins and brings out the cassis.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and finish with herb butter.
  4. Lamb chops with rosemary. Savoury, herbal lamb echoes the red pepper and cedar notes.
    Recipe idea: Rub chops with garlic, rosemary and olive oil, sear 3 minutes per side and serve with roasted potatoes.
  5. Aged Gouda. Nutty, salty aged cheese balances the firm grip and long finish.
    Recipe idea: Serve aged Gouda in chunks at room temperature with walnuts, dried figs and crusty bread.
