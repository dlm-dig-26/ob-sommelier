About this wine

Apothic Dark comes from Apothic Wines, a California brand based in Modesto that is known for bold red blends. Dark is a California red blend led by Petite Sirah, a deeply coloured and robust grape, with other varieties added to round it out. It is full-bodied and smooth, with black cherry and plum fruit and aromas of lightly roasted oak, spice and dark chocolate. It is finished slightly off-dry, which gives it a rich, rounded feel.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. A rich, dark blend with a touch of sweetness stands up to a deeply spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Plummy fruit and smoky oak match the slow-cooked depth of a Caribbean beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Sear beef cubes, add onion, tomato, peppers, celery and a splash of vinegar, and simmer for two hours. Add potatoes for the last 30 minutes.
  3. Braised short ribs. Full body and velvety texture are a natural fit for tender, rich braised beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown short ribs, then braise in a covered pot with onion, garlic, tomato paste and beef stock at 160 C for about three hours.
  4. Aged cheese board. The ripe dark fruit and chocolate notes balance the salty, nutty bite of aged cheese.
    Recipe idea: Set out aged Gouda and sharp cheddar with walnuts, dried figs and crackers.
  5. Spiced chocolate brownies. Its slight sweetness and dark chocolate notes echo a spiced chocolate dessert.
    Recipe idea: Make a brownie batter with dark chocolate, butter, sugar, eggs and flour, add a pinch of cinnamon and chili, and bake at 175 C for 25 minutes.
