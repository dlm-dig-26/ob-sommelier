About this wine

La Chouette is a red blend from Bodega Monteviejo in the Uco Valley, made from Cabernet Franc and Cabernet Sauvignon, listed in equal parts for the 2015 vintage. The grapes come from Vista Flores, at about 1,000 metres, from vines trained on a high density trellis. The name is French for the owl, a tribute to the night bird that watches over the vineyards at the foot of the Andes. Expect red and black fruit, white pepper and violet, gentle oak notes of vanilla, a broad, juicy palate and round, polished tannins.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Round tannins and red fruit suit stewed chicken, and the white pepper note echoes the spices.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, a little cumin and stock, then simmer 45 minutes until tender.
  2. Keshi yena. Fresh acidity cuts the melted cheese and the wine's pepper and fruit match the savoury filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a Gouda lined dish with chicken cooked with tomato, onion, raisins, olives and capers, cover with cheese and bake 30 minutes.
  3. Herb crusted lamb chops. Cabernet Franc's herbal, peppery side is a natural fit for lamb with garden herbs.
    Recipe idea: Coat lamb chops in mustard and breadcrumbs mixed with parsley, mint and garlic, sear 2 minutes per side and finish 6 minutes in a hot oven.
  4. Tagliatelle with beef ragu. A juicy, medium full red with fresh acidity balances tomato and slow cooked meat.
    Recipe idea: Simmer minced beef with onion, carrot, celery, tomato and red wine for 90 minutes and toss with fresh tagliatelle and grated cheese.
  5. Aged Gouda with apple. Polished tannins and dark fruit meet the nutty, salty crystals of aged cheese.
    Recipe idea: Serve thin slices of aged Gouda with crisp apple wedges, walnuts and a few slices of crusty bread.
