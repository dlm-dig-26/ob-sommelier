About this wine

Gemma di Luna is an Italian wine brand marketed in the United States by Enovation Brands of Florida, and Quebec retailer listings name Enoitalia as the producer. This Pinot Grigio comes from the Delle Venezie DOC, the broad appellation of northeastern Italy that spans Veneto, Friuli Venezia Giulia and Trentino. Made entirely from Pinot Grigio and without oak, it is a fresh, crisp white with pear, melon and white flower notes and a soft, gently fruity finish. A small amount of residual sugar gives it a smooth feel.

On the shelf: Curved bottle with a pearl white capsule and an oval label with a sparkling, diamond-like foil effect; turquoise tones.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pisca hasa. Crisp acidity cleans up fried fish while the pear notes stay fresh next to the Creole sauce.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets with lime, salt and garlic, dust with flour and fry; serve with a quick sauce of onion, sweet pepper and tomato.
  2. Ceviche. A light, fresh white that echoes the lime and keeps the delicate fish in focus.
    Recipe idea: Marinate cubes of white fish in lime juice with red onion, a little hot pepper and cilantro for 20 minutes; serve chilled.
  3. Funchi with fish. Its gentle body suits mild cornmeal and flaky white fish without overpowering them.
    Recipe idea: Stir cornmeal into salted boiling water with butter until thick, then serve with pan-fried fish and a tomato and onion sauce.
  4. Pasta primavera. Light and smooth, it matches spring vegetables, lemon and a little Parmesan.
    Recipe idea: Toss pasta with sautéed zucchini, peas, cherry tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, lemon zest and grated Parmesan.
  5. Greek salad. Fresh acidity balances salty feta and briny olives.
    Recipe idea: Combine tomato, cucumber, red onion, olives and feta, dress with olive oil, lemon and dried oregano.
