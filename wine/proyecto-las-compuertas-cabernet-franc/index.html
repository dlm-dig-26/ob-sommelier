About this wine

Proyecto Las Compuertas is the family project of brothers Héctor and Pablo Durigutti in Las Compuertas, which they describe as the coolest corner of western Luján de Cuyo, Mendoza. It began in 2007 with five hectares of historic Malbec near their winery and has grown to 34 hectares called Finca Victoria, named after their mother. This Cabernet Franc comes from organically farmed vines planted in 2015 at about 1,050 metres. The grapes are hand-picked, fermented as whole berries with native yeasts in concrete eggs, and aged eight months in concrete, with no oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Peppery, herbal Cabernet Franc echoes the spices in goat stew, and its freshness cuts the richness.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic and cumin, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper.
  2. Pastechi di carni. Medium body and fine grip suit the spiced beef filling and crisp pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef cooked with onion, sweet pepper, raisins and spices, fold, seal and fry until golden.
  3. Grilled pork chops. Red fruit and fresh acidity balance juicy, lightly charred pork.
    Recipe idea: Season pork chops with garlic, thyme, salt and pepper and grill a few minutes per side until just cooked.
  4. Herb roast chicken. The wine's herbal notes pick up rosemary and thyme on the bird.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, garlic, rosemary, thyme and salt and roast at 200 C for about an hour.
  5. Grilled vegetables with herbs. Savoury, unoaked and fresh, it loves peppers, eggplant and zucchini from the grill.
    Recipe idea: Slice sweet pepper, eggplant and zucchini, brush with olive oil, grill and finish with garlic, parsley and a little vinegar.
