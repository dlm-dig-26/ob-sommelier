About this wine

Bodega Monteviejo, built in 2001 in Vista Flores in the Uco Valley, was the first winery on the Clos de los Siete estate, a project born when Michel Rolland invited a group of French wine families to Mendoza. Catherine Péré-Vergé of Château Le Gay in Bordeaux led the company until 2013, when her son Henry Parent took over, and winemaker Marcelo Pelleriti began his career here. Petite Fleur is the winery's blend line; the 2022 vintage combines Malbec, Merlot, Syrah and Cabernet Sauvignon, with dark berry, plum, violet and vanilla notes and a generous, full body.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled steak. A full-bodied, smooth Malbec blend made for charred beef and its juices.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick rib-eye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 to 5 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and finish with chimichurri.
  2. Carni stoba. Rich beef stew stands up to the wine's body, and the spice echoes its violet and pepper notes.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a glass of red. Simmer covered 2 hours until tender.
  3. Keshi yena with beef. The baked cheese and spiced minced beef filling need a red with fruit and structure.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam, or a dish lined with Gouda slices, with minced beef cooked with onion, tomato, olives and raisins. Bake 30 minutes at 180 C.
  4. Lamb chops with rosemary. Ripe plum and blackcurrant fruit with balanced oak suit herb-crusted lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with olive oil, garlic, rosemary and salt. Grill or pan fry 3 minutes per side for pink meat.
  5. Mushroom and beef lasagna. Earthy, savoury layers match the wine's depth and smooth tannins.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with a beef and mushroom tomato sauce and bechamel, top with cheese and bake 40 minutes at 180 C.
