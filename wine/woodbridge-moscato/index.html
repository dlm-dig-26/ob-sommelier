About this wine

Woodbridge was founded by Robert Mondavi in 1979 and is based in Acampo, near Lodi, in California's Central Valley. Its Moscato is made mainly from grapes grown around Lodi, led by Muscat Canelli and Orange Muscat with a little Gewürztraminer. The wine was inspired by Mondavi's earlier Moscato d'Oro. Expect a light, gently sweet white with a faint prickle of bubbles and aromas of orange blossom, honeysuckle and citrus. Serve it slightly chilled with fresh fruit or simple desserts.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Coconut flan. Silky caramel custard and coconut meet the wine's honeyed apricot, and its acidity keeps the pairing light.
    Recipe idea: Blend eggs, condensed milk, coconut milk and vanilla, pour over caramelised sugar in a mould and bake in a water bath until set.
  2. Bolo pretu. Rich, dark fruit cake gets a fresh, floral counterpoint from a lighter sweet white served cold.
    Recipe idea: Soak dried fruit in rum, fold into a dark spiced batter with burnt-sugar syrup and bake low and slow. Slice thin.
  3. Fruit salad with mint. Peach and orange blossom notes mirror ripe fruit; the wine should be as sweet as the bowl.
    Recipe idea: Toss mango, papaya, pineapple and melon with lime juice, a little honey and chopped mint. Chill before serving.
  4. Spicy jerk chicken wings. Gentle sweetness and low alcohol soothe the Scotch bonnet heat and play with the allspice.
    Recipe idea: Marinate wings in jerk paste of scallion, Scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme and lime, then grill or bake until crisp.
  5. Pound cake with peaches. A warm, buttery slice with fruit is just what the producer suggests for this light, aromatic Moscato.
    Recipe idea: Bake a classic pound cake. Serve warm slices with sliced peaches tossed in a little sugar and lemon juice.
