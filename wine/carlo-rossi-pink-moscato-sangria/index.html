About this wine

Carlo Rossi is a California brand owned by E. & J. Gallo, best known for its easy-going jug wines. It started in 1962 as Red Mountain and took the Carlo Rossi name, after a Gallo salesman related to the family by marriage, when it went into national distribution in the 1970s. The Pink Moscato Sangria is a non-vintage, light-bodied sweet pink wine from California, built on Moscato, with peach, tropical fruit, a hint of red fruit and citrus, and soft floral notes. It is meant to be served chilled or over ice.

On the shelf: Twist-off cap; the large 4 L size comes as a jug.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L, 4 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Cala. Served well chilled, the sweetness and low alcohol cool down these salty, spicy bean fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, peel, blend with onion, hot pepper and salt into a thick batter, then fry spoonfuls until golden.
  2. Mango ceviche. Sweet peach and tropical fruit balance the lime and chili in a fruity ceviche.
    Recipe idea: Mix cubed fresh fish with lime juice, then add diced mango, red onion, chili and cilantro; chill 20 minutes before serving.
  3. Fruit salad. Peach and tropical notes mirror fresh fruit; the wine should be at least as sweet as the dish.
    Recipe idea: Combine pineapple, mango, papaya and strawberries, add a squeeze of lime and some mint, and serve cold.
  4. Coconut flan. A light, sweet pink wine that goes with a creamy caramel dessert without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Whisk eggs, condensed milk and coconut milk, pour into a caramel-lined dish and bake in a water bath at 160°C for about 50 minutes; chill.
  5. Sweet chili chicken wings. Sweetness and fruit soften the chili heat of sticky wings.
    Recipe idea: Bake chicken wings until crisp, toss in sweet chili sauce with lime and garlic, and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
