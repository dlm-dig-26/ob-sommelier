About this wine

Casarena, whose name means sand house, is a winery in Perdriel, Luján de Cuyo, about 20 minutes from Mendoza city. The project began in 2007 with the renovation of a winery building from 1937 on Brandsen street; its address, Brandsen 505, also gives its name to the 505 range. Casarena farms its own vineyards in Agrelo and Perdriel. The 505 Chardonnay is made from 100 percent Chardonnay, fermented in stainless steel and given only a light touch of French oak, so the fruit stays in front: apple, stone fruit and tropical fruit, a fresh, mineral feel and a soft, creamy finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pisca hasa. Fresh acidity and a soft, creamy feel suit crisp fried fish without covering its flavour.
    Recipe idea: Season snapper fillets with lime, garlic and salt, dust with flour and fry in hot oil until golden. Serve with funchi and a fresh onion and pepper sauce.
  2. Keshi yena. Medium body and a light touch of oak match the melted cheese and the mildly spiced chicken filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam with stewed chicken, olives, raisins and capers, cover with cheese slices and bake at 180°C for about 30 minutes.
  3. Sopi di pisca. Gentle fruit and a mineral edge lift a light fish soup with fresh herbs.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, tomato, garlic, potato and celery in stock, season with lime and fresh herbs and serve hot.
  4. Roast chicken with lemon and herbs. Apple and citrus notes echo the lemon, and the creamy finish suits the juicy meat.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, lemon zest, garlic and thyme and roast at 200°C for about an hour, basting a few times.
  5. Garlic butter shrimp. Its rounded texture matches the butter, while the freshness keeps the dish light.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp in butter with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for about 3 minutes, finish with lemon and serve with bread or rice.
