About this wine

Montes is a Chilean winery whose home is its Apalta winery near Santa Cruz, in the Colchagua Valley. The Classic Series is labelled Reserva. In recent vintages this Cabernet Sauvignon comes from the Apalta and Marchigüe vineyards in Colchagua, is hand-picked and includes about 15 percent Merlot. Just under half of the wine spends eight months in French oak, adding a touch of vanilla without hiding the fruit. Older bottlings carried the broader Central Valley appellation, which is why both names appear on shelves.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Cassis fruit and a hint of pepper suit the cumin and spice in the goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  2. Keshi yena. Smooth tannins and dark fruit handle the melted cheese and savoury filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Gouda or Edam with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, top with cheese and bake 30 minutes.
  3. Rosemary lamb chops. The mint and pepper notes in the wine love lamb and herbs.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary and olive oil, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and serve with roast potatoes.
  4. Grilled ribeye. A classic Cabernet match: grilled beef softens the tannins and brings out the fruit.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  5. Barbecue ribs. Light oak and ripe fruit stand up to smoky, slightly sweet ribs.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, garlic and brown sugar, bake covered at 150°C for 2 hours, then glaze and grill.
