About this wine

Proyecto Las Compuertas was started in 2007 by brothers Pablo and Héctor Durigutti, who bought old Malbec vines on Callejón de la Reta in Las Compuertas, Luján de Cuyo. Their Criolla comes from the Don Danielle vineyard, planted in 1943 and trained on a traditional overhead pergola. It is fermented in concrete eggs with native yeasts and aims to revive the light, everyday local wine of Mendoza's past.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. A light, fresh red with low tannin can partner grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole red snapper with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, grill over medium heat for about 8 minutes per side and finish with a squeeze of lime.
  2. Galinja stoba. High acidity and light body keep a chicken stew lively.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a pinch of cumin, then simmer until tender and saucy. Serve with white rice.
  3. Pastechi. A light everyday red suits fried pastries with savoury fillings.
    Recipe idea: Fill small pastry half-moons with seasoned cheese, tuna or ground beef, seal the edges with a fork and fry or bake until golden.
  4. Beef empanadas. Fresh acidity cuts through pastry and lifts a spiced filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef, onion, cumin, paprika, olive and egg, fold and crimp, then bake at 200C until golden.
  5. Charcuterie board. Light, bright reds are a natural match for cured meats.
    Recipe idea: Arrange salami, ham, a few cheeses, olives, pickles and crusty bread on a board and serve slightly chilled wine alongside.
