About this wine

Casarena is a winery in Luján de Cuyo, Mendoza, whose building dates from 1937. It sits in Perdriel, at roughly 900 metres above sea level, and is co-owned by a North American and an Argentine family. Winemaker Agustín Alcoleas works with sustainably farmed estate vineyards planted with Malbec, Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Franc and several white grapes. A Malbec rosé is made from red grapes with only brief skin contact, which gives a pale colour, fresh red fruit and lively acidity.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Lively acidity and red fruit freshen the lime-cured fish without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, sweet pepper and cilantro, chill 20 minutes and serve with crackers.
  2. Pastechi. A cool, fruity rosé cleans up the fried pastry and suits cheese, meat or tuna fillings.
    Recipe idea: Roll out a simple dough, fill with gouda, spiced ground beef or tuna, fold into half moons, seal the edges and fry until golden.
  3. Grilled shrimp skewers. Strawberry notes and fresh acidity suit sweet, lightly charred shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Toss shrimp with olive oil, garlic, paprika and lime, thread onto skewers and grill 2 minutes per side.
  4. Watermelon and feta salad. A light, juicy rosé matches the sweet fruit and salty cheese.
    Recipe idea: Combine watermelon cubes, crumbled feta, mint and red onion, then finish with olive oil and a squeeze of lime.
  5. Chicken tacos. A hint of softness in the wine calms mild spice in the salsa.
    Recipe idea: Grill chicken thighs seasoned with cumin and paprika, slice, and serve in warm tortillas with pico de gallo and avocado.
