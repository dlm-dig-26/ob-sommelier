About this wine

La Vieille Ferme is a red from the Perrin family, owners of Château de Beaucastel in the southern Rhône, who have made it since 1970. It comes from the slopes of Mont Ventoux and blends Grenache and Syrah with Carignan and Cinsault. The grapes are fermented in cement vats with long skin contact, and the wine is aged mostly in cement with a small part in large oak casks. It is sealed with a screwcap.

On the shelf: Label shows a farmyard scene with poultry, a nod to the name, which means the old farm.

Bottle sizes made: 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. A peppery, medium red with soft tannins suits chicken stew with tomato.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a pinch of cumin, then simmer until tender and saucy. Serve with white rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Dark fruit and spice match the savoury depth of a beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Pizza. Fresh, fruity and easy, it works with tomato, cheese and cured meats.
    Recipe idea: Spread tomato sauce on pizza dough, add mozzarella, salami and oregano and bake in a very hot oven until bubbling.
  4. Grilled sausages. Spicy Grenache and Syrah love smoky, herby sausages.
    Recipe idea: Grill pork or chicken sausages over medium heat until browned all over and serve with mustard, bread and a green salad.
  5. Ratatouille. Provence-style vegetables suit a southern French red.
    Recipe idea: Stew aubergine, courgette, peppers, onion and tomato slowly in olive oil with garlic and thyme until soft and rich.
