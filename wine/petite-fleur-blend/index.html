About this wine

Bodega Monteviejo sits in Vista Flores, Tunuyán, in Mendoza's Uco Valley. Founded in 2001, it was the first winery built within Clos de los Siete, the project Michel Rolland started with a group of French vintners. It was long run by Catherine Péré-Vergé of Château Le Gay in Bordeaux, and her son Henry Parent took over after her death in 2013. Petite Fleur is the estate's approachable line, a blend led by Malbec and Merlot with smaller parts of other Bordeaux style grapes.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Tender stewed beef and its rich sauce match this blend's dark fruit and silky tannins.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, garlic, tomato and a dash of soy, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours; serve with rice.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. The plum and herbal notes in the wine sit nicely with the cumin and gentle heat of goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Sear goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin, a little hot pepper and stock, then cook slowly covered until tender.
  3. Grilled steak with chimichurri. A classic for Uco Valley reds: grilled beef and herbs echo the wine's fruit and herbal edge.
    Recipe idea: Grill a seasoned steak to taste; blend parsley, garlic, oregano, chilli flakes, vinegar and olive oil into a quick chimichurri.
  4. Beef kofta skewers. Spiced minced meat suits the wine's soft spice and generous, rounded palate.
    Recipe idea: Mix minced beef with onion, garlic, cumin and parsley, shape around skewers and grill; serve with yogurt sauce and flatbread.
  5. Duck breast with figs. Rich duck and sweet figs pair with the blend's plum, berry and violet notes.
    Recipe idea: Score and pan-fry duck breast skin side down 8 minutes, flip 3 minutes, rest; warm halved figs in the pan juices with a little honey.
