About this wine

Rutini Wines was founded by Felipe Rutini, an Italian from the Marche region who started Bodega La Rural in Maipú, Mendoza, in 1885. The family later expanded into the higher, cooler Uco Valley, and today the company also makes wine at a winery in Tupungato. The Colección range draws on Uco Valley vineyards, and this Pinot Noir is made entirely from that grape. It spends 12 months in French oak barrels, part of them new, under head winemaker Mariano Di Paola. The result is a lively, medium-weight red with raspberry, cherry and quince, a touch of forest floor and a balanced, lingering finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. Light body and fine tannins let the red fruit lift a meaty fish without clashing.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with garlic, lime, salt and a little Madame Jeanette pepper; grill 6 to 8 minutes a side and serve with pan bati or salad.
  2. Galinja stoba. Fresh acidity and cherry fruit match tender chicken stewed with tomato and sweet pepper.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, a little soy sauce and stock; simmer 45 minutes until tender. Serve with rice.
  3. Seared salmon. Bright raspberry and a gentle structure suit rich salmon with crisp skin.
    Recipe idea: Season salmon fillets, sear skin-side down 4 minutes, flip for 2 more, and finish with lemon and a few thyme leaves.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Forest-floor notes in the wine echo earthy mushrooms and creamy rice.
    Recipe idea: Soften onion in butter, toast arborio rice, add hot stock ladle by ladle with sautéed mushrooms; finish with parmesan and parsley.
  5. Roast chicken with herbs. Cherry and quince flavours flatter golden roast chicken and its herb-scented juices.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, garlic, thyme and lemon; roast at 200 °C for about 75 minutes and baste with the pan juices.
