About this wine

Yellow Tail is made by Casella Family Brands in the Riverina region of New South Wales, Australia. This Pink Moscato is made from Muscat grapes and is light, at about 7.5% alcohol. It is semi-sweet with a gentle fizz: rose petal, orange blossom, apple and pear on the nose, then strawberry, raspberry, watermelon and peach on the palate, with a crisp, citrusy finish. Serve it well chilled in a white wine glass; it also works with a splash of soda or in a summer sangria.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Fresh fruit salad. Strawberry and peach notes meet the fruit; make the salad a little less sweet than the wine.
    Recipe idea: Mix strawberries, watermelon, mango and pineapple with a little lime juice and mint, and chill well.
  2. Coconut flan. Sweet, light and gently fizzy, it refreshes after a creamy coconut custard.
    Recipe idea: Caramelise sugar in a mould, pour in eggs blended with coconut milk and condensed milk, bake in a water bath for 50 minutes at 170°C and chill overnight.
  3. Cala. Gentle sweetness and fizz cool the hot sauce served with these bean fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, rub off the skins, blend with onion, salt and a little hot pepper, and fry spoonfuls until golden.
  4. Shrimp ceviche with mango. Fruity sweetness and fresh acidity match sweet mango and a little chili.
    Recipe idea: Mix cooked shrimp with diced mango, red onion, chili, coriander and lime juice, rest 10 minutes and serve cold.
  5. Spicy chicken wings. Sweet fruit tames chili heat, and the bubbles refresh the palate.
    Recipe idea: Toss wings in paprika, garlic and chili, bake 40 minutes at 200°C and glaze with honey and hot sauce.
