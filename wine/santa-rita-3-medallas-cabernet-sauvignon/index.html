About this wine

Viña Santa Rita has been making wine in Chile since 1880 and is linked to Alto Jahuel. In Chile this everyday red is sold as Santa Rita 120 3 Medallas Cabernet Sauvignon. It comes from the Maipo Valley under the wider Valle Central appellation. Chilean retailers describe an intense ruby colour, a velvety texture with good body and notes of coffee and chocolate next to the cherry and blackcurrant fruit. It is a dry red with medium tannins, easy to open for a weeknight meal.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 700 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Cherry fruit and easy tannins sit well with a homely beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Simmer beef cubes with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, capers and olives for about 90 minutes until tender.
  2. Pastechi with beef. A medium red like this suits the spiced meat filling without fighting the fried crust.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with spiced minced beef, raisins and olives, fold into half-moons, seal and deep-fry until golden.
  3. Beef empanadas. The coffee and chocolate notes go nicely with cumin and paprika.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with beef fried with onion, cumin and paprika, fold, seal and bake at 200°C for about 20 minutes.
  4. Grilled burgers. A simple, fruity Cabernet is an easy match for a juicy burger.
    Recipe idea: Shape seasoned minced beef into patties, grill 4 minutes per side and serve on buns with tomato, onion and cheese.
  5. Pasta with meat sauce. Fresh acidity meets the tomato and the fruit carries the meat.
    Recipe idea: Brown minced beef with onion and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and oregano, simmer 30 minutes and toss with spaghetti.
