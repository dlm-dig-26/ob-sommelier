About this wine

Red Diamond is a Washington State wine brand owned by Ste. Michelle Wine Estates. It first became known for its Merlot and later added a Cabernet Sauvignon. The Cabernet is an easygoing, fruit-led red; earlier vintages were blended mainly from Cabernet Sauvignon with small parts of Cabernet Franc, Merlot and Syrah. Expect black cherry and dark fruit with hints of coffee and oak, and soft, supple tannins that make it an easy match for grilled and stewed meat.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Dark fruit and soft tannins stand up to a rich, spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little Madame Jeanette pepper, then simmer covered for about 2 hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. Cherry fruit and gentle oak match slow-cooked beef in tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste, cumin and stock, simmer for about 1.5 hours and serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Its body and coffee notes suit a charred, juicy steak.
    Recipe idea: Season the steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat for about 4 minutes per side, rest for 5 minutes and top with garlic butter.
  4. Beef burger. Juicy fruit and soft tannins are a natural fit for a smoky grilled burger.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into thick patties, season well, grill for 4 to 5 minutes per side and serve with cheese, onion and tomato.
  5. Beef lasagna. Plum and cherry flavours go well with the meat sauce and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Layer lasagna sheets with beef and tomato sauce and a simple white sauce, top with grated cheese and bake at 180°C for about 40 minutes.
