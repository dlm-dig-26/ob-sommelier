About this wine

Rutini Wines traces its roots to Felipe Rutini, an agricultural engineer from Italy's Marche region who founded Bodega La Rural near Mendoza in 1885. The company began planting in the Uco Valley in 1925 and opened a winery in Tupungato in 2008; Mariano Di Paola leads its winemaking. Encuentro, Spanish for encounter, is one of its lines. This Malbec comes from the Uco Valley, with fruit from Gualtallary, and is fermented in stainless steel before ageing in French oak barrels. Expect plum, blackberry and violet with spice, cocoa and velvety tannins.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Tender stewed beef suits the wine's ripe plum and velvety tannins.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes with onion and garlic, add tomato, sweet pepper and stock, and simmer gently until the meat is tender.
  2. Keshi yena. Gouda and a spiced meat filling stand up well to a Malbec with good body.
    Recipe idea: Fill a Gouda-lined dish with ground beef cooked with onion, tomato, olives and raisins, cover with cheese and bake until bubbling.
  3. Grilled steak with chimichurri. A classic Argentine match: herbs and char lift the violet and dark fruit notes.
    Recipe idea: Grill a steak to taste and serve with a chimichurri of parsley, garlic, oregano, chili flakes, vinegar and olive oil.
  4. Beef empanadas. A savory, cumin-spiced filling and golden pastry suit a polished, fruity Malbec.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef, onion, cumin, paprika, olives and chopped hard-boiled egg, seal and bake until golden.
  5. Barbecue pork ribs. A smoky, slightly sweet glaze meets the wine's cocoa and spice.
    Recipe idea: Rub ribs with paprika, garlic and brown sugar, bake covered at low heat for two hours, then brush with sauce and grill briefly.
