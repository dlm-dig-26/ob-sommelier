About this wine

Viña Las Perdices is a family winery in Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo, founded by Juan Muñoz López, who came from Andalusia. Its Ala Colorada range includes this Ancellotta, a grape that comes originally from Emilia-Romagna in Italy. The 2013 vintage spent 15 months in new oak barrels, half French and half American. It is deep red with bluish tones and shows red fruit, plum, dried fig, tobacco and chocolate.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Braised short ribs. Plush dark fruit and new oak suit slow-braised beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef short ribs, add onion, carrot, garlic and red wine, cover and cook at 160°C for about 3 hours until very tender.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Dried fig and spice meet the warm spices of goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then stew slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little chili for about two hours until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Aged cheese board. Chocolate and fig notes love aged Gouda.
    Recipe idea: Serve aged Gouda, manchego and cheddar at room temperature with walnuts, dried figs and crusty bread.
  4. Carni stoba. A deep, full red for a hearty beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a splash of vinegar, and simmer about two hours until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  5. Grilled steak. Plum and tobacco notes match the char of grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 3 to 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
