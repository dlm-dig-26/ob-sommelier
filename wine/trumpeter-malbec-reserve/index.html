About this wine

Trumpeter is a range from Rutini Wines, a Mendoza house founded in 1885 by Felipe Rutini, who had emigrated from the Marche region of Italy at 18 and opened his La Rural winery in Mendoza. The family planted its first vines in Tupungato, high in the Uco Valley, and Rutini also farms estates in Altamira, Gualtallary and La Consulta. Head winemaker Mariano di Paola has been with the company since 1994. Malbec is Argentina's signature grape, and in this Reserve version a touch of French oak adds vanilla and spice to ripe plum and blackberry fruit.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di karni. A spiced beef stew matches the ripe plum fruit and round tannins of this Malbec.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a splash of wine and simmer covered until tender.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Goat stew has the richness to stand next to an oaked, full-bodied red.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic and curry, brown it, add onion, tomato and sweet pepper and simmer slowly for about two hours.
  3. Grilled steak with chimichurri. The Argentine classic: char and herbs meet dark fruit and a hint of spice.
    Recipe idea: Grill a seasoned sirloin on high heat 4 minutes per side and top with chimichurri of parsley, garlic, oregano, vinegar and oil.
  4. Beef empanadas. Savoury minced beef with cumin and paprika is an easy match for a juicy Malbec.
    Recipe idea: Cook minced beef with onion, cumin and paprika, add chopped egg and olives, fill pastry rounds, seal and bake until golden.
  5. Mushroom and cheese pizza. Earthy mushrooms and melted cheese suit the soft tannins and vanilla notes.
    Recipe idea: Spread tomato sauce on pizza dough, add sliced mushrooms, mozzarella and a little oregano and bake in a very hot oven.
