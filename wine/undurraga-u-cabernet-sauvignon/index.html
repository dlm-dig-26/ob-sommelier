About this wine

Undurraga is a Chilean winery established in 1885. Its U line comes from the Central Valley, and the producer lists this wine as 85 percent Cabernet Sauvignon with 15 percent Syrah. A retailer notes that it ferments in steel tanks without barrel ageing, at 13 percent alcohol. It is an intense ruby colour, dry and balanced, with red and dark fruit, plum, a herbal touch and smooth tannins.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. A medium body and smooth tannins suit an everyday beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a splash of vinegar, and simmer about two hours until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Pastechi. Fresh red fruit makes a lively match for meat-filled pastechi.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry with spiced minced beef or Gouda cheese, fold into half-moons, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  3. Grilled steak. A dry Cabernet with enough grip for simple grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 3 to 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Beef empanadas. Herbal and plum notes go well with cumin and olives.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with minced beef cooked with onion, paprika, cumin, olives and egg, fold, crimp and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  5. Grilled burgers. Easy and fruity, it pairs well with a grilled burger.
    Recipe idea: Shape minced beef into patties, season well and grill 4 minutes per side. Serve in buns with cheese, tomato and onion.
