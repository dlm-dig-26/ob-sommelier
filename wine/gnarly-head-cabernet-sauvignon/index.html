About this wine

Gnarly Head was born in 2004, when its winemakers went to Lodi, California, in search of old-vine Zinfandel. The brand is part of Delicato Family Wines and today sources grapes from vineyards in California for a big, juicy house style. This Cabernet Sauvignon is a California red based on Cabernet, with recent vintages also including some Petite Sirah and Malbec. After fermentation it is aged with a mix of American and French oak, which brings spice and a smoky edge. Expect black cherry, blackberry, peppercorn and charred oak, with a dense, dark texture, bold tannins and a generous finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Bold tannins and dark fruit stand up to slow-cooked goat with cumin and pepper.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces with onion, garlic and cumin, add tomato, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper, cover and simmer two hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. The oak spice and firm structure match a rich, tomato-based beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, tomato, peppers, capers and olives, cover with stock and simmer 90 minutes; serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled ribeye steak. Charred oak and tannins love the fat and crust of a well-grilled steak.
    Recipe idea: Rub the steak with salt, pepper and garlic, grill hot for 4 to 5 minutes per side and rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Barbecue pork ribs. Jammy black fruit meets the smoky, sticky glaze, and the tannins cut through the fat.
    Recipe idea: Rub ribs with paprika, salt and brown sugar, bake covered for 2 hours at 150°C, then brush with barbecue sauce and grill until sticky.
  5. Beef burger with aged cheddar. A dense, juicy red for a juicy burger; the sharp cheese meets the peppery notes.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season well, grill to your liking, top with aged cheddar and serve on toasted buns with onion.
