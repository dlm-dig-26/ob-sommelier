About this wine

Barefoot began in 1965, when Davis Bynum started making wine in Alameda County, California. From 1985 it was run by Michael Houlihan and Bonnie Harvey, who built up the brand, and since 2005 it has been part of E. & J. Gallo. The wines are made by Barefoot Cellars in Modesto, California. This Sauvignon Blanc is fruit-forward and crisp, with honeydew melon, nectarine and sweet lime, and a light hint of jalapeño.

On the shelf: Look for the Barefoot footprint on the label; the label colour changes by grape.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 500 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L, 3 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Its lime and jalapeño notes are made for lime-cured fish with chili.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, chili, cilantro and salt.
  2. Sopi di pisca. A crisp white keeps a light fish soup with lime fresh and lively.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, garlic, tomato, potato, pumpkin and herbs for about 20 minutes. Finish with lime juice.
  3. Goat cheese salad. Sauvignon Blanc and goat cheese are a classic match; the acidity balances the tang.
    Recipe idea: Top mixed greens with crumbled goat cheese, sliced apple and walnuts, and dress with olive oil and lemon.
  4. Garlic shrimp. Fruity and crisp, it lifts buttery garlic shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp in butter and olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 3 minutes. Serve with bread or rice.
  5. Grilled chicken. A light body and fresh fruit suit chicken with lime and oregano.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in garlic, lime, oregano and olive oil, then grill 6 to 7 minutes per side until cooked through.
