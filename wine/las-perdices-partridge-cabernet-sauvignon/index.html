About this wine

Viña Las Perdices is a winery in Luján de Cuyo, Mendoza, and the grapes for this wine come from its own vineyards in Agrelo. The Partridge line was created in 2009 as an entry-level range for export markets, alongside a Malbec and a Chardonnay. This 100% Cabernet Sauvignon gets a cold soak of four to five days, then about 12 days of fermentation, followed by time in oak. Expect a full, structured red with tobacco and vanilla, best enjoyed within two years of the vintage. Juan Carlos Muñoz is the winemaker.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled steak. Firm tannins and dark fruit are a classic match for charred, fatty beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt, pepper and garlic, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. A full, structured red holds its own against rich, spiced goat.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat in lime, garlic and pepper, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, cumin and a little Scotch bonnet for 2 to 3 hours.
  3. Carni stoba. Tobacco and vanilla notes suit a deep, slow-cooked beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  4. Beef empanadas. An Argentine red for an Argentine favourite: cumin and beef meet ripe Cabernet.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef cooked with onion, cumin, paprika, olives and egg, fold, crimp the edges and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  5. Grilled lamb chops. Rosemary and garlic echo the savoury side of Cabernet, and its tannins cut the fat.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, olive oil and salt, and grill 3 minutes per side for medium rare.
