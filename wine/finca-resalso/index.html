About this wine

Bodegas Emilio Moro is a family winery in Pesquera de Duero, in the Ribera del Duero region of northern Spain, where the family has worked in wine for four generations. Finca Resalso takes its name from a vineyard planted in 1932, the year Emilio Moro was born, while the wine itself comes from younger vines. First released in 1994, it is 100% Tempranillo. After a short maceration and malolactic fermentation in steel, the importer says it spends about six months in used French and American oak, keeping the fruit fresh.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi di carni. Juicy red fruit and soft tannins suit the spiced beef filling without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef cooked with onion, sweet pepper, raisins and spices, fold, seal and fry until golden.
  2. Keshi yena. A medium-bodied red with fresh acidity balances the melted cheese and savoury filling.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with olives, capers and raisins, cover with cheese and bake until bubbling.
  3. Grilled chorizo. Spanish Tempranillo and smoky paprika sausage are a natural match.
    Recipe idea: Grill chorizo until crisp at the edges and serve with crusty bread and roasted peppers.
  4. Carni stoba. Bright cherry fruit lifts a slow-cooked beef stew without feeling heavy.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper and a little vinegar, then simmer until tender.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. The wine's acidity matches the tomato and its fruit loves the meat sauce.
    Recipe idea: Simmer minced beef with onion, carrot, garlic and tomatoes for an hour and toss with spaghetti and Parmesan.
