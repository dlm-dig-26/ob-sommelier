About this wine

Proyecto Las Compuertas is a line from the brothers Héctor and Pablo Durigutti, born in Mendoza, who made their first wine in 2002. In 2007 they bought 5 hectares in Las Compuertas, Luján de Cuyo, and since 2008 they have worked in a restored old winery there; the Proyecto Las Compuertas line started in 2017. This wine comes from organically farmed vines at about 1,050 metres. Charbono is another name for Bonarda. Expect red cherry, bramble, herbs and spice, fresh natural acidity and soft tannins.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi. Juicy cherry and soft tannins suit crisp, meat-filled pastechi.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry with spiced minced beef or Gouda cheese, fold into half-moons, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  2. Galinja stoba. Fresh and vibrant, it lifts chicken stewed in tomato and pepper.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then stew with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a little soy or Worcestershire sauce for about 45 minutes. Serve with rice.
  3. Roast pork. Peppery notes and red fruit go well with garlic and cumin roast pork.
    Recipe idea: Rub a pork loin with garlic, cumin, orange zest and salt and roast at 190°C for about 1 hour, then rest 10 minutes.
  4. Grilled chicken. A medium red, light enough for grilled chicken with herbs.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in garlic, lime, oregano and olive oil, then grill 6 to 7 minutes per side until cooked through.
  5. Pizza. Bright acidity and herbs match tomato sauce and oregano.
    Recipe idea: Top pizza dough with tomato sauce, mozzarella and your favourite toppings and bake in a very hot oven for 10 to 12 minutes.
