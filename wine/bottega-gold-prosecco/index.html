About this wine

Bottega is a family wine and grappa company based at Villa Rosina in Bibano di Godega di Sant'Urbano, in the province of Treviso. Gold is a Prosecco DOC Spumante Brut made from 100 percent Glera grown in the Treviso hills up to the slopes of Valdobbiadene. The grapes are pressed gently, the must is kept cold and fermentation runs slowly for about 40 days at 14 to 15°C before the wine rests on its lees. The bubbles come from a second fermentation in a sealed pressure tank. It is light and lively, with green apple, pear, citrus and white flowers.

What to eat with it

  1. Fish ceviche. Crisp acidity and fine bubbles match the lime and lift the delicate raw fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, chili, cilantro and salt.
  2. Pisca hasa. Bubbles cut through the fried coating and the citrus notes suit lime-seasoned fish.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets with lime, garlic and salt, dust with flour, fry until golden and serve with funchi and Creole sauce.
  3. Cala. A brut sparkling wine is ideal with salty, crisp fried snacks.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, peel, blend with garlic, chili and salt, then fry spoonfuls until crisp and golden.
  4. Keshi yena. Lively acidity refreshes the palate between bites of rich melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Gouda or Edam with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, top with cheese and bake 30 minutes.
  5. Garlic grilled shrimp. Green apple and pear flavours stay fresh next to sweet shrimp and garlic.
    Recipe idea: Toss shrimp with garlic, olive oil, lime and parsley, grill 2 minutes per side and serve right away.
