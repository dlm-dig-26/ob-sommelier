About this wine

Pink Gold is a Prosecco DOC Rosé from Bottega, a family company based in Bibano di Godega di Sant'Urbano in the Treviso province of Veneto. Aldo Bottega, a master distiller, founded the business in 1977 with his wife Rosina Zambon, starting with grappa. After his death in 1983 his children Sandro, Barbara and Stefano took over and later added sparkling wines. Pink Gold blends Glera with Pinot Nero, which gets a short time on the skins for its colour, and the wine is refermented in tank by the Martinotti (Charmat) method for about two months. The grapes come from hilly vineyards on clay soils of alluvial origin.

On the shelf: Metallised pink bottle, the colour fused into the outer surface of the glass, finished with gold details.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Shrimp ceviche. Bright acidity and red berry fruit lift the lime and sweet shrimp without covering them.
    Recipe idea: Marinate diced raw shrimp in lime juice for 20 minutes, then add red onion, cilantro, a little Madame Jeanette pepper and diced mango. Serve chilled.
  2. Pastechi. Fine bubbles and freshness cut through the crisp fried pastry and salty cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with grated Gouda, fold into half moons, crimp the edges and deep fry until golden.
  3. Pisca hasa. Light body and red fruit suit lean fried white fish with a squeeze of lime.
    Recipe idea: Season red snapper with salt, garlic and lime, dust with flour and pan fry until crisp. Serve with funchi and a little pika.
  4. Salmon tartare. Strawberry notes and a light touch of sweetness match rich raw salmon.
    Recipe idea: Finely dice fresh salmon, mix with shallot, lemon zest, olive oil, salt and chives. Shape in a ring and serve with toast.
  5. Prosciutto and melon. Juicy fruit and lively bubbles balance salty ham and sweet melon.
    Recipe idea: Wrap thin slices of prosciutto around wedges of ripe cantaloupe and finish with black pepper and a few basil leaves.
