About this wine

Wapisa is a young family winery, established in 2016 in San Javier, about 30 km from Viedma, in the lower valley of the Río Negro in Patagonia. Its vineyards feel the influence of the nearby sea, and the name means whale. The winery also offers wines aged underwater in the Golfo San Matías. This Pinot Noir spends about eight months in oak and shows strawberry, ripe cherry and rose petal, with soft, silky tannins and fresh acidity.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. A light, fresh Pinot with soft tannins that sits well with grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole snapper with oil, garlic and herbs, grill 6 to 8 minutes per side and finish with lemon.
  2. Keshi yena. Red cherry and fresh acidity balance the rich, melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam, or a dish lined with cheese slices, with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives and raisins; cover with cheese and bake until melted.
  3. Roast chicken. Silky and elegant, it does not overpower simple roast chicken.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, lemon and thyme and roast at 200°C for about 1 hour 15 minutes, until the juices run clear.
  4. Grilled salmon. Strawberry and cherry fruit flatter salmon glazed with soy and honey.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with oil, soy sauce and a little honey and grill or pan-fry 4 minutes per side.
  5. Mushroom risotto. Earthy notes and fresh acidity match mushrooms and parmesan.
    Recipe idea: Fry mushrooms and onion in butter, add risotto rice, then add hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 18 minutes. Finish with parmesan.
