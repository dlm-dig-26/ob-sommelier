About this wine

Yellow Tail is made by Casella Family Brands, the family wine company that Filippo and Maria Casella, who came to Australia from Sicily, founded in 1969 in Yenda, in the Riverina area of New South Wales. The brand went on sale in the United States in 2001. This Shiraz uses grapes from South Eastern Australia. It is fermented in stainless steel at controlled temperatures with extended skin contact, then matured in American oak, which adds vanilla and spice to the ripe berry fruit. The style is soft, fruit-forward and easy to enjoy.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe blackberry fruit and a peppery edge sit well with the warm spices of a slow-cooked goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. A rich but soft Shiraz matches the depth of braised beef without fighting the tomato.
    Recipe idea: Sear beef cubes, add onion, tomato, peppers, celery and a splash of vinegar, and simmer for two hours. Add potatoes for the last 30 minutes.
  3. Barbecue beef ribs. Jammy fruit and vanilla notes love sweet, smoky barbecue sauce.
    Recipe idea: Season beef ribs with salt, paprika and brown sugar, bake covered at low heat for three hours, then brush with sauce and finish on the grill.
  4. Grilled lamb chops. Soft tannins and spice match the savory, slightly charred flavor of lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper and grill over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes a side.
  5. Pepperoni pizza. Juicy fruit handles the spice and salt of pepperoni and the tang of tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Spread pizza dough with tomato sauce, add mozzarella and pepperoni slices, and bake in a very hot oven until bubbling.
