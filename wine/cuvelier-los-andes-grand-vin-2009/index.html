About this wine

Cuvelier Los Andes belongs to the Cuvelier family of Bordeaux, owners of Château Léoville Poyferré in Saint-Julien, and is one of the wineries of the Clos de los Siete project in Vista Flores, Tunuyán, in the Uco Valley. The estate focuses on age worthy red blends from its own vineyards. The Grand Vin is made from the best wines of each harvest; the 2009 blends 70 percent Malbec with Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah, Merlot and Petit Verdot, and was aged 18 months in barrels, half of them new French oak. Only around 30,000 bottles a year are made.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. A structured, mature blend handles the rich, spiced goat, and its cedar notes suit the slow braise.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat with onion and garlic, add tomato, cumin, a few cloves and a splash of the wine, cover with stock and simmer 2 to 3 hours.
  2. Carni stoba. Firm tannins and dark fruit match beef braised until tender in a peppery tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a little vinegar, cover and simmer 2 hours, adding potatoes near the end.
  3. Grilled ribeye. The marbled steak softens the tannins while the char echoes the oak spice.
    Recipe idea: Bring the ribeye to room temperature, season well, grill over high heat 4 to 5 minutes per side and rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Herb crusted rack of lamb. Cassis and graphite pair with lamb, and the herbs lift the wine's savoury side.
    Recipe idea: Sear the rack, brush with mustard, press on breadcrumbs mixed with parsley, thyme and garlic, then roast about 20 minutes at 200 C.
  5. Red wine braised short ribs. A long braise and a deep sauce suit a mature, layered blend built to age.
    Recipe idea: Brown short ribs, add onion, carrot, garlic, tomato paste, red wine and stock, cover and bake 3 hours at 160 C until falling apart.
