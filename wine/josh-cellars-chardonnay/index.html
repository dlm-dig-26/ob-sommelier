About this wine

Josh Cellars was founded in 2007 by Joseph Carr, who named it after his father Josh. Carr worked as a restaurant wine steward and sommelier before moving into the wine trade. Deutsch Family Wine & Spirits bought the brand in 2011, and Wayne Donaldson has been head winemaker since 2009. This California Chardonnay shows bright citrus, honey and juicy peach, with a light touch of oak for complexity and a balanced, easy finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Honeyed fruit and a round texture go well with melted cheese and the sweet-savoury filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or Gouda with spiced chicken, olives, raisins and capers, cover with cheese slices and bake at 180°C for 30 to 40 minutes.
  2. Galinja stoba. Peach and light oak suit tender chicken in a gently sweet tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then simmer with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic, a little sugar and capers for about 45 minutes.
  3. Grilled salmon. A rounder white with enough body for rich, honey-glazed salmon.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with olive oil, salt, pepper and a little honey, grill skin-side down 6 minutes, flip and cook 2 minutes more.
  4. Roast chicken. Citrus and butter notes echo the lemon and butter on the bird.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, thyme and lemon, roast at 200°C for about 1 hour 15 minutes until the juices run clear.
  5. Creamy chicken pasta. Creamy texture meets cream sauce, while acidity keeps it fresh.
    Recipe idea: Pan-fry chicken strips, add garlic, cream and grated Parmesan, simmer until thick and toss with fettuccine and black pepper.
