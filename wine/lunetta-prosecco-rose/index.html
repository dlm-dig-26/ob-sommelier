About this wine

Lunetta is made by Cavit, a co-operative from Trentino, at its sparkling wine facility near Trento; the name means little moon. This Prosecco Rosé uses Glera from the hills above Treviso in the Veneto, with Pinot Noir made as a red wine and blended in for colour. The Glera is pressed gently and fermented in stainless steel, and the bubbles come from a second fermentation in tank on fine lees. It is pale pink with cherry tints, with redcurrant and citrus, about 11.5 percent alcohol and a clean finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Fish ceviche. Lively bubbles and citrus notes match lime-cured fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, chili, cilantro and salt.
  2. Pastechi with beef. Bubbles cut the fried pastry and the red-berry fruit suits the spiced filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with spiced minced beef, raisins and olives, fold into half-moons, seal and deep-fry until golden.
  3. Garlic grilled shrimp. A touch of sweetness and red fruit flatter sweet shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Toss shrimp with garlic, olive oil, lime and parsley, grill 2 minutes per side and serve right away.
  4. Cala. Fresh, fizzy rosé is ideal with salty fried snacks.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, peel, blend with garlic, chili and salt, then fry spoonfuls until crisp and golden.
  5. Smoked salmon toasts. Pinot Noir in the blend gives enough fruit for smoky salmon.
    Recipe idea: Spread cream cheese on toasted bread, top with smoked salmon, red onion, capers and a squeeze of lemon.
