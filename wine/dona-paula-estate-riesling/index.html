About this wine

Doña Paula is a Mendoza winery owned by Chile's Santa Rita Estates. Its Estate Riesling comes from a small plot at Finca Alluvia in Gualtallary, in the Uco Valley, about 1,350 metres above sea level, where rain is scarce. The winery began planting this sandy, rocky site in 2007 after trialling many grape varieties lower down in Luján de Cuyo, and its team said it took four harvests to make a Riesling ready for release. The first vintage on sale was 2013. The wine is dry and unoaked, with a mineral, lime-led style.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Sharp lime and mineral notes mirror the citrus cure and lift the raw fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, hot pepper and cilantro, chill 20 minutes and serve right away.
  2. Sopi di pisca. A dry, zesty white keeps a light fish soup fresh and brings out its herbs.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces in stock with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, potato and a little hot pepper, then finish with lime and herbs.
  3. Pisca hasa. High acidity cuts through the crisp fried coating like a squeeze of lime.
    Recipe idea: Season whole fish with salt, lime and garlic, dust with flour and fry until crisp. Serve with funchi and a fresh onion sauce.
  4. Sushi rolls. Lime and green apple freshness suits rice, fish and a touch of soy without clashing.
    Recipe idea: Spread sushi rice on nori, add strips of raw tuna or cooked shrimp, cucumber and avocado, roll tightly and slice.
  5. Goat cheese salad. The wine's bright acidity matches the tang of fresh goat cheese.
    Recipe idea: Top mixed greens with crumbled goat cheese, apple slices and toasted walnuts, and dress with olive oil and lemon.
