About this wine

Noble Vines names each of its wines after a grape clone, and 337 is a Cabernet Sauvignon clone that came from Bordeaux in France. The idea behind the brand is to plant each clone where it grows best. For the 337, the fruit comes from the Clay Station Vineyard in Lodi, California, on red soils with cobblestones. The wine is aged for several months in a mix of French and American oak, giving a rich, dark-fruited red.

On the shelf: Label carries the Noble Vines name and the clone number 337, with the line Not All Vines Are Created Equal.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. A full, tannic Cabernet holds its own against rich goat and warm spice.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Dark fruit and mocha notes suit a deep, meaty beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Firm tannins soften against the fat and char of steak.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat for 4 minutes a side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Barbecue ribs. Ripe blackberry and spice stand up to a smoky glaze.
    Recipe idea: Bake pork ribs low and slow wrapped in foil for two hours, then brush with barbecue sauce and finish on a hot grill.
  5. Cheeseburger with bacon. A big red cuts through rich grilled beef and cheese.
    Recipe idea: Grill seasoned beef patties, top with cheddar and crisp bacon and serve in toasted buns with onion and tomato.
