About this wine

Apothic Red is a California red blend first released in 2009 and made every year since. The wine team settled on Zinfandel, Syrah and Merlot after a period of trial blends, then worked with different oak influences to shape the style. Zinfandel brings bold dark fruit and spice, Merlot adds a soft texture and black fruit, Syrah gives a touch of blueberry, and oak rounds it off with gentle vanilla and mocha. The result is a smooth, fruit-forward red made for easy drinking.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe dark fruit and soft tannin stand up to rich, spiced goat stew without clashing with its warmth.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. A smooth, slightly sweet red with mocha notes matches slow-cooked beef in a savory tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, sweet pepper, a splash of soy and Worcestershire, then simmer 90 minutes.
  3. BBQ cheeseburger. Juicy fruit and a hint of sweetness work with smoky beef, melted cheese and tangy barbecue sauce.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season well, grill 4 minutes per side, top with cheddar and serve in a bun with barbecue sauce and onion.
  4. Pulled pork sandwich. The wine's soft fruit and vanilla flatter sweet, smoky pork, while low tannin keeps it easy.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork shoulder with paprika, brown sugar and garlic, roast covered at 150 C for 4 hours, shred and mix with barbecue sauce.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. Dark fruit and gentle spice suit a meaty tomato sauce, and the soft texture keeps it friendly.
    Recipe idea: Cook onion, carrot and celery, brown ground beef, add tomatoes and a splash of red wine, simmer 45 minutes and toss with spaghetti.
