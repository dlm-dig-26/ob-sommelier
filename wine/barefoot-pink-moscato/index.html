About this wine

Barefoot is made by Barefoot Cellars in Modesto, California, and Jen Wall, its head winemaker, has made Barefoot wines since the 1995 harvest. E. & J. Gallo, the family-owned winery founded in 1933 in Modesto by brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo, bought the brand in 2005. Pink Moscato is one of its sweet, light wines: it opens with aromas of raspberry and pomegranate, follows with juicy red fruit and the grapey character of Moscato, and ends with a lingering citrus note. It has a screw cap and comes in 750 ml and 1.5 liter bottles. Serve it well chilled.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Coconut flan. A sweet wine meets a sweet dessert, and the bright red fruit lifts the creamy coconut.
    Recipe idea: Caramelize sugar in a mold, whisk eggs with coconut milk, condensed milk and vanilla, pour in and bake in a water bath until set; chill.
  2. Spicy shrimp in Creole sauce. Clear sweetness and juicy fruit cool the chili heat of a spicy Creole sauce.
    Recipe idea: Saute onion, sweet pepper and garlic, add tomato, a little Madame Jeanette pepper and thyme, simmer, then cook shrimp in the sauce for 3 minutes.
  3. Pastechi. The sweet, fruity wine makes a fun contrast with a salty, savory fried pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with seasoned ground beef or cheese, fold into half moons, seal the edges and fry until golden.
  4. Raspberry cheesecake. Raspberry and pomegranate notes echo the berry topping, and the sweetness matches the dessert.
    Recipe idea: Press crushed biscuits with butter into a pan, top with cream cheese whipped with sugar, eggs and vanilla, bake, cool and spoon over raspberry sauce.
  5. Tropical fruit salad. Light and juicy, it matches ripe fruit and adds a bright, refreshing finish.
    Recipe idea: Combine mango, papaya, pineapple, watermelon and berries; add a squeeze of lime and a few mint leaves and chill before serving.
