About this wine

Yellow Tail is made by Casella Family Brands in Yenda, a small town in the Riverina region of New South Wales. Filippo and Maria Casella founded Casella Wines there in 1969, and John Casella introduced the Yellow Tail brand around 2000 to 2001. Nearly a third of the fruit comes from the family's own vineyards in the Riverina. This Sauvignon Blanc carries the South Eastern Australia label and is made without oak, keeping the focus on fresh passionfruit, grapefruit and citrus, with bright acidity and a clean finish.

On the shelf: Brightly coloured label with the yellow-footed rock-wallaby logo; screw cap.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Zesty citrus and high acidity mirror the lime in the fish and keep it fresh.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili and cilantro, chill 20 minutes and serve with crackers.
  2. Pisca hasa. Crisp acidity cuts through fried fish and lifts the Creole sauce.
    Recipe idea: Season snapper with salt, lime and garlic, fry until golden and serve with a sauce of onion, sweet pepper and tomato.
  3. Grilled red snapper. A light, unoaked white that lets delicate grilled fish shine.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic and herbs, grill 6 to 8 minutes per side and serve with lime wedges.
  4. Grilled shrimp. Tropical fruit notes pair well with sweet, smoky shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Toss shrimp with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest and paprika, grill 2 minutes per side and finish with parsley.
  5. Goat cheese salad. The wine's herbal edge and acidity match tangy fresh goat cheese.
    Recipe idea: Toss mixed greens with cucumber and cherry tomatoes, top with crumbled fresh goat cheese and a lemon vinaigrette.
