About this wine

Santa Rita 120 is made by Viña Santa Rita in Buin, Chile, from Central Valley grapes. The name remembers 120 Chilean patriots who hid in the cellars of the Santa Rita estate after the Battle of Rancagua in 1814. Some contact with oak gives the Cabernet Sauvignon gentle vanilla and tobacco notes alongside its ripe fruit. The range got new packaging in 2017, with an embossed horseman on the bottle.

On the shelf: Sleek bottle with an embossed soldier on horseback, honouring the 120 patriots in the wine's name.

Bottle sizes made: 375 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Ripe dark fruit and moderate tannins match beef stewed in tomato.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Juicy fruit and a little vanilla suit goat with cumin and pepper.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Grilled steak. A classic Cabernet match: tannins and fruit meet charred beef.
    Recipe idea: Season sirloin with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 3 to 4 minutes a side and rest before slicing.
  4. Cheeseburger. An easy Cabernet works with grilled beef and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season well, grill, top with cheddar and serve in toasted buns with onion and pickles.
  5. Chili con carne. Ripe fruit balances a mildly spicy, meaty chili.
    Recipe idea: Brown ground beef with onion and garlic, add beans, tomatoes, cumin and chili powder and simmer for an hour.
