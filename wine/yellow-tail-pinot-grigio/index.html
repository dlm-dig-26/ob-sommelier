About this wine

Yellow Tail is a brand of Casella Family Brands, the family winery founded by Filippo and Maria Casella in 1969 in Yenda, New South Wales. Casella launched Yellow Tail in the United States in 2001. This Pinot Grigio is made from grapes sourced across South Eastern Australia. It is light and crisp, with pear, red apple and passionfruit, at a modest 11.5 percent alcohol, and it is sealed with a screw cap.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Zesty citrus and a touch of fruit sweetness balance lime and a little chili.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, chili, cilantro and salt.
  2. Pisca hasa. Light and crisp, it refreshes the palate between bites of fried fish.
    Recipe idea: Season snapper fillets with lime, salt and garlic, dust with flour and fry until golden. Serve with lime wedges and a fresh salad.
  3. Fried calamari. Fresh acidity cuts through the crunchy coating, like a squeeze of lemon.
    Recipe idea: Cut squid into rings, toss in flour seasoned with salt and paprika and fry at 180°C for 2 minutes. Serve with lemon.
  4. Garlic shrimp. Pear and apple notes stay bright next to garlic and butter.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp in butter and olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 3 minutes. Serve with bread or rice.
  5. Grilled chicken. A light white that suits simple lime and herb marinated chicken.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in garlic, lime, oregano and olive oil, then grill 6 to 7 minutes per side until cooked through.
