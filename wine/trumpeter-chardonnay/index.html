About this wine

Trumpeter is a range from Rutini Wines, whose roots go back to Felipe Rutini and the founding of Bodega La Rural in Mendoza in 1885. Rutini now makes its wines in Tupungato in the Uco Valley, with winemaker Mariano Di Paola. This Chardonnay comes from high-altitude vineyards in Tupungato. About 30 percent of the wine goes through malolactic fermentation and five months in oak while the rest stays in tank, giving a fresh white with tropical fruit, green apple, a touch of vanilla, crisp acidity and a mineral finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. Crisp acidity and citrus fruit flatter lime-seasoned grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole red snapper, rub with lime, garlic and salt, grill 6 to 8 minutes per side and serve with Creole sauce.
  2. Pisca hasa. Fresh acidity cuts through the fried coating and suits the Creole sauce.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets with lime, garlic and salt, dust with flour, fry until golden and serve with funchi and Creole sauce.
  3. Keshi yena. A light touch of oak and cream works with melted cheese and chicken.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Gouda or Edam with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, top with cheese and bake 30 minutes.
  4. Chicken in mushroom cream sauce. The vanilla note matches a creamy sauce while the acidity keeps it light.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken breasts, add mushrooms, garlic and cream, and simmer 15 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  5. Garlic grilled shrimp. Pineapple and green apple flavours sit nicely with sweet shrimp and garlic.
    Recipe idea: Toss shrimp with garlic, olive oil, lime and parsley, grill 2 minutes per side and serve right away.
