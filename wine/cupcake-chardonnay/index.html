About this wine

Cupcake Vineyards is a California winery based in Ripon, with Jessica Tomei as winemaker. For this Chardonnay the grapes come from cool coastal vineyards in Monterey County, where morning fog and a long growing season let the fruit ripen slowly while keeping its natural acidity. The wine is barrel fermented, which gives it a creamy, rich texture. Expect citrus, pear and green apple with floral notes and a light touch of oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. A creamy, round white stands up to melted Gouda while citrus keeps it fresh.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or Gouda with spiced chicken, olives, raisins and capers, cover with cheese slices and bake at 180°C for 30 to 40 minutes.
  2. Galinja stoba. Ripe fruit and a touch of oak suit tender chicken in a mild tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then simmer with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic, a little sugar and capers for about 45 minutes.
  3. Garlic shrimp. Buttery texture and fresh acidity balance garlic and sweet shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Sauté peeled shrimp in olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 2 to 3 minutes, finish with lemon juice.
  4. Roast chicken. Pear, apple and gentle oak match crisp skin and herbs.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, thyme and lemon, roast at 200°C for about 1 hour 15 minutes until the juices run clear.
  5. Creamy chicken pasta. A rich, creamy Chardonnay mirrors a cream sauce, and its acidity keeps each bite light.
    Recipe idea: Pan-fry chicken strips, add garlic, cream and grated Parmesan, simmer until thick and toss with fettuccine and black pepper.
