About this wine

Casarena is a family-owned winery in Perdriel, Luján de Cuyo, Mendoza. Its name means sand house and refers to the restored 1930s stone building at Brandsen 505, the street number that gives this wine its name. The wine project began in 2007, and the family owns vineyards in Agrelo and Perdriel. The 505 Malbec is 100% Malbec, closed with a screw cap and suitable for vegans. It is juicy and medium-bodied, with red fruit and plum, soft tannins and a spicy finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Beef empanadas. Juicy Malbec and a spiced beef filling are a natural Argentine pairing.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef cooked with onion, cumin, paprika, olives and egg, fold, crimp the edges and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  2. Carni stoba. Plum and soft tannins sit comfortably with tender stewed beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  3. Galinja stoba. A medium-bodied red with soft tannins that will not overpower chicken in tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then simmer with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic, a little sugar and capers for about 45 minutes.
  4. Barbecue chicken. Red fruit and a spicy finish suit smoky, charred chicken.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in barbecue sauce with garlic and a little hot pepper, grill until charred and cooked through, brushing with more sauce.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. Fresh acidity and cherry fruit match tomato and beef.
    Recipe idea: Cook ground beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and a splash of red wine, simmer 45 minutes and serve over spaghetti.
