About this wine

Barefoot is a California wine brand made by Barefoot Cellars, part of E. and J. Gallo Winery in Modesto. Fruitscato is its range of sweet, fruit inspired wines, with watermelon one of nine flavours. This one starts from California grown Muscat grapes and is blended with natural watermelon flavour, giving a light, low alcohol wine of about 7.5 percent that is meant to be served well chilled. Retailers also suggest it as a base for sangria, spritzers or a frozen rosé.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Spicy shrimp ceviche. Sweetness and low alcohol calm chilli heat, while the juicy melon fruit suits lime and seafood.
    Recipe idea: Cook shrimp briefly, chop and marinate in lime juice with red onion, cilantro, a little Madame Jeanette pepper and diced mango for 20 minutes.
  2. Pastechi. The wine's sweet, fresh fruit offsets the salty, fried pastry and spiced filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with spiced ground beef or cheese, fold, seal with a fork and deep fry until golden; serve warm.
  3. Watermelon feta salad. Feta's saltiness balances the sweetness, and the watermelon in the glass mirrors the plate.
    Recipe idea: Cube watermelon, add crumbled feta, mint, thin red onion and a squeeze of lime; drizzle with a little olive oil.
  4. Thai red curry with chicken. Sweet wines are a good match for spicy, coconut rich dishes, cooling the heat.
    Recipe idea: Fry red curry paste, add coconut milk, sliced chicken and vegetables, simmer 10 minutes and finish with lime and basil; serve with rice.
  5. Fresh fruit tart. A light, sweet dessert matches the wine's sweetness so neither tastes flat.
    Recipe idea: Fill a baked tart shell with vanilla custard and top with strawberries, kiwi and peach slices; glaze with warm apricot jam.
