About this wine

Main & Vine is an everyday range from Beringer, the California winery founded in St. Helena in the Napa Valley by the brothers Jacob and Frederick Beringer. This Cabernet Sauvignon is made from grapes grown across California and picked at full ripeness to keep the fruit forward. The winemaking adds sweet oak spice for a soft, round, easy-going red. Beringer was one of the first wineries to use gravity-fed cellars and hand-dug caves.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Ripe dark fruit and soft tannins match the rich tomato gravy of a slow beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Jammy fruit stands up to goat and warm cumin without clashing with the spice.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Cheeseburger. Soft tannins and vanilla oak love grilled beef and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season well, grill to your liking, top with cheddar and serve in toasted buns with onion and pickles.
  4. Barbecue ribs. Fruit-forward and round, it handles a smoky, slightly sweet glaze.
    Recipe idea: Bake pork ribs low and slow wrapped in foil for two hours, then brush with barbecue sauce and finish on a hot grill.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. Medium body and ripe fruit suit a meaty tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown ground beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add tomatoes and a splash of red wine, simmer for an hour and toss with spaghetti.
