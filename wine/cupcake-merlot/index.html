About this wine

Cupcake Vineyards is a California wine brand owned by The Wine Group. This Merlot is made from 100 percent Merlot grown on the Central Coast of California, where warm days and cool nights shaped by the Pacific give the grapes a long growing season. Aging on American oak adds richness and notes of cocoa and vanilla. It is medium-bodied and fruit-forward, with ripe plum and red cherry, soft tannins and a medium-length finish, at 14.5 percent alcohol.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Soft tannins and ripe plum fruit sit well with slow-braised beef in a savory tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, then simmer with onion, tomato, sweet pepper, garlic and a splash of wine for about two hours. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Galinja stoba. Medium body and gentle oak suit stewed chicken without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and stock, and simmer until tender. Finish with olives and capers.
  3. Grilled lamb chops. Juicy red fruit and a touch of oak match the savory char of the lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, salt and olive oil, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and let them rest before serving.
  4. Mushroom risotto. The cocoa and earthy notes in the wine echo the mushrooms.
    Recipe idea: Saute mushrooms and onion, toast arborio rice, add hot stock a ladle at a time until creamy, then stir in butter and parmesan.
  5. Pepperoni pizza. Fruity and soft, it handles the spice and richness of cured sausage with ease.
    Recipe idea: Spread tomato sauce on pizza dough, add mozzarella and pepperoni, and bake in a very hot oven until the crust is crisp.
