About this wine

Yellow Tail is made by Casella Family Brands in Yenda, Australia. This Moscato is a sweet, lively white with a light, gentle fizz and just 7.5 percent alcohol, from grapes grown in South Eastern Australia. The producer describes white peach, passionfruit, orange blossom, kiwi and melon, and lists the wine as vegan friendly. Its sweetness and low alcohol make it a natural partner for fruit desserts and for spicy food, where the sugar softens the heat.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Spicy shrimp creole. The sweetness calms the chili heat while the fruit matches the sweet pepper and tomato.
    Recipe idea: Cook shrimp in a sauce of onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and Madame Jeanette pepper for 5 minutes and serve with white rice.
  2. Cala. Light fizz cuts through the fried batter and cools the chili in these bean fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, peel, blend with garlic, chili and salt, then fry spoonfuls until crisp and golden.
  3. Tropical fruit salad. Peach and passionfruit flavours mirror mango and pineapple; the wine is as sweet as the fruit.
    Recipe idea: Cut mango, pineapple, papaya and melon into cubes, add lime juice and fresh mint, and chill before serving.
  4. Coconut flan. A sweet wine is needed with dessert, and the floral notes suit coconut and caramel.
    Recipe idea: Blend coconut milk, condensed milk, eggs and grated coconut, pour into a caramel-lined dish and bake in a water bath for 50 minutes.
  5. Spicy Thai chicken curry. Sweet, low-alcohol Moscato tames chili and coconut better than a dry wine.
    Recipe idea: Fry red curry paste, add coconut milk, chicken strips and vegetables, simmer 10 minutes and serve with jasmine rice.
