About this wine

Casillero del Diablo is a range made by Concha y Toro in Chile. This Reserva Red Blend comes from the Central Valley. A review of the 2017 vintage lists 70 percent Syrah and 30 percent Cabernet Sauvignon, aged in oak barrels; the blend can change from year to year. It is deep purple, with ripe black fruit, plum, black cherry, dark chocolate and toasty spice, a velvety feel and a touch of sweetness.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Ripe black fruit and a velvety texture suit a rich beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a splash of vinegar, and simmer about two hours until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Pastechi. Smooth and fruity, it is a good match for spiced beef pastechi.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry with spiced minced beef or Gouda cheese, fold into half-moons, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  3. Beef empanadas. Plum and spice go well with cumin and paprika in the filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with minced beef cooked with onion, paprika, cumin, olives and egg, fold, crimp and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  4. BBQ pork ribs. A touch of sweetness matches a sticky barbecue glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, brown sugar and garlic, bake covered at 150°C for 2 hours, then grill with barbecue sauce until sticky.
  5. Grilled steak. Body and chocolate notes stand up to grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 3 to 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
