About this wine

Cavicchioli 1928 is based in San Prospero, near Modena in Emilia-Romagna. Umberto Cavicchioli began bottling wine under the family name in 1928, and today his grandsons run the winery, with Sandro as winemaker. This Prosecco DOC is 100% Glera from the province of Treviso. The grapes are picked by hand, slightly before full ripeness, and the second fermentation takes place in tank using the Charmat method. The result is pale and lively, with fine bubbles, candied fruit and white flowers, apple, pear and white peach.

On the shelf: Label with a floral design.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi. Lively bubbles and crisp acidity cleanse the palate after crisp, salty pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with spiced ground beef, cheese or tuna, fold into half moons, seal the edges and deep-fry until golden.
  2. Ceviche. Green apple and citrus freshness match lime-cured fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt.
  3. Keshi yena. High acidity and bubbles cut through rich melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or Gouda with spiced chicken, olives, raisins and capers, cover with cheese slices and bake at 180°C for 30 to 40 minutes.
  4. Sushi rolls. A light, off-dry Prosecco suits rice, soy and a little ginger.
    Recipe idea: Roll seasoned sushi rice and nori around cucumber, avocado and crab or salmon, slice and serve with soy sauce and pickled ginger.
  5. Fried chicken. Bubbles lift the crunchy coating and a hint of sweetness rounds out the salt.
    Recipe idea: Soak chicken pieces in seasoned buttermilk, coat in spiced flour and fry at 170°C for 12 to 15 minutes until golden and cooked through.
