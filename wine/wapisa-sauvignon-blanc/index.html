About this wine

Wapisa is made by Fincas Patagónicas, the family company also behind Tapiz in Mendoza. Its vineyard, the Los Acantilados estate at San Javier near Viedma in Río Negro, sits close to the Atlantic coast of Patagonia, where cool sea winds shape the grapes. The winemaker is Fabián Valenzuela, with Bordeaux consultant Jean Claude Berrouet advising since 2012. The winery is also known for an undersea cellar in the Golfo San Matías. A Canadian retailer lists this Sauvignon Blanc as aged eight months in new and used French and American oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Sharp acidity and a salty edge mirror lime-cured fish.
    Recipe idea: Cut fresh snapper into cubes, marinate in lime juice with red onion, chili and cilantro for 15 minutes and serve chilled.
  2. Pisca hasa. The crisp, herbal wine cleans the palate after golden fried fish.
    Recipe idea: Season fish with lime, garlic and salt, dust in flour, fry until crisp and top with sautéed onion and sweet pepper.
  3. Sopi di pisca. Fresh herbs and citrus in the wine lift a light fish soup.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, garlic, celery, potato and tomato in water with lime, then finish with fresh herbs.
  4. Grilled garlic shrimp. Citrus and a saline finish love sweet, garlicky shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Toss shrimp with olive oil, garlic, chili and salt, grill two minutes per side and finish with lemon and parsley.
  5. Goat cheese salad. Classic Sauvignon Blanc match: high acidity balances tangy, creamy cheese.
    Recipe idea: Top mixed greens with warm goat cheese on toast, sliced apple, walnuts and a mustard vinaigrette.
