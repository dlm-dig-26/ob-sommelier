About this wine

Barefoot traces its roots to 1965, when Davis Bynum made the first Barefoot Bynum Burgundy in his garage in Albany, California. In 1986 Bonnie Harvey and Michael Houlihan bought the brand and relaunched it as Barefoot Cellars with its footprint label, and E. & J. Gallo acquired it in 2005. Jennifer Wall has been the winemaker since 1995, and the winery is based in Modesto. This Red Moscato is a sweet and vibrant red, with juicy red cherry, raspberry and floral aromas. It is best served well chilled.

On the shelf: Look for the Barefoot name and the brand signature bare footprint on the label.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi with hot sauce. Sweet berry fruit cools the chili heat of crisp, savory pastechi.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with spiced ground beef or cheese, fold, seal and fry until golden. Serve hot with a spicy pepper sauce.
  2. Keshi yena. Its sweetness plays well with the raisins, olives and salty melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives and raisins, cover with more cheese and bake until bubbling.
  3. Spicy chicken wings. A sweet, chilled red balances fiery, sticky wings.
    Recipe idea: Toss chicken wings with salt and paprika, bake at 220 C until crisp, then coat in a mix of hot sauce, honey and melted butter.
  4. Chocolate brownies. Red cherry and raspberry flavors pair naturally with rich chocolate.
    Recipe idea: Melt butter with dark chocolate, beat in sugar, eggs and a little flour, pour into a tin and bake about 25 minutes until just set.
  5. Strawberries and cream. Juicy red fruit in the glass mirrors fresh berries in the bowl.
    Recipe idea: Hull and halve ripe strawberries, sprinkle with a little sugar and serve with lightly whipped cream.
