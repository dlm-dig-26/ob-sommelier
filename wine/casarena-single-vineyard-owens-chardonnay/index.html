About this wine

Casarena is a family winery in Luján de Cuyo, Mendoza, run from a restored 1937 bodega in Perdriel. The Dartley family owns it and named their four estate vineyards after their grandchildren: Naoki, Lauren, Owen and Jamilla. This Chardonnay belongs to the Single Vineyard range and comes from the Owen vineyard in Agrelo, at about 960 meters above sea level, where old vines planted in 1933 grow on traditional pergolas over sand, clay, pebbles and limestone sediments. Winemaker Martina Galeano and the team study each parcel to show its own character.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. Fresh, mineral acidity and citrus fruit lift the clean, flaky flesh, while the creamy texture matches the char.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, and grill 6 to 8 minutes per side. Serve with lime wedges.
  2. Keshi yena. The wine's creamy texture and gentle oak handle the melted cheese and savory chicken filling with ease.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, pepper, olives, raisins and capers, top with cheese and bake until bubbling.
  3. Garlic butter shrimp. Citrus and peach notes and a round mouthfeel pair naturally with buttery, garlicky shellfish.
    Recipe idea: Saute shrimp in butter with plenty of garlic and a pinch of chili, finish with lemon juice and parsley, and serve with bread or rice.
  4. Roast chicken. A medium-bodied white with subtle oak matches crisp skin and juicy meat without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, salt, thyme and lemon zest, put the lemon inside and roast at 200 C for about 75 minutes.
  5. Mushroom risotto. Creamy rice and earthy mushrooms meet the wine's creamy texture, and its acidity keeps each bite fresh.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio rice in butter with onion, add a splash of wine, then stock ladle by ladle with sauteed mushrooms. Finish with Parmesan.
