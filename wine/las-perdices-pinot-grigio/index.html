About this wine

Viña Las Perdices is a family winery in Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo, in Mendoza. Its roots go back to Don Juan Munoz-Lopez, who came from Andalucía in the early 1950s to grow grapes here. His sons grew up among the vines, and in 2004 they built the winery in the heart of the family vineyard. Pinot Grigio from this warm, sunny part of Mendoza is usually made as a fresh, unoaked white, picked to keep its crisp fruit and light body.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Crisp acidity and citrus notes match the lime in the fish and keep each bite fresh.
    Recipe idea: Cube firm white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chopped hot pepper and cilantro, rest 20 minutes in the fridge and serve cold.
  2. Pisca hasa. A light, fresh white cuts through fried fish without covering its delicate flavour.
    Recipe idea: Season red snapper with salt, lime and garlic, dust with flour and fry until golden. Serve with funchi or fried plantain.
  3. Garlic shrimp pasta. Pear and citrus fruit lift the garlic and butter while the light body leaves the shrimp in front.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp in butter and garlic, add a splash of the wine and lemon zest, then toss with spaghetti and parsley.
  4. Greek salad with feta. Fresh acidity stands up to tomato, lemon and salty cheese.
    Recipe idea: Mix tomato, cucumber, red onion, olives and feta, and dress with olive oil, lemon juice and oregano.
  5. Lemon herb chicken. Simple roast chicken with lemon suits a dry, clean white with soft fruit.
    Recipe idea: Rub chicken thighs with lemon, garlic, thyme and olive oil and roast at 200 °C for 35 minutes until golden.
