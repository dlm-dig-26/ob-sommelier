About this wine

Barefoot is made by Barefoot Cellars in Modesto, California, where head winemaker Jen Wall has made its wines since the 1995 harvest. E. & J. Gallo, the family-owned winery founded in 1933 by brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo, bought the brand in 2005. This Pinot Noir is a smooth, fruit-forward California red with dark cherry, raspberry and a hint of strawberry, soft brown spice and a silky finish. Its tannins are mild, so it is easy to enjoy at room temperature or lightly chilled. It comes in 750 ml and 1.5 liter bottles.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Soft red fruit and mild tannins sit well with melted Gouda and gently spiced chicken.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, cover with more cheese and bake until bubbling.
  2. Galinja stoba. A light, juicy red that matches tender stewed chicken without heavy tannins.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, sweet pepper, garlic, tomato, capers and olives, then simmer with a little broth until tender.
  3. Grilled salmon. Pinot Noir is light enough for rich fish, and its cherry notes suit the smoky char.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with olive oil, garlic and lemon, season with salt and pepper and grill skin side down for 4 to 5 minutes per side.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms bring out the savory side of the wine, while its fruit keeps the dish lively.
    Recipe idea: Saute mushrooms and onion in butter, stir in arborio rice, add hot broth a ladle at a time until creamy, and finish with parmesan.
  5. Mushroom pizza. Soft fruit and gentle spice suit earthy mushrooms and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Spread tomato sauce on pizza dough, top with sliced mushrooms, mozzarella and thyme, and bake in a very hot oven until the crust is crisp.
