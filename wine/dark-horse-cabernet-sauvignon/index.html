About this wine

Dark Horse is a California wine brand based in Modesto. Its Cabernet Sauvignon is a blend of California grapes, and in the 2022 vintage the fruit came from select vineyards in Lodi. The grapes are picked in the cool morning hours, each lot is fermented and kept apart before blending, and some lots stay on their skins for up to 28 days for more depth and a richer feel in the mouth. Winemaker Beth Liston aims to balance big, ripe fruit with a dry, savory side. Expect a full-bodied red with dark berries, coffee, toasted oak and a hint of dark chocolate.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Full body and firm tannins stand up to a rich, well-spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with garlic, lime and cumin, brown it, add onion, tomato and Scotch bonnet, then simmer slowly for about 2 hours.
  2. Carni stoba. Dark fruit and toasted oak match the deep flavors of slow-braised beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, peppers, tomato, cumin and a splash of the wine; simmer covered for 2 hours until tender.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Firm tannins and dark berry fruit are made for fatty, charred beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat for 4 to 5 minutes a side and rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Grilled cheeseburgers. Ripe fruit and hints of cola and coffee suit smoky grilled beef and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season well, grill 4 minutes a side, top with cheddar to melt and serve in toasted buns.
  5. Cottage pie. Savory minced beef and mashed potato match this wine in weight and warmth.
    Recipe idea: Brown minced beef with onion and carrot, add tomato paste and stock, simmer 20 minutes, top with mashed potato and bake until golden.
