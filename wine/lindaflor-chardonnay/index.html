About this wine

Bodega Monteviejo was the first winery completed at Clos de los Siete in Vista Flores, in the Uco Valley, in 2001. The project was started by Michel Rolland, who invited French wine families to Mendoza. Monteviejo was led by Catherine Péré-Vergé, owner of Château Le Gay in Bordeaux, and is now run by her son Henry Parent. Winemaker Marcelo Pelleriti trained under Rolland. Lindaflor is the top tier at Monteviejo, made as small-lot microvinifications from hand-harvested grapes and aged in barrel. The Chardonnay comes from French clones planted in Vista Flores and shows ripe fruit framed by oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. A creamy, oak-rounded Chardonnay matches melted Gouda and the savoury chicken filling.
    Recipe idea: Hollow a Gouda or line a dish with slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, cover with cheese and bake until bubbling.
  2. Grilled red snapper with creole sauce. Ripe pear and peach and a fresh mineral finish carry the sweet pepper and tomato of a mild creole sauce.
    Recipe idea: Grill snapper fillets with garlic and lime; simmer onion, sweet pepper and tomato with a pinch of thyme and spoon over the fish.
  3. Lobster with garlic butter. Buttery, toasty notes in the wine echo garlic butter and the sweetness of lobster.
    Recipe idea: Split lobster tails, brush with butter melted with garlic, parsley and lemon, and grill 6 to 8 minutes until just opaque.
  4. Chicken in creamy mushroom sauce. Body and hazelnut notes stand up to a rich cream sauce without feeling heavy.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken thighs and set aside; sauté mushrooms and shallot, add stock and cream, return the chicken and simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Sopi di pisca. Mineral freshness and ripe citrus brighten a fragrant fish soup with herbs and vegetables.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish heads and bones with onion, garlic, celery and bay for stock; strain, add potato, carrot and fish chunks, cook 10 minutes and finish with lime.
