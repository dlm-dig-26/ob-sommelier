About this wine

Josh Cellars was founded by Joseph Carr, who named the brand after his father Josh, a lumberjack, US Army veteran and volunteer firefighter. Its winemaker is Australian-born Wayne Donaldson, in the role since 2009, and the brand works with Deutsch Family Wine and Spirits. For this Pinot Noir, a US retailer gives the appellation as Central Coast, California. It is made in a silky, approachable style with red cherry and berry fruit and a light touch of toasted oak.

On the shelf: The name Josh is written in a flowing cursive script on the label.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. A light, low-tannin red is one of the few that works with grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic, salt and olive oil, and grill about six minutes per side.
  2. Galinja stoba. Red cherry fruit and gentle tannins suit tender stewed chicken.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper and a splash of soy sauce, and simmer until cooked through.
  3. Roast pork. Juicy fruit and fresh acidity balance rich, crackly pork.
    Recipe idea: Rub a pork loin with garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper and roast at 190 C until just cooked; rest before slicing.
  4. Grilled salmon. Classic Pinot Noir match: silky fruit meets an oily, flavourful fish.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with olive oil, salt and a little honey and soy, and grill skin side down until just cooked.
  5. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms and toasty oak bring out the wine's savoury side.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio rice in butter, add stock little by little, stir in sautéed mushrooms and finish with Parmesan.
