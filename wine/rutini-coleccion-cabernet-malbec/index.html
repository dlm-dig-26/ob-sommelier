About this wine

Rutini Wines traces its roots to 1885, when Italian immigrant Felipe Rutini founded the winery in Coquimbito, Mendoza. Today it farms high altitude vineyards in the Uco Valley and Luján de Cuyo. The Colección Cabernet-Malbec is an equal blend of Cabernet Sauvignon and Malbec from the Uco Valley, aged about twelve months in French oak, mostly in used barrels with a share of new ones, which keeps the fruit in front while adding structure and spice.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Slow cooked beef in a rich sauce has the weight to meet this wine's firm tannins and dark fruit.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and stock, then simmer covered 2 hours; serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Goat stew's bold spice and fat soften the tannins and bring out the plum and pepper notes.
    Recipe idea: Sear goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin, a little hot pepper and stock, then cook slowly until tender.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Fatty grilled beef and a structured Cabernet-Malbec blend are a classic match.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 to 5 minutes per side, rest and slice.
  4. Roast leg of lamb. Herb crusted lamb picks up the wine's blackcurrant, cedar and spice.
    Recipe idea: Stud lamb with garlic and rosemary, rub with olive oil and roast at 180 C about 1.5 hours; rest before carving.
  5. Beef burgers. A juicy burger from the grill handles the tannin and makes an easy weeknight pairing.
    Recipe idea: Shape seasoned ground beef into patties, grill 4 minutes per side, top with cheese and serve with grilled onions.
