About this wine

Lindaflor is the top line of Bodega Monteviejo, a winery in Vista Flores in Mendoza's Uco Valley. Built in 2001, it was the first winery completed within Clos de los Siete, the project Michel Rolland started with a group of French wine families. Monteviejo was owned by Catherine Péré Vergé of Château Le Gay in Pomerol, and her son Henry Parent now leads it. Lindaflor wines are made as small-lot microvinifications from the estate vineyards, with hand selection and barrel aging. Winemaker Marcelo Pelleriti began his career as head winemaker here.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Rich goat stew with cumin and pepper meets the blend's dark fruit and firm tannins.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a scotch bonnet, then simmer slowly for about two hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. Slow-cooked beef in a savory sauce matches the wine's weight and smooth structure.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste, a splash of vinegar and stock; simmer until tender and serve with funchi.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Charred fat and meat juices soften the tannins and bring out the ripe fruit.
    Recipe idea: Rub the steak with salt, pepper and garlic, grill over high heat a few minutes per side and let it rest before slicing.
  4. Rosemary lamb chops. Herbs and full-flavoured lamb suit the spicy, dark-fruited side of the blend.
    Recipe idea: Marinate the chops in olive oil, rosemary, garlic and lemon zest for an hour, then grill or pan-sear until pink inside.
  5. Pasta with meat ragù. Tomato acidity and savory meat keep the wine fresh and balanced.
    Recipe idea: Simmer ground beef with onion, garlic, crushed tomatoes and oregano for an hour; toss with pasta and grated cheese.
