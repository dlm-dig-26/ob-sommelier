About this wine

Rutini Wines traces its roots to 1885, when Felipe Rutini, an Italian immigrant from the Marche, founded a winery in Mendoza, and the family planted its first vines in Tupungato in 1925. Antología is a limited series that began in 1997, with each edition numbered in Roman numerals and its plots chosen anew each vintage. Edition XXXVIII is listed as 35% Malbec from Altamira, 35% Malbec from Gualtallary, plus 10% each of Petit Verdot, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon, all from the Uco Valley. Half the blend spends 12 months in French oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. A full Malbec blend with spice and fine tannins matches rich, slow-cooked goat.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic and cumin, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper.
  2. Carni stoba. Ripe cherry fruit and velvety texture suit tender beef in a deep tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper and a splash of vinegar, then simmer for two hours.
  3. Grilled steak. The Argentine classic: Malbec and beef from the grill.
    Recipe idea: Salt a thick steak generously, grill over hot coals and serve with chimichurri made of parsley, garlic, oregano, oil and vinegar.
  4. Roast lamb. Mint and spice notes in the wine pick up herbs and roasted lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub a leg of lamb with garlic, rosemary, olive oil and salt and roast until pink inside; rest before slicing.
  5. Beef empanadas. Juicy fruit and soft tannins pair well with a spiced, savoury filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef, onion, cumin, paprika, egg and olives, seal and bake until golden.
