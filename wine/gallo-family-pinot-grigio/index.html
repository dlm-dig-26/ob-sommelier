About this wine

Gallo Family Vineyards goes back to 1933, when brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo started making wine in California, and the company is still family owned. This Pinot Grigio is a Wine of California made from grapes grown in the Central Valley, at the foot of the Sierra Nevada, where warm days and cool nights let the grapes ripen slowly. It is a pale, light-bodied white with melon, lemon, green apple and pear, zesty acidity, a touch of sweetness and a clean, short finish. Serve it well chilled.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pisca hasa. A light, crisp white with citrus notes keeps fried fish fresh without covering its flavor.
    Recipe idea: Season red snapper with salt, lime and garlic, dust with flour and fry in hot oil until golden; serve with lime wedges.
  2. Ceviche. Zesty acidity and lemon notes echo the lime that cures the fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, cilantro and a little hot pepper, and chill for 20 minutes.
  3. Grilled lemon chicken. Melon and green apple flavors suit simple grilled chicken without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken in lemon juice, olive oil, garlic and oregano for an hour, then grill until cooked through.
  4. Garlic shrimp pasta. The light body and clean finish match a buttery shrimp pasta.
    Recipe idea: Saute shrimp in butter and garlic, add lemon juice and parsley, and toss with freshly cooked spaghetti.
  5. Greek salad. Fresh acidity stands up to tomato, feta and olives in a crisp summer salad.
    Recipe idea: Mix tomato, cucumber, red onion, olives and feta, then dress with olive oil, oregano and a little vinegar.
