About this wine

Trumpeter is a line from Rutini Wines, whose winery sits at Tupungato in Mendoza, Argentina. This Pinot Noir comes from Mendoza, where high, cool vineyard sites help the grape keep its fresh acidity. The winemaker describes a ruby wine with a delicate, silky texture and aromas of raspberry, strawberry and violet with some oak. In the mouth the tannins are soft and fine, the acidity is vibrant and the finish is smooth, which makes it a light, food-friendly red.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. Light body, low tannin and bright acidity let a red go with meaty grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, and grill 6 to 8 minutes per side. Serve with funchi.
  2. Galinja stoba. Red berry fruit and soft tannins suit chicken stewed in a mild tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, capers and olives. Simmer 40 minutes and serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled salmon. Pinot Noir's acidity and silky texture are a classic match for rich salmon.
    Recipe idea: Season salmon fillets with salt, pepper and a little soy sauce. Grill skin side down 4 minutes, flip and cook 2 more.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms bring out the wine's subtle oak and red fruit.
    Recipe idea: Fry onion and mushrooms in butter, add arborio rice, then stock a ladle at a time for 18 minutes. Finish with parmesan.
  5. Roast chicken. A delicate red that will not overpower tender white meat and crispy skin.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, garlic, thyme and salt. Roast at 200 C for about 75 minutes until the juices run clear.
