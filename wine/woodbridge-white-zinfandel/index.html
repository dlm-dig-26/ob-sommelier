About this wine

Woodbridge comes from the Robert Mondavi Winery and is linked to Lodi, California. This White Zinfandel is mostly Zinfandel, with some Muscat and a little Grenache. The pink juice is drawn off with the saignée method and fermented cold in stainless steel, with no oak. The result is a light, semi-sweet rosé with about 9.5 percent alcohol and flavours of strawberry, cherry and watermelon. Serve it well chilled.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 500 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Spicy shrimp creole. Gentle sweetness and low alcohol calm the chili heat.
    Recipe idea: Cook shrimp in a sauce of onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and Madame Jeanette pepper for 5 minutes and serve with white rice.
  2. Cala. A chilled, fruity rosé refreshes after crisp, peppery fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, peel, blend with garlic, chili and salt, then fry spoonfuls until crisp and golden.
  3. Tropical fruit salad. Strawberry and watermelon flavours match fresh tropical fruit.
    Recipe idea: Cut mango, pineapple, papaya and melon into cubes, add lime juice and fresh mint, and chill before serving.
  4. Spicy Thai chicken curry. A semi-sweet wine handles chili and coconut better than a dry one.
    Recipe idea: Fry red curry paste, add coconut milk, chicken strips and vegetables, simmer 10 minutes and serve with jasmine rice.
  5. Strawberries and whipped cream. The red-berry flavours in the wine echo the fruit, and the dessert is no sweeter than the wine.
    Recipe idea: Slice fresh strawberries, sprinkle with a little sugar and lime zest, and serve with lightly whipped cream.
