About this wine

19 Crimes takes its name from a chapter of British history: people found guilty of any of nineteen crimes could be sent to Australia instead of facing the death penalty, and many of them went on to become colonists and pioneers. Each label shows a portrait of one of these convicts, and with the brand's augmented reality app the figure on the bottle tells you who they were. The Red Blend is an Australian red in a round, generous style, with ripe dark fruit, vanilla from oak and a soft, smooth finish.

On the shelf: Label shows a portrait of a historical convict under the 19 Crimes name; pointing the brand's augmented reality app at it makes the portrait tell its story.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Barbecue pork ribs. Sticky, sweet-smoky glaze matches the wine's jammy fruit and touch of sweetness.
    Recipe idea: Rub ribs with paprika, brown sugar and garlic, bake covered 2 hours at 150 C, then brush with barbecue sauce and grill.
  2. Jerk chicken. A hint of sweetness and ripe fruit help tame the heat of Caribbean jerk spices.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken in scallion, garlic, allspice, thyme, scotch bonnet and lime overnight; grill until charred.
  3. Karni stoba. Hearty beef stew suits a bold red with soft tannins and dark chocolate notes.
    Recipe idea: Sear beef chunks, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, cloves and stock; simmer low for two hours.
  4. Cheeseburger. Juicy beef and melted cheese love a smooth, fruity red with soft tannins.
    Recipe idea: Grill beef patties, top with cheddar to melt, serve in toasted buns with onion and pickles.
  5. Chili con carne. Gentle spice and beans go well with a ripe, jammy red with a little sweetness.
    Recipe idea: Brown ground beef with onion and garlic, add chili powder, cumin, tomatoes and kidney beans; simmer 45 minutes.
