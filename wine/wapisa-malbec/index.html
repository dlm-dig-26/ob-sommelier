About this wine

Wapisa is the Patagonian project of Fincas Patagónicas, the family company also behind Bodega Tapiz in Mendoza. Its Los Acantilados estate lies in San Javier, in the Río Negro province, close to the Atlantic coast, and the winery credits sea breezes and the nearby ocean for the character of its wines. This Malbec is made from 100% Malbec and aged in oak, which adds spice and a hint of chocolate to dark plum and blackberry fruit. In the glass it is bright red with violet tints, with ripe tannins and balanced acidity.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Dark plum fruit and ripe tannins match slow-cooked goat and its warm spices without overpowering them.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a splash of vinegar until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. A medium-bodied red with fresh acidity balances a rich, savoury beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown cubed beef, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, capers and olives, cover with stock and simmer until the meat is soft.
  3. Grilled steak with chimichurri. Classic Argentine match: the tannins tame the char and the acidity cuts the fat.
    Recipe idea: Grill a seasoned steak to taste and serve with a sauce of chopped parsley, garlic, oregano, chili flakes, vinegar and olive oil.
  4. Mushroom lasagna. Earthy mushrooms and tomato sauce bring out the spice and dark fruit from oak ageing.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with sautéed mushrooms, tomato sauce, ricotta and mozzarella and bake until bubbling.
  5. Beef burgers. Juicy fruit and moderate tannins suit grilled beef and a smoky topping.
    Recipe idea: Shape seasoned ground beef into patties, grill and serve in buns with cheese, grilled onions and tomato.
