About this wine

Ménage à Trois is a California brand from Trinchero Family Estates, a Napa family wine company since 1948. Midnight is the darkest wine in the range: a blend of Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon, Petite Sirah and Petit Verdot, aged in French and American oak. It pours dark garnet and shows blackberry, spiced plum and black cherry with mocha and spice, a silky texture and a long finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Spiced plum and mocha match the deep, warm spices of goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat in lime, garlic and pepper, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, cumin and a little Scotch bonnet for 2 to 3 hours.
  2. Carni stoba. A dark, plush red with enough body for rich, long-simmered beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  3. Barbecue ribs. Silky texture and ripe fruit match sticky, smoky pork.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, brown sugar and salt, bake wrapped in foil at low heat for 2 hours, then glaze with barbecue sauce on the grill.
  4. Grilled steak. Blackberry and oak spice flatter a well-charred steak.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt, pepper and garlic, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  5. Chocolate brownies. Mocha notes and dark fruit meet dark chocolate in a not-too-sweet brownie.
    Recipe idea: Melt dark chocolate with butter, stir in sugar, eggs and a little flour, and bake at 175°C for about 25 minutes until just set.
