About this wine

La Violeta is a Malbec from Bodega Monteviejo, part of its Lindaflor range, and is presented as the Argentine counterpart of Château La Violette in Pomerol, Bordeaux; winemaker Marcelo Pelleriti works at both. The grapes come from the Vista Flores vineyard in Tunuyán, Valle de Uco, at about 1,100 metres. The bunches are destemmed by hand and the grapes are cold-soaked for about 20 days in French oak barrels. For the 2013 vintage, the wine then aged 24 to 36 months in new French oak and rested several years in bottle before release. Expect plum, blackberry, violet and earthy notes.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Concentrated dark fruit and firm tannins hold their own against slow-cooked, spiced goat.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little Madame Jeanette pepper, then simmer covered for about 2 hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. The depth and earthy notes of the wine match a rich beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste, cumin and stock, simmer for about 1.5 hours and serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled beef tenderloin with chimichurri. A classic Argentine match: the tannins soften with the meat and the herbs echo the herbal notes.
    Recipe idea: Grill the tenderloin over high heat to medium rare, rest it, slice and serve with a chimichurri of parsley, garlic, oregano, vinegar and olive oil.
  4. Rack of lamb with rosemary. Violet and dark berry notes suit the rich flavour of lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub the lamb with garlic, rosemary, salt and olive oil, sear it, then roast at 200°C for 15 to 20 minutes and rest before slicing.
  5. Aged Gouda. Firm tannins soften against the salty, nutty flavour of a well-aged cheese.
    Recipe idea: Serve thin slices of aged Gouda at room temperature with walnuts, dried figs and crackers.
