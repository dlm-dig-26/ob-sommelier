About this wine

Frontera Merlot is made by Concha y Toro, the Chilean wine house founded in 1883 by Don Melchor Concha y Toro, with its home in Pirque, near Santiago. The grapes come from Chile's Central Valley, grown on alluvial and colluvial soils, and part of the harvest is picked by hand between late March and April. The wine is then aged for four months in stainless steel tanks to keep it fresh and smooth. In the glass it is ruby red, with cherry, a touch of bell pepper and a hint of cocoa, and a round, medium-bodied feel.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Soft, round Merlot with cherry fruit suits the mild cheese and chicken filling without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or slices of Gouda with stewed chicken, olives, raisins, capers and tomato, then bake until the cheese melts.
  2. Galinja stoba. Gentle tannins and medium body match a homey chicken stew with tomato and peppers.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then simmer with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a little soy sauce until tender. Serve with rice.
  3. Pastechi. A smooth, fruity red is an easy match for savory pastry filled with spiced meat or cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with seasoned ground beef or Gouda, fold into half-moons, seal the edges and fry until golden.
  4. Spaghetti bolognese. Fresh acidity and cherry fruit work well with a tomato and meat sauce.
    Recipe idea: Cook ground beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and simmer 30 minutes. Toss with spaghetti and grated cheese.
  5. Grilled chicken. Medium body and soft tannins let the smoky grilled chicken shine.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in garlic, lime, oregano and olive oil for an hour, then grill until cooked through and lightly charred.
