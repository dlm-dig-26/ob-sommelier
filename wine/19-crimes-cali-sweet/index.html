About this wine

Cali Sweet is part of the 19 Crimes range made with Snoop Dogg, who is named as the brand partner on this wine. Unlike the Australian 19 Crimes reds, it comes from California. It is a non-vintage sweet red blend at 12.5% alcohol with a screw cap. Expect jammy dark raspberry and dark fruit, warm spice, vanilla and floral notes, a medium body and a long, juicy finish.

On the shelf: Colourful label with bold text and the 19 Crimes emblem.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Barbecue chicken. Sweet, jammy fruit matches sticky barbecue sauce and softens its heat.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in barbecue sauce with garlic and a little hot pepper, grill until charred and cooked through, brushing with more sauce.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. A sweet red tames the Scotch bonnet heat in a spicy goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat in lime, garlic and pepper, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, cumin and a little Scotch bonnet for 2 to 3 hours.
  3. Pastechi. Soft tannins and sweet fruit play well against salty, spiced fillings.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with spiced ground beef, cheese or tuna, fold into half moons, seal the edges and deep-fry until golden.
  4. Chocolate brownies. A sweet wine with a chocolate dessert: raspberry and vanilla meet cocoa.
    Recipe idea: Melt dark chocolate with butter, stir in sugar, eggs and a little flour, and bake at 175°C for about 25 minutes until just set.
  5. Pepperoni pizza. Juicy sweetness is a fun contrast to spicy pepperoni and tomato.
    Recipe idea: Spread pizza dough with tomato sauce, top with mozzarella and pepperoni, and bake in a very hot oven for 10 to 12 minutes.
