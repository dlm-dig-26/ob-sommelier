About this wine

Portillo is a range from Bodegas Salentein, which established its estate in the Uco Valley in 1996. Its vineyards lie between roughly 1,050 and 1,700 metres above sea level, on alluvial, sandy soils with drip irrigation. Senior winemaker Carlos Bauzá leads the team under the guidance of José Galante, and the wines are made in a three-level gravity-flow winery, so grapes and wine move without pumping. The whole Portillo range is vegan friendly.

On the shelf: The front label carries food pairing suggestions.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di karni. Juicy dark fruit and medium tannins stand up to slow-cooked beef with peppers.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a little wine and simmer covered for two hours.
  2. Pastechi. Spiced beef filling and fried pastry suit a smooth, medium-bodied red.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with ground beef cooked with onion, sweet pepper and raisins, fold, seal and fry until golden.
  3. Grilled steak. Black pepper notes and firm tannins love a charred steak.
    Recipe idea: Season a beef steak with salt and cracked pepper, grill 4 minutes per side over high heat and rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms meet the wine's spice and red fruit, a good vegetarian match.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio rice in butter with onion, add stock little by little while stirring, then fold in sautéed mushrooms and parmesan.
  5. Aged gouda. Nutty, salty cheese softens the tannins and brings out the fruit.
    Recipe idea: Cut aged gouda into chunks and serve with crackers, walnuts and a few slices of apple.
