About this wine

Cavit is a wine company from Trentino, in the mountains of northern Italy, with more than half a century of winemaking behind it. Its Select Red Blend brings together Teroldego, the local red grape of Trentino, with Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah. Because each variety ripens at a different time, each is picked and made into wine separately before the final blend. The vineyards cool down in the evening and are fanned by the Ora del Garda, a dry breeze from Lake Garda, which helps the grapes build aroma. The wine then matures in oak for about a year.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Velvety tannins and plum fruit suit slow cooked beef with tomato and spices.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a splash of wine and simmer covered for two hours until tender.
  2. Pastechi. A smooth, medium bodied red balances the fried pastry and its savoury meat filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with ground beef cooked with onion, sweet pepper and raisins, fold, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  3. Pizza with salami. Tomato, cheese and spicy salami call for a dry Italian red with ripe fruit.
    Recipe idea: Spread pizza dough with tomato sauce, add mozzarella and salami slices, sprinkle with oregano and bake at 240 C for 10 to 12 minutes.
  4. Lasagna. Layers of meat sauce and cheese match the blackberry fruit and soft structure.
    Recipe idea: Layer lasagna sheets with beef and tomato sauce, bechamel and parmesan, repeat three times and bake at 180 C for 40 minutes.
  5. Grilled sausages. Juicy, herby sausages go well with a dry red that has a touch of spice.
    Recipe idea: Grill Italian style sausages over medium heat for about 15 minutes, turning often, and serve with grilled peppers and onions.
