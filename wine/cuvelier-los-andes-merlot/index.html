About this wine

Cuvelier Los Andes belongs to the Cuvelier family from France, part-owners of Château Léoville-Poyferré in Saint-Julien, Bordeaux. The family joined Michel Rolland's Clos de los Siete project in Mendoza in the 1990s, planted its own vines in 1999 and made its first wine in 2003. Their vineyard lies inside Clos de los Siete in Vista Flores, Tunuyán, in the Uco Valley, with Michel Rolland as consulting winemaker. The house style is often described as Argentine fruit made in a French way.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. A medium-bodied Merlot with some tannin suits slow-cooked beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Galinja stoba. Soft plum fruit goes with chicken in a savoury tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a pinch of cumin, then simmer until tender and saucy. Serve with white rice.
  3. Mushroom risotto. Merlot's round fruit matches earthy mushrooms and parmesan.
    Recipe idea: Sauté onion and mushrooms, toast arborio rice, add stock a ladle at a time until creamy, then stir in butter and parmesan.
  4. Roast pork loin. Medium body and soft structure suit juicy roast pork.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork loin with garlic, thyme, salt and pepper, roast at 190C for about an hour and rest before slicing.
  5. Lasagne. Ripe fruit and moderate tannin handle meat sauce and cheese.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with beef ragu, béchamel and mozzarella, finish with parmesan and bake at 180C for 40 minutes.
