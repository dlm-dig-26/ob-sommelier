About this wine

Ice Impérial Rosé is made by Moët & Chandon in Champagne, France, and the house presents it as the first rosé Champagne created to be enjoyed over ice. It followed the white Ice Impérial, launched in 2011. The blend is led by Pinot Noir and Meunier, each including a share of red wine for colour, with a little Chardonnay and 20 to 30 percent reserve wines. A generous dosage gives it a demi-sec sweetness that stays balanced when the wine is served cold over ice. The house pours it into a large glass with three ice cubes and suggests an optional garnish such as mint, citrus zest or red berries.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Shrimp creole. Red fruit and gentle sweetness soften the pepper heat of a tomato creole sauce.
    Recipe idea: Sauté onion, sweet pepper and garlic, add chopped tomatoes, thyme and a little hot pepper, simmer 15 minutes, then cook shrimp in the sauce for 3 minutes.
  2. Pastechi. Gentle sweetness and red berry fruit contrast nicely with crisp, salty cheese pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with grated Gouda, fold into half moons, crimp the edges and deep fry until golden.
  3. Grilled red snapper with mango salsa. Fruity sweetness and the grapefruit finish match a sweet and spicy salsa on grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Grill a whole snapper rubbed with lime, garlic and salt. Top with diced mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice and a little hot pepper.
  4. Spicy salmon poke bowl. Sweetness and cherry fruit calm the chili and suit rich raw salmon.
    Recipe idea: Cube raw salmon and toss with soy sauce, sesame oil and sriracha mayo. Serve over rice with cucumber, avocado and mango.
  5. Strawberry and nectarine salad. Cherry and nectarine notes echo ripe fruit that is not too sugary, keeping the pairing fresh.
    Recipe idea: Toss sliced strawberries and nectarines with a squeeze of lime and torn mint leaves. Serve chilled with a spoon of yoghurt.
