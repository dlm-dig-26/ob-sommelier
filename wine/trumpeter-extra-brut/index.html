About this wine

Trumpeter is a range from Rutini Wines, whose winery stands in Tupungato in the Uco Valley, with winemaker Mariano Di Paola. This Extra Brut is made with the traditional method, with the second fermentation in the bottle, from 100 percent Chardonnay. A competition listing for the 2022 vintage places the grapes in Gualtallary, Tupungato. It is pale yellow with fine, steady bubbles, white fruit and light floral notes, a creamy mousse, fresh acidity and a dry finish.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Dry bubbles and crisp acidity refresh lime-cured fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, chili, cilantro and salt.
  2. Pastechi. Bubbles cut through fried pastry and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry with spiced minced beef or Gouda cheese, fold into half-moons, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  3. Cala. A dry sparkling wine is a natural partner for crisp, salty fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, rub off the skins, blend with onion, chili and salt into a thick batter and fry spoonfuls until golden.
  4. Fried calamari. Green apple and lemon notes act like a squeeze of citrus.
    Recipe idea: Cut squid into rings, toss in flour seasoned with salt and paprika and fry at 180°C for 2 minutes. Serve with lemon.
  5. Garlic shrimp. A creamy mousse and freshness balance garlic and butter.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp in butter and olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 3 minutes. Serve with bread or rice.
