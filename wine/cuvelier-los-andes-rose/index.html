About this wine

Cuvelier Los Andes belongs to the Cuvelier family from France, part-owners of Château Léoville-Poyferré in Saint-Julien, Bordeaux. The family joined Michel Rolland's Clos de los Siete project in Mendoza, planted its own vines in 1999 and made its first wine in 2003. Their vineyard lies inside Clos de los Siete in Vista Flores, Tunuyán, high in the Uco Valley, with Michel Rolland as consulting winemaker. The house style is often described as Argentine fruit made in a French way.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. A dry, crisp rosé with high acidity loves lime and fresh fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice and leave 15 minutes, then mix in red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt. Serve cold.
  2. Grilled red snapper. Light body and red fruit suit grilled whole fish.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole red snapper with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, grill over medium heat for about 8 minutes per side and finish with a squeeze of lime.
  3. Pisca hasa. Fresh acidity cuts through crisp fried fish.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets with lime, garlic and salt, dust lightly with flour and pan-fry until crisp. Serve with funchi or pan bati and a fresh creole sauce.
  4. Grilled chicken salad. A light Malbec rosé matches grilled chicken and a lemony dressing.
    Recipe idea: Grill seasoned chicken breast, slice over mixed greens, tomato, cucumber and avocado and dress with lemon and olive oil.
  5. Shrimp tacos. Crisp red berry fruit balances lime, slaw and mild spice.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp with chili and garlic, serve in warm tortillas with shredded cabbage, lime, cilantro and a little sour cream.
