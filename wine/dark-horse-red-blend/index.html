About this wine

Dark Horse is a California wine brand from E. & J. Gallo, the family wine company based in Modesto. The range went into national distribution in the United States in 2015 with a simple aim: wine that tastes like a pricier bottle at an everyday price. Winemaker Beth Liston leads the team. For this Red Blend the grapes are picked at full ripeness and fermented warm to bring out intense flavour and a velvety feel, then the wine is aged for balance. Expect red cherry and raspberry over toasted oak and mocha, with cassis on the finish.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Juicy dark fruit and soft tannins stand up to slow-cooked goat and its warm spices.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a splash of vinegar, then simmer covered for about two hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. Mocha and toasted oak echo the deep, savoury gravy of this beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Sear beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste, a little soy sauce and broth, and simmer slowly until soft. Serve with rice.
  3. Barbecue ribs. A touch of ripe fruit sweetness matches smoky, sticky barbecue sauce.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, garlic and brown sugar, bake low and covered, then brush with barbecue sauce and finish on the grill.
  4. Cheeseburgers. Bold fruit and medium tannin cut through grilled beef and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Season ground beef with salt and pepper, grill the patties, top with cheddar and serve in toasted buns with onion and tomato.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. Bright cherry fruit lifts a rich tomato and meat sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown minced beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add crushed tomatoes, simmer for 45 minutes and toss with spaghetti and parmesan.
