About this wine

Lamole is a hamlet of Greve in Chianti, high in the hills of Chianti Classico, with vineyards between 470 and 655 metres on old terraces built from local sandstone. Wine has been made here for centuries: in 1350 the Gherardini family of Florence built a castle to protect the valley, and its old cellars now house the winery. Lareale is the estate's Riserva, made from about 95 percent Sangiovese with a little Canaiolo Nero from certified organic vineyards, and aged for around two years in oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Firm tannins and fresh acidity cut through rich, spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and pepper; simmer slowly until the meat falls apart.
  2. Karni stoba. Slow-cooked beef softens the wine's firm tannins and brings out its red fruit.
    Recipe idea: Sear beef chunks, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste and stock; stew on low heat for two hours.
  3. Pasta with beef ragù. Sangiovese and a tomato meat sauce are a classic Tuscan match.
    Recipe idea: Simmer ground beef with onion, carrot, celery, red wine and tomato for two hours; toss with pappardelle.
  4. Grilled T-bone steak. A big, charred steak stands up to a structured, tannic Riserva.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick T-bone with salt and rosemary, grill hot 5 minutes per side, rest, slice and add olive oil.
  5. Mushroom pizza. Earthy mushrooms echo the wine's forest-floor and resin notes.
    Recipe idea: Top pizza dough with tomato, mozzarella, sliced mushrooms and thyme; bake very hot until crisp.
