About this wine

Trumpeter is the everyday line of Rutini Wines, the Mendoza house that Italian-born Felipe Rutini founded in 1885 as Bodega La Rural. Head winemaker Mariano Di Paola makes this Cabernet Sauvignon from Tupungato fruit in the Uco Valley. In past vintages the wine went through full malolactic fermentation and spent about nine months in a mix of second-use French oak and new American oak. Expect a deep ruby colour, blackberry and red currant, soft tannins and a hint of vanilla and cocoa.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Spiced goat stew meets the wine's blackcurrant fruit and firm but rounded tannins.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces with onion and garlic, add tomato, cumin, a little hot pepper and stock, and simmer for about two hours.
  2. Carni stoba. Savory stewed beef softens the tannins and brings out the dark berry fruit.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste and stock, simmer until tender and serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Grilled steak. Grilled beef and Cabernet are a classic match; the fat smooths the tannins.
    Recipe idea: Season the steak with salt and black pepper, grill over high heat to taste and rest five minutes before slicing.
  4. Duck breast with cherry sauce. Cherry sauce echoes the red currant notes, and rich duck suits the wine's structure.
    Recipe idea: Score and sear the duck skin side down until crisp, finish in the oven, and simmer cherries with stock and a little honey for the sauce.
  5. Meat lasagna. Tomato, beef and cheese match a medium-bodied red with oak spice.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with beef ragù, béchamel and grated cheese, then bake for about 40 minutes until golden.
