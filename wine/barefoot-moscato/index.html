About this wine

Barefoot Moscato is made by Barefoot Cellars in Modesto, California. A New Zealand retailer lists it as a blend: about 58% Moscato, 25% Symphony, with smaller amounts of Riesling, French Colombard and other grapes, from vineyards in the Central Valley near the Sacramento Delta. The grapes are picked at night and fermented with aromatic yeast to keep the fruit bright. The result is a sweet, light white of about 9% alcohol, with peach, apricot, tangerine and orange blossom notes. Serve it well chilled.

What to eat with it

  1. Fruit salad. Peach and citrus in the wine echo fresh tropical fruit, and the sweetness matches.
    Recipe idea: Mix papaya, mango, pineapple and melon with a squeeze of lime and a few mint leaves; chill before serving.
  2. Coconut flan. A sweet wine should be at least as sweet as the dessert, and the honey notes love coconut.
    Recipe idea: Blend eggs, condensed milk, coconut milk and grated coconut, pour over caramel in a mould and bake in a water bath.
  3. Spicy shrimp creole. Sweetness and low alcohol calm the heat of a peppery Caribbean sauce.
    Recipe idea: Sauté onion, garlic, celery and sweet pepper, add tomato and hot pepper, then simmer shrimp in the sauce for a few minutes.
  4. Jerk chicken. Fruity sweetness cools down the Scotch bonnet heat and smoky spice.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken in a paste of Scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme, garlic, lime and soy overnight, then grill slowly.
  5. Peach cobbler. The wine's juicy peach and apricot meet the baked fruit head on.
    Recipe idea: Toss sliced peaches with sugar and cinnamon, top with a simple butter batter and bake until golden and bubbling.
