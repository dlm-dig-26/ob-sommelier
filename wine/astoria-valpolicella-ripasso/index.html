About this wine

Astoria is a family wine house led by Giorgio and Paolo Polegato, founded in 1987 at Crocetta del Montello in the Treviso hills and best known for Prosecco. Its Valpolicella Ripasso comes from Veneto and is built on Corvina with Corvinone and Rondinella, picked by hand. After a first fermentation the young wine is passed over the pressed skins left from Amarone for around ten days, which adds depth and a second fermentation. It then spends about a year in oak before further rest in bottle.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. The ripe cherry and spice of the Ripasso stand up to slow cooked goat with cumin and pepper without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin, a little hot pepper and stock, then simmer covered for about 2 hours until tender.
  2. Keshi yena. Melted aged cheese and a savoury meat filling love this wine's body and dry, structured finish.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Gouda or ramekins lined with cheese slices with spiced chicken, olives, raisins and capers, then bake until bubbling.
  3. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms and parmesan echo the wine's dried fruit depth while its acidity cuts the creaminess.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio in butter, add sliced mushrooms, then ladle in hot stock while stirring for 18 minutes; finish with parmesan.
  4. Aged cheese and cured meats board. Firm, salty cheeses and salami match the wine's ripe fruit and medium tannins.
    Recipe idea: Arrange aged Gouda or parmesan, salami and prosciutto with olives, crusty bread and a few dried figs.
  5. Beef ragù with pappardelle. A rich tomato and beef sauce matches the Ripasso's weight and dark cherry fruit.
    Recipe idea: Brown minced beef with onion, carrot and celery, add tomato passata and a splash of red wine, simmer 1.5 hours, toss with pasta.
