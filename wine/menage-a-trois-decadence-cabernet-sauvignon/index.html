About this wine

Ménage à Trois began in 1996 at Folie à Deux Winery in St. Helena, California, when two psychiatrists created a red blend of three grapes. The brand is now part of Trinchero Family Estates, a family-owned Napa Valley wine company active since 1948. Decadence was added in September 2017 as a rich, full-bodied California Cabernet Sauvignon. An early release was made mostly from Cabernet Sauvignon with small amounts of Petite Sirah and Merlot, giving dark berry fruit, a soft, round texture and a smooth, chocolate-tinged finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Dark fruit and firm but smooth tannin stand up to the rich, spiced flavors of slow-cooked goat.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. A full-bodied Cabernet matches the depth of beef stewed for hours in a savory tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, sweet pepper, a splash of soy and Worcestershire, then simmer 90 minutes.
  3. Grilled ribeye steak. Tannin and blackcurrant fruit cut through the fat of a well-marbled steak and love the charred crust.
    Recipe idea: Bring the steak to room temperature, season with salt and pepper, grill 4 to 5 minutes per side, then rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Smoked beef brisket. Vanilla spice and chocolate notes from the wine pick up the smoky bark of slow-cooked brisket.
    Recipe idea: Rub brisket with salt, pepper, paprika and garlic, cook low at 120 C for 8 to 10 hours, wrapped in foil after the first 4 hours.
  5. Aged Gouda cheese board. Firm, nutty aged cheese softens the tannin and brings out the wine's ripe dark fruit.
    Recipe idea: Slice aged Gouda and sharp cheddar, add walnuts, dried figs and crackers, and let the cheese warm to room temperature before serving.
