About this wine

Gallo Family Vineyards is a brand of E. and J. Gallo Winery, founded in 1933 in Modesto, California, by brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo and still family owned. This Merlot is made from grapes grown in the Central Valley of California, at the foot of the Sierra Nevada, where warm days, cooler nights and a long season let the fruit ripen slowly. It is smooth and soft, with black cherry, plum and raspberry, velvety tannins and hints of vanilla and spice.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Soft and fruity, it suits the savory chicken filling and melted Gouda.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Gouda or a cheese-lined dish with stewed chicken, olives, raisins and capers, then bake until the cheese melts.
  2. Galinja stoba. Gentle tannins and ripe plum match a tomato-based chicken stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and stock, and simmer until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled burgers. Easygoing cherry fruit is a natural match for grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season with salt and pepper, grill 4 minutes per side and serve in toasted buns.
  4. Roast pork. Soft red fruit and velvety tannins suit tender, juicy pork.
    Recipe idea: Rub a pork loin with garlic, thyme, salt and pepper, roast at 190 C until cooked through and rest before slicing.
  5. Meatloaf. A smooth, fruity red for a classic comfort dish.
    Recipe idea: Mix ground beef with egg, breadcrumbs, onion and herbs, shape into a loaf, glaze with ketchup and bake for about an hour.
