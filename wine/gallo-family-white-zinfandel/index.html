About this wine

This White Zinfandel comes from Gallo Family Vineyards, a label of E. & J. Gallo Winery, founded in 1933 in Modesto, California, by brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo and still family owned. Despite the name, it is a pink wine: a semi-sweet California rosé made from red Zinfandel grapes. Expect candied strawberry, cherry blossom and ripe apple, a touch of sweet citrus and a crisp, refreshing finish. It is light and easy, best served well chilled, and is sold in 750 ml and 1.5 liter bottles.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi. Gentle sweetness and red fruit balance the salty, savory filling of a warm pastechi.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with seasoned ground beef or cheese, fold into half moons, seal the edges and fry until golden.
  2. Galinja stoba. Soft red fruit and a light body suit a mildly spiced chicken stew without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, sweet pepper, garlic, tomato, capers and olives, then simmer with a little broth until tender.
  3. Spicy shrimp in Creole sauce. A touch of sweetness cools the chili heat, and the strawberry fruit suits the tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Saute onion, sweet pepper and garlic, add tomato, a little Madame Jeanette pepper and thyme, simmer, then cook shrimp in the sauce for 3 minutes.
  4. Fresh fruit salad. Strawberry and melon notes echo the fruit, and the wine is sweet enough to match it.
    Recipe idea: Combine papaya, mango, pineapple, strawberries and melon; add a squeeze of lime and a few mint leaves and chill before serving.
  5. Strawberry shortcake. Candied strawberry flavors mirror the berries, and the soft sweetness keeps the pairing light.
    Recipe idea: Split warm biscuits, fill with sliced strawberries tossed in a little sugar and top with whipped cream.
