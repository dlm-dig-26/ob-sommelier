About this wine

Santa Carolina is one of the oldest wineries in Chile. Luis Pereira founded it in 1875, named it after his wife, and hired the French winemaker Germain Bachelet to make fine wines. Today it belongs to Carolina Wine Brands, part of the Watt's group owned by the Larrain family. The Cellar Selection Cabernet Sauvignon is an everyday Chilean red. Oak ageing adds hints of vanilla and spice to its dark fruit.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Stewed chicken in tomato sauce suits a medium-bodied red with soft tannins and juicy cherry fruit.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a little vinegar; simmer 40 minutes.
  2. Pastechi di karni. The spiced beef filling and crisp pastry match the wine's easy plum fruit and light spice.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with spiced ground beef, raisins and olives; fold, seal and deep-fry until golden.
  3. Pasta bolognese. Tomato and slow-cooked meat sauce love the wine's red cherry fruit and fresh acidity.
    Recipe idea: Cook ground beef with onion, carrot, celery and crushed tomatoes for an hour; toss with spaghetti and Parmesan.
  4. Grilled chicken thighs. Smoky, juicy chicken is a good fit for a fruity, medium-weight Cabernet.
    Recipe idea: Marinate thighs in garlic, paprika, lime and oil for an hour, then grill 6 to 7 minutes per side.
  5. Pepperoni pizza. Salty, spicy pepperoni and cheese are balanced by soft tannins and ripe fruit.
    Recipe idea: Top pizza dough with tomato sauce, mozzarella and pepperoni; bake at 250 C for 10 to 12 minutes.
