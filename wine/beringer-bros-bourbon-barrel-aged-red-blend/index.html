About this wine

Beringer was founded in 1876 by brothers Jacob and Frederick Beringer in St. Helena, Napa Valley, and is now part of Treasury Wine Estates. The Beringer Bros range is made by fifth-generation winemaker Mark Beringer. This red blend is led by Petite Sirah and Syrah, with Petit Verdot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot. After fermentation in stainless steel, part of the wine spends 60 days in used, charred American oak barrels that once held bourbon. That gives notes of caramel, smoke, vanilla and toasted coconut over blackberry and plum.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Barbecue ribs. Smoke and caramel from the bourbon barrels mirror a sticky, smoky glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, brown sugar and salt, bake wrapped in foil at low heat for 2 hours, then glaze with barbecue sauce on the grill.
  2. Carni stoba. Ripe dark fruit and ripe tannins carry a rich, long-cooked beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  3. Stobá di kabritu. A bold, spicy red with enough weight for goat stewed with cumin and pepper.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat in lime, garlic and pepper, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, cumin and a little Scotch bonnet for 2 to 3 hours.
  4. Grilled steak. Vanilla and toasted notes suit the charred crust of grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt, pepper and garlic, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  5. Cheeseburger. Creamy texture and dark fruit stand up to cheese, bacon or a smoky sauce.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season with salt and pepper, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and top with cheddar, onion and tomato.
