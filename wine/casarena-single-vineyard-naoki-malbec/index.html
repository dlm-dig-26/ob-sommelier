About this wine

Bodega Casarena is based in Perdriel, Luján de Cuyo, in a restored winery building from 1937, and works with four vineyards named Jamilla, Naoki, Owen and Lauren. Naoki is a single-vineyard Malbec from Agrelo, grown at about 950 metres on sand and limestone soils. The grapes are hand-picked, given a long maceration, and the wine spends 12 months in French oak. Casarena's wines are vegan and certified sustainable.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Plum fruit and polished tannins match a rich, tender beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Dark fruit and spice from oak stand up to goat and cumin.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Grilled tenderloin. Elegant Malbec with fine tannins flatters lean, tender beef.
    Recipe idea: Season beef tenderloin with salt and pepper, sear on all sides, roast at 200C to medium-rare and rest before slicing.
  4. Lamb chops with rosemary. Violet and plum notes suit herby lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, olive oil and salt, grill 3 minutes a side and rest briefly before serving.
  5. Aged gouda. A structured, mineral red pairs well with nutty aged cheese.
    Recipe idea: Serve slices of aged gouda with walnuts, dried fruit and crackers at room temperature.
