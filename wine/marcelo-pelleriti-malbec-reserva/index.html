About this wine

Marcelo Pelleriti is an Argentine winemaker with more than 30 years of experience in Argentina and France, and Michel Rolland was his mentor. He made his first wine in the Uco Valley in 1994 and led the Monteviejo winery, part of the Clos de los Siete project. He has also made wine at Bordeaux estates, including in Pomerol. This Malbec comes from Uco Valley vineyards at around 1,000 metres, watered with snowmelt and split into small plots of one to three hectares. The grapes are picked by hand.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Slow-cooked goat with spice stands up to the full body and firm tannins.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic and curry, brown it, add tomato, onion and sweet pepper and simmer slowly until soft.
  2. Carni stoba. Rich beef stew softens the tannins and lets the dark fruit shine.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, then simmer covered for two hours until tender.
  3. Grilled ribeye with chimichurri. Char and fat from the grill are the classic partner for Uco Valley Malbec.
    Recipe idea: Salt a ribeye, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and spoon over chimichurri made with parsley, garlic, oregano, vinegar and oil.
  4. Braised short ribs. Tender, fatty beef matches the weight of the wine and its ripe fruit.
    Recipe idea: Brown short ribs, add onion, carrot, garlic and a cup of red wine, cover and cook in the oven at 160 C for three hours.
  5. Aged cheese board. Firm, salty aged cheeses balance the tannins and bring out the fruit.
    Recipe idea: Serve aged gouda, manchego and parmesan with walnuts, dried figs and crusty bread.
