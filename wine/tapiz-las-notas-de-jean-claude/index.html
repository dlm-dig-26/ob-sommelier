About this wine

Bodega Tapiz is a winery in San Pablo, in the Uco Valley of Mendoza, and part of Fincas Patagónicas; Patricia Ortiz is its CEO and Fabián Valenzuela its winemaker. The Las Notas de Jean Claude line is overseen by Jean Claude Berrouet, a consultant formerly of Château Pétrus. This wine is a Bordeaux-style blend led by Merlot: the 2020 vintage is about three quarters Merlot, with Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot and a little Malbec, all from the San Pablo estate. It is aged 11 months in French oak, partly new, and shows red fruit, cassis, plum, spice and tobacco with fine tannins and a long finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Plum, spice and tobacco suit the deep, slow-cooked flavour of goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. A structured, oak-aged red matches the weight of a rich beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Simmer beef cubes with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, capers and olives for about 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Juicy, charred beef shows off the cassis fruit and fine tannins.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Rosemary lamb chops. Lamb and herbs are a classic match for a Merlot-based Bordeaux-style blend.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary and olive oil, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and serve with roast potatoes.
  5. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms bring out the savoury, tobacco side of the wine.
    Recipe idea: Fry mushrooms and onion, add arborio rice, then ladle in hot stock while stirring for 18 minutes and finish with parmesan.
