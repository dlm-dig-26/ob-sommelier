About this wine

Chandon Rosé is made by Chandon in Mendoza, Argentina, from vineyards planted high in the foothills of the Andes, between 1,000 and 1,200 metres. The blend is mostly Chardonnay with Pinot Noir and a small splash of Malbec. The winery uses a long Charmat method: base wines are fermented in steel, blended with reserve wines from earlier years, then given a second fermentation in sealed tanks and left on the yeast for a few months to build a creamy texture.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Fine bubbles and bright red fruit lift the lime and cool down the hot pepper.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice and leave 15 minutes, then mix in red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt. Serve cold.
  2. Pastechi. The fizz cuts through fried pastry and the soft fruit suits a cheese filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill small pastry half-moons with seasoned cheese, tuna or ground beef, seal the edges with a fork and fry or bake until golden.
  3. Grilled red snapper. A light, fruity sparkler stays fresh next to delicate grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole red snapper with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, grill over medium heat for about 8 minutes per side and finish with a squeeze of lime.
  4. Salmon sushi. Strawberry notes and a creamy mousse sit well with rich salmon and rice.
    Recipe idea: Press seasoned sushi rice onto nori, add slices of fresh salmon and avocado, roll tightly and slice. Serve with a little soy sauce.
  5. Shrimp pasta. A touch of fruity sweetness and fresh acidity balance garlicky shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp with garlic, chilli flakes and olive oil, add cherry tomatoes and a splash of the wine, then toss with spaghetti and parsley.
