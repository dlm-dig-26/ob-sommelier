About this wine

Gallo Family Vineyards Pink Moscato is a sweet, low-alcohol rosé of about 9% made from Muscat grapes grown in select vineyards of California's Central Valley, near the mouth of the Sacramento Delta. Winemaker Cal Dennison shapes it as an aromatic, easygoing wine with flavours of nectarine and pomegranate on top of the floral Muscat perfume. It is sealed with a screwcap and is made to be served well chilled and enjoyed young rather than cellared. Its pink colour and gentle sweetness make it a natural partner for fruit desserts and spicy snacks.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Spicy shrimp with Madame Jeanette pepper. The sweetness and low alcohol cool the chili heat, while red-fruit flavours lift the sweet shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp with garlic, onion and a little minced Madame Jeanette pepper, finish with lime and cilantro. Serve with white rice.
  2. Pastechi. Fried, savoury pastry with a spicy dip loves a cold, sweet pink wine that refreshes after every bite.
    Recipe idea: Fill dough rounds with seasoned ground beef or cheese, fold, crimp and fry until golden. Serve with a spicy papaya-pepper sauce.
  3. Cashew cake. Moist, sweet cashew cake needs a wine at least as sweet; the berry notes add a fresh contrast.
    Recipe idea: Bake a butter cake with ground cashews and a splash of rum, then dust with powdered sugar or drizzle with a light glaze.
  4. Strawberry shortcake. Raspberry and peach flavours echo the berries, and the light body won't swamp the soft cream.
    Recipe idea: Split warm biscuits, fill with sugared sliced strawberries and whipped cream, and serve right away.
  5. Pad thai with chili. A sweet, fruity rosé tames chili and tamarind tang better than any dry red.
    Recipe idea: Stir-fry rice noodles with egg, shrimp or chicken, tamarind, fish sauce and sugar. Top with peanuts, lime and chili flakes.
