About this wine

Cupcake Vineyards, based in Ripon, California, makes this Pinot Grigio from Italian grapes. The vines grow in the foothills of the Alps in the Veneto, on alluvial and calcareous soils, under the Delle Venezie DOC. The grapes are picked by hand and gently pressed, and the wine then rests for three weeks on its lees in stainless steel, which adds a soft texture without any oak. The result is bright and crisp, with pear, lemon zest and honeysuckle and a citrus finish. Jessica Tomei is the winemaker.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Crisp acidity and lemon notes echo the lime in the fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt.
  2. Pisca hasa. A light, clean white refreshes the palate between bites of fried fish.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets or whole fish with lime, garlic and salt, fry in hot oil until golden and serve with a fresh onion and pepper sauce.
  3. Sopi di pisca. Light body and citrus suit a delicate fish soup without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, garlic, celery, potato, tomato and a little hot pepper in water for 30 minutes, finish with lime juice.
  4. Garlic shrimp. Pear and lemon zest lift the garlic and keep the dish fresh.
    Recipe idea: Sauté peeled shrimp in olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 2 to 3 minutes, finish with lemon juice.
  5. Goat cheese salad. High acidity matches tangy goat cheese and a lemon dressing.
    Recipe idea: Toss mixed greens with a lemon vinaigrette, top with crumbled goat cheese, sliced cucumber and toasted nuts.
