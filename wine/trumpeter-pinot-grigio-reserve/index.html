About this wine

Trumpeter is a range from Rutini Wines, which traces its history to Felipe Rutini, an Italian immigrant who founded the La Rural winery in Mendoza in 1885; his family planted vineyards in the Uco Valley in 1924. The Trumpeter Reserve Pinot Grigio is made entirely from Pinot Grigio grown in Mendoza, and recent vintages carry the Uco Valley appellation. Reviewers describe it as light and easy to drink, with peach, guava and strong citrus and a long, steely finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pisca hasa. Light and crisp, it cuts through the crunch of fried fish with lime.
    Recipe idea: Season snapper fillets with lime, salt and garlic, dust with flour and fry until golden. Serve with lime wedges and a fresh salad.
  2. Ceviche. Bright citrus in the wine echoes the lime in the fish.
    Recipe idea: Dice very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili, cilantro and salt, and chill for 15 minutes before serving.
  3. Sopi di pisca. A fresh white keeps a light fish soup tasting clean and lively.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, garlic, tomato, potato and herbs in fish stock, and finish with a squeeze of lime.
  4. Shrimp salad. Peach and citrus notes suit sweet shrimp and crisp greens.
    Recipe idea: Toss cooked shrimp with lettuce, cucumber, avocado and cherry tomatoes, and dress with olive oil, lemon and a pinch of salt.
  5. Pasta with lemon and garlic. A light wine for a light pasta with fresh citrus.
    Recipe idea: Cook spaghetti, toss with olive oil, sliced garlic warmed in the pan, lemon zest and juice, parsley and grated parmesan.
