About this wine

Devil's Carnaval is a newer, playful line from Viña Concha y Toro, the Chilean house behind Casillero del Diablo. It reworks the famous Casillero devil with carnival masks and bright colours. The range was launched in Chile in late 2023 and has four wines. This white is sold in some markets as Phenomenal Sauvignon. A retailer lists it as 85% Sauvignon Blanc with 15% Moscatel de Alejandría, which adds a floral, fruity lift. It is made to be fresh and light, with citrus and tropical fruit.

On the shelf: Colourful carnival-style label with a masked version of the Casillero del Diablo devil; each wine in the range has its own mask.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Lime and fresh fish meet the wine's crisp citrus acidity head on.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili and cilantro, rest 15 minutes and serve cold.
  2. Pisca hasa. A light, zesty white refreshes the palate after crispy fried fish.
    Recipe idea: Season whole snapper with lime, garlic and salt, dust in flour, fry until golden and serve with onion sauce.
  3. Sushi. Clean, light and fruity, it stays in the background next to rice and raw fish.
    Recipe idea: Roll sushi rice and nori with cucumber, avocado and salmon or tuna; slice and serve with a little soy.
  4. Fish and chips. Bright acidity cuts through the batter and keeps every bite fresh.
    Recipe idea: Dip cod or snapper in a flour and soda water batter, fry until crisp and serve with fries and lemon.
  5. Shrimp and mango salad. The tropical fruit in the wine echoes sweet mango and juicy shrimp.
    Recipe idea: Toss grilled shrimp with mango, lettuce, red onion and a lime and olive oil dressing.
