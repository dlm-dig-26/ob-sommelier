About this wine

Rutini Wines goes back to Felipe Rutini, an Italian from Le Marche who founded Bodega La Rural near Mendoza in 1885. In 1925 the company began planting vineyards in the Uco Valley, at Tupungato, where its modern winery opened in 2008. Apartado means set apart: this Malbec is a selection of the best barrels from top Uco Valley sites such as Tupungato, La Consulta and Altamira, so the blend changes every year. Earlier vintages spent about 18 months in French oak. Expect dark berries, peppery spice and a floral touch, with fine tannins and a spicy finish; it can age for years.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled beef tenderloin. Concentrated dark fruit and fine tannins suit lean, tender beef from the grill.
    Recipe idea: Season a whole tenderloin with salt and pepper, sear on all sides, finish over indirect heat until pink inside and rest before slicing.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. A powerful Malbec with enough spice to match the deep, cumin-scented flavours of goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces with onion, garlic and cumin, add tomato, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper, cover and simmer two hours until tender.
  3. Carni stoba. Ripe plum and cocoa notes stand up to a slow beef stew with olives and capers.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, tomato, peppers, capers and olives, cover with stock and simmer 90 minutes; serve with rice or funchi.
  4. Aged Gouda. Fine tannins and dark fruit balance the salty, nutty bite of old Gouda.
    Recipe idea: Serve thin slices of aged Gouda at room temperature with dried figs, walnuts and crusty bread.
  5. Rosemary lamb chops. Peppery spice and structure love the herbs and richness of grilled lamb.
    Recipe idea: Marinate lamb chops in olive oil, garlic and rosemary for an hour, then grill 3 minutes per side.
