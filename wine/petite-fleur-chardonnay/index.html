About this wine

Bodega Monteviejo is the estate founded by Catherine Péré-Vergé, owner of Château Le Gay in Bordeaux, and it was the first winery built in the Clos de los Siete project that Michel Rolland started in the Uco Valley. The estate was created in 1998 and the winery went up in 2001 in Vista Flores, Tunuyán, where the vines are drip-irrigated and picked by hand. Her son Henry Parent now leads the company. Petite Fleur Chardonnay is 100 percent Chardonnay, with part of the wine fermented in second-fill French oak barrels. It is fresh and elegant, with white stone fruit, lively acidity and about 13.5 percent alcohol.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Its light oak and soft fruit sit well with the melted cheese and mild spiced chicken filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or a dish lined with Gouda slices with stewed chicken, olives, raisins and capers. Cover with cheese and bake until bubbling.
  2. Grilled red snapper. Fresh acidity and citrus lift the delicate fish without covering it.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole snapper with lime, garlic, salt and pepper. Grill for about 8 minutes per side and serve with lime and a green salad.
  3. Pastechi. The bright finish cuts through the fried pastry and salty cheese filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with Gouda cheese, fold into half moons, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  4. Roast chicken with herbs. Pear and a hint of vanilla match the golden skin and juicy meat.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, thyme, garlic and lemon. Roast at 200 C for about 75 minutes and rest before carving.
  5. Shrimp pasta in garlic cream sauce. Medium body handles the creamy sauce while the acidity keeps it fresh.
    Recipe idea: Saute shrimp in butter with garlic, add cream and a squeeze of lemon, then toss with linguine and parsley.
