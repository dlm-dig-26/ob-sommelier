About this wine

Yellow Tail is made by Casella Family Brands in Yenda, a small town in New South Wales, Australia. Filippo and Maria Casella emigrated from Sicily in 1957, and for years the family winery sold its wine in bulk to other wineries. In 2000 the family created Yellow Tail so it could sell its own bottled wine, at first mostly for export, and by 2011 it was the top imported wine brand in the United States. The grapes come from the family vineyard in the Riverina and from other growers across South Eastern Australia. The Merlot is made to be soft, fresh and easy to drink.

On the shelf: Look for the orange label, the colour Yellow Tail uses for its Merlot.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Soft tannins and juicy plum fruit suit tender stewed chicken with mild spices.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and a little curry, then simmer covered for 45 minutes until tender.
  2. Pastechi. A smooth, fruity red refreshes fried pastry with a savoury meat or cheese filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with spiced ground beef or gouda, fold, seal with a fork and fry until golden and crisp.
  3. Spaghetti bolognese. Tomato and minced meat love a mellow Merlot with ripe fruit and light oak.
    Recipe idea: Fry onion, carrot and garlic, brown minced beef, add crushed tomatoes and oregano, simmer 40 minutes and serve over spaghetti.
  4. Antipasto platter. Cured meats, cheese and olives go well with an easy red that is not too heavy.
    Recipe idea: Arrange salami, ham, gouda, olives, roasted peppers and crusty bread on a board and drizzle the vegetables with olive oil.
  5. Barbecue chicken. Light smoke and sweet glaze match the soft, fruity style without clashing.
    Recipe idea: Season chicken thighs with paprika, garlic and salt, grill until cooked through and brush with barbecue sauce in the last minutes.
