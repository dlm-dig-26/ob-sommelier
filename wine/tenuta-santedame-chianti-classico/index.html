About this wine

Santedame is one of the estates of the Tuscan wine house Ruffino, in Castellina in Chianti, in the heart of the Chianti Classico area. The vines grow on high hills at 350 to 470 metres, on soils that range from loamy clay to loamy sand with plenty of gravel. The wine is about 80 percent Sangiovese, completed with Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and other varieties. It ferments in temperature-controlled steel tanks and then ages in concrete tanks and small oak barrels. It is ruby red, with violet, cherry, black pepper and tobacco.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Bright acidity cuts through the tomato sauce, and cherry fruit suits the slow-cooked beef.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste, cumin and stock, simmer for about 1.5 hours and serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Pastechi. Fresh acidity and juicy tannins refresh the palate after crisp, savoury pastry filled with meat.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with spiced ground beef, raisins and olives, fold, seal the edges with a fork and fry until golden.
  3. Spaghetti bolognese. A classic Tuscan red with a meat and tomato sauce: the acidity matches the tomato.
    Recipe idea: Brown ground beef with onion, carrot and celery, add tomatoes and a splash of wine, simmer for an hour and toss with spaghetti and parmesan.
  4. Pizza margherita. Fresh acidity and red fruit match tomato, mozzarella and basil.
    Recipe idea: Spread tomato sauce on pizza dough, add mozzarella, bake in a very hot oven for about 10 minutes and finish with fresh basil and olive oil.
  5. Grilled pork chops with rosemary. Medium body and lively tannins suit grilled pork, and the herbs echo the peppery notes.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork chops with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper, grill for 4 to 5 minutes per side and rest briefly before serving.
