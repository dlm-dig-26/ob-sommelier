About this wine

Cuvelier Los Andes is the Argentine estate of the Cuvelier family from Bordeaux, who also own Château Léoville Poyferré in Saint-Julien. In the 1990s the family joined the Clos de los Siete project led by Michel Rolland in Vista Flores, in the Uco Valley of Mendoza. They began planting their own vineyards in 1999 and made their first wine in 2003, with Rolland overseeing the winemaking. The Grand Vin is the top red of the estate: in recent vintages a Malbec-led blend with Cabernet Sauvignon and smaller parts of Petit Verdot and Syrah, aged in French oak. Expect dense black and red fruit, oak spice, fine ripe tannins and a dry finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Dense dark fruit and firm but ripe tannins hold up to a rich, spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces with onion and garlic, add tomato, cumin and a splash of red wine, then simmer two hours; serve with funchi.
  2. Carni stoba. Savory braised beef softens the tannins and lets the oak spice come through.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, capers and olives, cover with stock and simmer until tender.
  3. Grilled ribeye with chimichurri. A classic Argentine match: the char and fat of the steak meet the depth of the Malbec.
    Recipe idea: Salt the steak well, grill on high heat to medium rare, rest 5 minutes and serve with parsley, garlic and oregano chimichurri.
  4. Rosemary lamb chops. The herbal notes in the wine pick up the rosemary and garlic on grilled lamb.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chops in olive oil, rosemary, garlic and lemon zest for an hour, then grill about 3 minutes per side.
  5. Braised short ribs. Long-cooked, rich beef matches the full body and dry finish of this blend.
    Recipe idea: Brown short ribs, add onion, carrot, garlic, tomato paste and red wine, cover and braise in the oven for 3 hours.
