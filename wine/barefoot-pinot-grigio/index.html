About this wine

Barefoot traces its roots to 1965, when Davis Bynum made the first Barefoot Bynum Burgundy in his garage in Albany, California. In 1986 Bonnie Harvey and Michael Houlihan bought the brand and relaunched it as Barefoot Cellars with its footprint label, and E. & J. Gallo acquired it in 2005. Jennifer Wall has been the winemaker since 1995, and the winery is based in Modesto. This Pinot Grigio is light bodied and crisp, with tart green apple, fresh white peach, floral and citrus aromas and a bright finish.

On the shelf: Look for the Barefoot name and the brand signature bare footprint on the label.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 200 ml, 500 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L, 3 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pisca hasa. Light and crisp, it refreshes the palate between bites of fried fish.
    Recipe idea: Season snapper fillets with lime, salt and garlic, dust with flour and fry until golden. Serve with lime wedges and a fresh salad.
  2. Ceviche. Citrus aromas and bright acidity echo the lime in the fish.
    Recipe idea: Cut very fresh white fish into cubes, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili and coriander, and chill 15 to 20 minutes before serving.
  3. Grilled chicken. A light white that matches simple poultry without overpowering it.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in lemon, garlic, olive oil and oregano for an hour, then grill until cooked through and lightly charred.
  4. Pasta with lemon and garlic. Green apple freshness lifts a light, buttery pasta.
    Recipe idea: Cook spaghetti, toss with olive oil, garlic softened in butter, lemon zest and juice, parsley and grated parmesan.
  5. Margherita pizza. Crisp acidity cuts through melted mozzarella and tomato.
    Recipe idea: Spread pizza dough with tomato sauce, top with mozzarella and bake in a very hot oven until bubbling. Finish with fresh basil.
