What to eat with it

  1. Spicy chicken curry. A sweet, light white cools chili heat and suits aromatic spice.
    Recipe idea: Simmer chicken with onion, garlic, ginger, curry powder, a little hot pepper and coconut milk until tender. Serve with rice.
  2. Cala. Sweetness balances salty, peppery fried bean fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, rub off the skins, blend with salt and a little hot pepper, then fry spoonfuls of the batter until crisp and golden.
  3. Bolo di cashupete. A sweet wine works best with a dessert that is a little less sweet than the wine.
    Recipe idea: Bake a rich butter cake with chopped cashew nuts, flavoured with vanilla, and serve in thin slices.
  4. Fresh fruit salad. Fruity sweetness matches ripe tropical fruit.
    Recipe idea: Cut mango, papaya, pineapple and melon into cubes, add a squeeze of lime and a few mint leaves and chill.
  5. Sweet and sour pork. Sweet white wine mirrors the sweet glaze and tames the tang.
    Recipe idea: Fry battered pork pieces, then toss in a sauce of pineapple, peppers, vinegar, sugar, ketchup and soy until glossy.
