About this wine

Laureano Gomez is a small family winery in Colonia Las Rosas, Tunuyán, in Mendoza's Uco Valley, run by the enologist whose name is on the label. The Reserva Malbec is one of its signature wines. For one vintage the grapes were picked over seven separate harvest days, and each lot was fermented on its own and finished in barrel before blending. The Uco Valley is high and cool, with well-drained alluvial soils that give Malbec dark fruit and freshness. The family also runs a restaurant at the winery that serves lunch.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di karni. Beef stew with peppers and spice meets the ripe dark fruit and firm but rounded tannins.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a splash of wine, then simmer covered for two hours until tender.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Goat stew has the depth and slightly wild flavour that a full Malbec handles well.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic and curry. Brown it, add tomato, onion, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper if you like, and simmer slowly until soft.
  3. Grilled steak. The classic Argentine match: char and fat soften the tannins and bring out the fruit.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with coarse salt, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Rosemary lamb chops. Herbs and lamb fat match the body and dark fruit of the wine.
    Recipe idea: Rub chops with garlic, rosemary and olive oil, sear in a hot pan 3 minutes per side and finish with a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Mushroom beef burger. Savoury mushrooms and grilled beef echo the earthy side of Malbec.
    Recipe idea: Season ground beef with salt and pepper, grill, top with sautéed mushrooms and aged gouda and serve on a toasted bun.
