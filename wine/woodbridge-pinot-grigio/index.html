About this wine

Woodbridge by Robert Mondavi is based in Lodi, California, and dates back to 1979. The Mondavi family story begins with Cesare and Rosa Mondavi, who came to Minnesota from Sassoferrato in Italy in the early 1900s. This wine is mostly Pinot Grigio with a small share of other white grapes. The grapes are gently pressed and cold-fermented in stainless steel, with no oak, to keep the fruit fresh. Expect citrus, lime and stone fruit, a round citrus palate and a crisp finish.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled red snapper. Light body and citrus freshness suit simply grilled fish with lime.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic, thyme and salt, and grill 6 to 8 minutes per side until the flesh flakes.
  2. Ceviche. Crisp acidity echoes the lime and keeps raw fish tasting clean.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt.
  3. Funchi with fish. A clean, unoaked white that lets the tomato-stewed fish and cornmeal shine.
    Recipe idea: Stir cornmeal into salted boiling water with butter until thick and smooth, and serve with fish stewed in tomato, onion and pepper.
  4. Garlic shrimp. Lime and stone fruit freshen up garlic and olive oil.
    Recipe idea: Sauté peeled shrimp in olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 2 to 3 minutes, finish with lemon juice.
  5. Sushi rolls. Gentle fruit and crispness suit rice, fish and a little soy.
    Recipe idea: Roll seasoned sushi rice and nori around cucumber, avocado and crab or salmon, slice and serve with soy sauce and pickled ginger.
