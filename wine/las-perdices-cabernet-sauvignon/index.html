About this wine

Viña Las Perdices is a family-owned producer in Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo. Its vineyards lie at about 1,030 metres, and the area gets only around 150 mm of rain a year. The name comes from the partridges kept by the founder, Juan Muñoz López, who came from Andalusia. This Cabernet Sauvignon is deep ruby and full-bodied, with blackcurrant, blackberry and cedar, vanilla and spice from oak, firm tannins and good acidity.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled steak. Firm tannins and blackcurrant are a classic match for grilled steak.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 3 to 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Red pepper and spice notes suit goat stewed with cumin.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then stew slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little chili for about two hours until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Grilled lamb chops. Cedar and herbs echo rosemary on grilled lamb.
    Recipe idea: Marinate lamb chops in olive oil, garlic and rosemary for an hour, then grill 3 minutes per side over high heat.
  4. Aged cheese board. Aged cheese softens firm tannins.
    Recipe idea: Serve aged Gouda, manchego and cheddar at room temperature with walnuts, dried figs and crusty bread.
  5. Carni stoba. A full body and good acidity keep a rich beef stew lively.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a splash of vinegar, and simmer about two hours until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
