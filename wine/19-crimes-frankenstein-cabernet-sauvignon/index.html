About this wine

This Cabernet Sauvignon is a limited Halloween release from 19 Crimes, made in partnership with Universal Monsters. The Frankenstein artwork was redesigned by creature designer Crash McCreery, and the label glows in the dark. Scanning the QR code on the back opens an augmented reality experience that brings the monster to life. The wine itself is a firm, full red with dark fruit.

On the shelf: Glow-in-the-dark label with Frankenstein's monster artwork and the 19 Crimes name. QR code on the back.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. A firm, full red stands up to rich, spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Dark cherry and chocolate notes suit a deep beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Barbecue ribs. Bold fruit and tannin handle smoke and sticky glaze.
    Recipe idea: Bake pork ribs low and slow wrapped in foil for two hours, then brush with barbecue sauce and finish on a hot grill.
  4. Grilled steak. Firm tannins soften with charred, fatty beef.
    Recipe idea: Season sirloin with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 3 to 4 minutes a side and rest before slicing.
  5. Pepperoni pizza. Full-bodied fruit works with spicy cured meat and cheese.
    Recipe idea: Spread tomato sauce on dough, top with mozzarella and pepperoni and bake in a very hot oven until crisp.
