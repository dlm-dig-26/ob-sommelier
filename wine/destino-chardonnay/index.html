About this wine

Destino Chardonnay is made by Rutini Wines in Mendoza, a winery whose story starts with Felipe Rutini, the Italian immigrant who founded the La Rural winery in Coquimbito, Maipú, in 1885; his family planted vineyards in the Uco Valley in 1924. Winemaker Mariano Di Paola leads the team. The wine is light straw in colour, with peach, vanilla and fine oak on the nose and pineapple, grapefruit and a mineral touch on the palate. The 2019 vintage was 13 percent alcohol and sealed with a screw cap.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Peach fruit and a touch of oak suit the melted Gouda and savoury chicken filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a buttered dish with sliced Gouda, add chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, top with more cheese and bake until bubbling.
  2. Grilled red snapper. Fresh acidity and citrus lift grilled whole fish without masking it.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with garlic, lime, salt and herbs, and grill over medium heat until the flesh flakes. Serve with lime.
  3. Funchi with fish. A medium-bodied white that matches creamy cornmeal and tender fish.
    Recipe idea: Cook cornmeal in salted water with a little butter, stirring until thick. Serve with pan-fried fish in a light tomato and onion sauce.
  4. Grilled salmon. Pineapple and grapefruit notes balance the richness of salmon.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with oil, season with salt, pepper and lemon zest, and grill skin side down until just cooked through.
  5. Seafood risotto. Its mineral edge and gentle oak pair well with creamy rice and shellfish.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio rice with onion, add white wine and fish stock little by little, then stir in shrimp and mussels and finish with butter and parsley.
