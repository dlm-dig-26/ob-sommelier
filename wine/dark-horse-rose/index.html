About this wine

Dark Horse is a California wine brand based in Modesto, with Beth Liston named as winemaker. For its rosé, the brand says the fruit comes from selected vineyards across California, and a US retailer describes it as a fruit-forward, Provence-style rosé with a screw cap. Expect a pale, dry pink wine with red berries, citrus and stone fruit and a crisp finish, made for drinking young and well chilled. The same rosé is also sold in a 375 ml can.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. A dry, crisp rosé with grapefruit notes stays fresh next to lime-cured fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili, cilantro and a little mango, and serve cold.
  2. Grilled red snapper. Light red fruit and bright acidity flatter grilled white fish.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with lime, garlic, salt and oil, and grill about six minutes per side.
  3. Cheese pastechi. Crisp acidity cleans the palate between bites of warm, salty Gouda pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with grated Gouda, fold, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  4. Watermelon and feta salad. The wine's watermelon and strawberry notes echo the fruit, while acidity balances salty feta.
    Recipe idea: Toss cubed watermelon with crumbled feta, mint, red onion, olive oil and a squeeze of lime.
  5. Shrimp tacos. Fruity and dry, it refreshes after lightly spiced shrimp and lime.
    Recipe idea: Sauté shrimp with chili and garlic, serve in warm tortillas with cabbage, avocado and lime crema.
