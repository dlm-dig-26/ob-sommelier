About this wine

Meiomi was created by winemaker Joe Wagner, and its name means coast in the languages of the Wappo and Yuki peoples of Northern California. True to the name, the Chardonnay blends grapes from three coastal counties: Sonoma for structure, Monterey for bright acidity and Santa Barbara for tropical richness. It is fermented in stainless steel, goes through full malolactic conversion and ages on French oak with lees stirring, which builds its creamy texture. Constellation Brands bought Meiomi in 2015.

Bottle sizes made: 375 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena with chicken. Creamy, rich Chardonnay matches melted Gouda, and its acidity cuts through the cheese.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda, fill with chicken stewed with tomato, onion, olives and raisins, top with more cheese and bake until golden.
  2. Lobster in butter sauce. Butter and sweet lobster meet the wine's custard and tropical notes; a classic buttery white match.
    Recipe idea: Split lobster tails, brush with garlic butter and lime, grill or broil until just opaque, and spoon extra butter over.
  3. Galinja stoba. Gently spiced stewed chicken is rich enough for a full white without needing red-wine tannin.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then stew with onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and a little soy sauce until tender. Serve with rice.
  4. Roast chicken with lemon. Crisp skin and juicy meat suit the wine's pear, peach and creamy texture.
    Recipe idea: Rub a chicken with butter, salt, lemon zest and thyme, stuff with lemon halves and roast at 200C for about 75 minutes.
  5. Pasta in brown butter sauce. Nutty brown butter echoes the wine's toasted almond and honey notes.
    Recipe idea: Brown butter with sage until nutty, toss with cooked tagliatelle and parmesan, and finish with black pepper.
