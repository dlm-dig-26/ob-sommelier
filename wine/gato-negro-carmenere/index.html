About this wine

Gato Negro is made by Viña San Pedro, founded in 1865 and one of the oldest and largest wine exporters in Chile, now part of VSPT Wine Group. The brand is built around easy, everyday wines, and the range even includes low-alcohol and alcohol-free versions. This Carmenère comes from the Central Valley of Chile, with Ana María Silva as winemaker. It is almost black-purple in the glass, with raspberry, blackberry and violet, hints of vanilla, coffee and smoke, a medium body and soft tannins.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Dark berries and a smoky edge suit a cumin-scented beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  2. Beef empanadas. Soft tannins and spice are an easy match for savoury, cumin-spiced beef.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef cooked with onion, cumin, paprika, olives and egg, fold, crimp the edges and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  3. Barbecue chicken. Coffee and smoke notes echo the char of grilled chicken.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in barbecue sauce with garlic and a little hot pepper, grill until charred and cooked through, brushing with more sauce.
  4. Stobá di kabritu. A medium-bodied red with enough fruit and spice for goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat in lime, garlic and pepper, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, cumin and a little Scotch bonnet for 2 to 3 hours.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. Fresh red fruit and gentle tannins suit a tomato and beef sauce.
    Recipe idea: Cook ground beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and a splash of red wine, simmer 45 minutes and serve over spaghetti.
