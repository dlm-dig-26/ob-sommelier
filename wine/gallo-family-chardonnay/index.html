About this wine

Gallo Family Vineyards is the everyday wine range of E. and J. Gallo Winery in Modesto, California, the company of the brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo, whose family has been making wine for over 80 years. This Chardonnay uses grapes from across California. It is fermented in stainless steel with a light touch of oak, which keeps it fruity and fresh while adding a soft, creamy feel.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. A creamy, medium-bodied white suits the melted cheese and mild spice.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or gouda with spiced chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, top with cheese and bake until golden and melted.
  2. Pisca hasa. Ripe apple and a touch of vanilla go well with crisp fried fish.
    Recipe idea: Season fish fillets with lime, garlic and salt, dust lightly with flour and pan-fry until crisp. Serve with funchi or pan bati and a fresh creole sauce.
  3. Sopi di pisca. Soft fruit and medium body match a gentle, herby fish soup.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish with onion, garlic, celery, potato, pumpkin and a little hot pepper in fish stock until tender. Finish with lime and fresh herbs.
  4. Chicken alfredo. Light oak and creaminess mirror a rich cream sauce.
    Recipe idea: Cook fettuccine, sauté sliced chicken in butter, add cream and parmesan, simmer until thick and toss with the pasta and black pepper.
  5. Grilled salmon. Fruity and smooth, it suits oily fish with lemon.
    Recipe idea: Season salmon with salt, pepper and lemon zest, grill skin side down for 6 minutes, flip for 2 more and finish with lemon juice.
