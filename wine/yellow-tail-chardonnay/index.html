About this wine

Yellow Tail comes from Casella Family Brands in Yenda, New South Wales. Filippo and Maria Casella, who came to Australia from Sicily in 1957, founded the winery in 1969, and the family still runs it. This wine is 100 percent Chardonnay, blended from several regions of South Eastern Australia and lightly oaked at the winery in Yenda. It shows melon, white peach and a little vanilla, with a creamy, silky finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. A creamy texture and ripe peach suit the mellow cheese and savoury filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam, or a dish lined with cheese slices, with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives and raisins; cover with cheese and bake until melted.
  2. Shrimp creole. Tropical fruit and a smooth body calm a gently spicy tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Cook onion, sweet pepper and celery in oil, add tomato, garlic and hot sauce, simmer 15 minutes and stir in shrimp for the last 4 minutes.
  3. Grilled red snapper. Lightly oaked and round, it flatters grilled white fish.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole snapper with oil, garlic and herbs, grill 6 to 8 minutes per side and finish with lemon.
  4. Roast chicken. A soft, creamy white that matches golden roast chicken.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, lemon and thyme and roast at 200°C for about 1 hour 15 minutes, until the juices run clear.
  5. Grilled salmon. Melon and vanilla notes work with the richness of salmon.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with oil, soy sauce and a little honey and grill or pan-fry 4 minutes per side.
