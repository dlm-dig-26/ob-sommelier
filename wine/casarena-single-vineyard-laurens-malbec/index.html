About this wine

Bodega Casarena makes its wines in a winery building dating from 1937 in Luján de Cuyo, the historic heart of Mendoza winemaking. Its single-vineyard wines are named after individual estates, and this Malbec comes from Lauren's Vineyard in the Agrelo district, planted at about 920 metres on deep clay soils. Lead winemaker Martina Galeano, who studied enology in Mendoza and earned a master's at UC Davis, works alongside agronomist Pablo Ceverino on the estates' soil and terroir research. Expect a ripe, generous Malbec with dark fruit, floral notes and a touch of mocha.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe plum and spice stand up to slow-cooked goat with cumin and pepper.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper. Simmer covered 2 hours until tender.
  2. Pastechi with beef. Juicy dark fruit and soft tannins suit savoury spiced beef in a crisp pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with spiced ground beef, raisins and olives, fold, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Ripe dark fruit and round tannins are a natural match for charred, well-marbled beef.
    Recipe idea: Salt a ribeye an hour ahead, grill on high heat 4 to 5 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Roast leg of lamb. Floral and mocha notes work with rosemary and garlic; the wine's length matches the rich meat.
    Recipe idea: Stud a lamb leg with garlic and rosemary, rub with oil and salt, roast at 180 C about 1 hour 20 minutes for pink meat.
  5. Smoked beef brisket. Plush fruit and mocha tones go with smoky bark and tender beef.
    Recipe idea: Rub brisket with salt, pepper and paprika, smoke or bake low at 120 C for 8 to 10 hours wrapped in foil, then slice thin.
