About this wine

Meiomi was founded by winemaker Joe Wagner, and its name means coast in the Wappo and Yuki languages. The Pinot Noir is a blend of grapes from cool coastal vineyards in Sonoma, Monterey and Santa Barbara counties, combined to give a consistent, fruit-rich style. The brand was bought by Constellation Brands in 2015, and Jason Becker leads the winemaking today. It is known for a smooth, generous take on Pinot Noir.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Soft tannins and ripe berry fruit suit chicken stewed in a light tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a pinch of cumin, then simmer until tender and saucy. Serve with white rice.
  2. Grilled red snapper. A smooth Pinot is one of the few reds gentle enough for grilled fish.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole red snapper with lime, garlic, salt and pepper, grill over medium heat for about 8 minutes per side and finish with a squeeze of lime.
  3. Roast duck. Jammy fruit and a hint of mocha meet rich duck meat nicely.
    Recipe idea: Prick duck skin, season with salt and five spice, roast at 180C for about 90 minutes until crisp, and serve with a cherry or orange sauce.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms bring out the soft oak and red fruit.
    Recipe idea: Sauté onion and mushrooms, toast arborio rice, add stock a ladle at a time until creamy, then stir in butter and parmesan.
  5. Glazed salmon. Ripe fruit and smooth texture work with a soy-honey glaze.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon with soy sauce, honey and garlic, bake at 200C for 12 minutes and serve with rice and greens.
