About this wine

Santa Margherita was founded in 1935 by Gaetano Marzotto and is based in Fossalta di Portogruaro, in eastern Veneto; today it is part of the HERITA Marzotto Wine Estates group. Its rosé carries the Trevenezie IGT label, which covers north-east Italy. It is an unusual blend: mostly Chardonnay with Sauvignon Blanc and other white grapes, plus Pinot Nero that gives the pale pink colour. Each grape is made into wine separately before blending, and malolactic fermentation is blocked to keep the colour bright and the acidity lively.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Lime-cured fish needs a crisp, dry rosé with lively acidity and citrus notes.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili and cilantro; chill 20 minutes.
  2. Sopi di pisca. A light fish soup suits a delicate, fresh rosé that will not overpower it.
    Recipe idea: Simmer fish pieces with onion, garlic, tomato, potato and herbs; finish with a squeeze of lime.
  3. Grilled salmon. Pink-fleshed fish and the wine's red-berry fruit are a natural match.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with olive oil, lemon and dill; grill skin-side down for 6 to 8 minutes.
  4. Shrimp cocktail. Sweet shrimp and a tangy sauce pair well with strawberry and citrus notes.
    Recipe idea: Poach shrimp 2 minutes, chill, and serve with ketchup mixed with horseradish and lemon.
  5. Tomato bruschetta. Fresh tomato and basil love a light, dry rosé with good acidity.
    Recipe idea: Toast bread, rub with garlic, top with diced tomato, basil, salt and olive oil.
