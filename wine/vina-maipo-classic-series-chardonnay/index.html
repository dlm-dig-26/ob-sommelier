About this wine

Viña Maipo is a Chilean winery founded in 1948, with around 700 hectares of vineyards spread across the Central Valley. This wide region runs south of Santiago and includes the Maipo, Rapel, Curicó and Maule valleys, where most vineyards sit on flat, fertile land sheltered from the sea. Max Weinlaub has been the winemaker since 2007. The Classic Series Chardonnay is made in a young, fruity and dry style, built around fresh tropical and stone fruit rather than heavy oak.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Fresh acidity and pineapple and citrus notes mirror the lime in the dish and keep raw fish bright.
    Recipe idea: Cube very fresh white fish, cover with lime juice and leave 15 minutes. Add red onion, chili, cilantro, salt and a little diced mango.
  2. Cala. A light, crisp white cuts through the crunch and oil of these bean fritters and refreshes the palate.
    Recipe idea: Soak black-eyed peas overnight, rub off skins, blend with onion, chili and salt into a thick batter and fry spoonfuls until golden.
  3. Funchi with fried fish. Simple, fruity Chardonnay suits mild cornmeal funchi and crisp fried fish without overwhelming them.
    Recipe idea: Whisk cornmeal into salted boiling water with butter and stir until thick. Serve with fish fillets fried in seasoned flour and lime.
  4. Grilled chicken salad. Light body and fresh fruit match grilled chicken and crisp greens and lets the fresh dressing shine.
    Recipe idea: Grill seasoned chicken breast, slice and serve over lettuce, cucumber, avocado and cherry tomatoes with a lemon and olive oil dressing.
  5. Shrimp tacos. Juicy tropical fruit and clean acidity pair well with seared shrimp, lime and a crunchy slaw.
    Recipe idea: Saute shrimp with garlic, paprika and lime, then serve in warm tortillas with shredded cabbage, avocado and a squeeze of lime.
