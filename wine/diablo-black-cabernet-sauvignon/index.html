About this wine

Diablo Black is a Cabernet Sauvignon from the Maule Valley in Chile, made by Viña Concha y Toro with winemaker Héctor Urzúa. The grapes are fermented in stainless steel and the wine then spends several months in oak, which adds smoky dark wood, tobacco and vanilla to the dark fruit. The recipe varies by market: the UK bottling is listed as 100 percent Cabernet Sauvignon aged in French and American oak, while a US listing adds a little Cabernet Franc and uses French oak only. Both describe a full-bodied, structured and silky red.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Firm tannins and smoky oak stand up to the strong, spiced flavour of goat.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. Slow-cooked beef softens a structured Cabernet and matches its weight.
    Recipe idea: Simmer beef cubes with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, capers and olives for about 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Charred, fatty beef is the best partner for firm Cabernet tannins.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Barbecue ribs. Smoke and tobacco notes in the wine echo the grill.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, garlic and brown sugar, bake covered at 150°C for 2 hours, then glaze and grill.
  5. Rosemary lamb chops. Lamb and rosemary handle the dark fruit and dark-wood notes well.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary and olive oil, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and serve with roast potatoes.
