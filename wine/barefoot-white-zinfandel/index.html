About this wine

Barefoot is a California wine brand, and its White Zinfandel is a pink wine made from Zinfandel, a red grape. In the White Zinfandel style the juice spends only a short time with the dark grape skins, which gives the pale pink colour and a light, fruity character. This one is light in body and moderately sweet, with lively acidity and low alcohol, around 9 percent. Expect strawberry up front, then pear, pineapple and ripe peach, finishing crisp and clean. Serve it well chilled.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Spicy ceviche. A touch of sweetness cools the chili heat, and the bright acidity matches the lime.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, cilantro and a little madame jeanette pepper. Chill 20 minutes.
  2. Pastechi di keshi. Light and fruity, it refreshes the palate between bites of salty fried cheese pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with grated Gouda, fold, seal with a fork and fry in hot oil until golden and puffed.
  3. Spicy chicken wings. Sweet strawberry fruit and low alcohol calm the heat of hot sauce.
    Recipe idea: Bake seasoned wings at 220 C for 40 minutes, then toss in melted butter mixed with hot sauce and a little honey.
  4. Strawberry fruit salad. Fruit with fruit: the wine echoes strawberry and peach without seeming too sweet.
    Recipe idea: Mix sliced strawberries, peach, pineapple and mint with a squeeze of lime. Chill and serve in small bowls.
  5. Mild cheese board. Soft, creamy cheeses pair easily with a light, off-dry pink wine.
    Recipe idea: Arrange brie, young Gouda and cream cheese with crackers, grapes and a few slices of fresh pear.
