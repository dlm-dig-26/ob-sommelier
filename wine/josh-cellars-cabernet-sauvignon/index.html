About this wine

Josh Cellars was started by Joseph Carr, who began as a wine steward, worked as a sommelier in leading restaurants and learned winemaking at Freemark Abbey in Napa. He named the label after his father, Josh, an Army veteran and volunteer firefighter from Berlin, New York, as a tribute from son to father. The winery works with growers across California regions such as Napa, Sonoma, Mendocino and the Central Coast. This Cabernet Sauvignon carries a California appellation and is round and juicy, with blackberry, toasted hazelnut and cinnamon over hints of vanilla and toasted oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pisca hasa with spicy sauce. Fried snapper with a rich creole sauce can take a fruity, round red; skip it with plain delicate fish.
    Recipe idea: Season whole snapper with lime, salt and garlic, fry until crisp, and top with a sauce of onion, tomato, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper.
  2. Carni stoba. Tender beef stew with cumin matches the wine's blackberry and cinnamon spice.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes and stew with onion, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a splash of vinegar until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Herb-crusted lamb chops. Well-seasoned lamb is what the producer suggests; the oak and dark fruit love rosemary and garlic.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, olive oil, salt and pepper, sear in a hot pan 3 minutes per side and rest briefly.
  4. Barbecue pork shoulder. Smoky, slightly sweet pork suits a juicy Cabernet with soft tannins.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork shoulder with paprika, brown sugar, garlic and cumin, roast low for 5 hours, shred and mix with a little barbecue sauce.
  5. Dark chocolate brownies. Rich chocolate desserts echo the toasted oak and vanilla; keep the brownies not too sweet.
    Recipe idea: Melt dark chocolate and butter, whisk in sugar, eggs and a little flour, add chopped hazelnuts and bake until just set in the centre.
