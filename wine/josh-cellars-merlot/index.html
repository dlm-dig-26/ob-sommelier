About this wine

Josh Cellars was founded by Joseph Carr, a former sommelier raised in upstate New York, who named the brand after his father Josh, a US Army veteran and volunteer firefighter. Carr started the company in California with his wife Dee, and the first vintage was a 2007 Cabernet Sauvignon. Since 2012 the brand has worked with Deutsch Family Wine and Spirits. This Merlot carries the California appellation and shows ripe plum, black cherry and mocha, with smooth tannins and a touch of vanilla and spice from oak.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe dark fruit and smooth tannins stand up to the rich, spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic and spices, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, sweet pepper and cumin until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Keshi yena. Smooth and fruity, it suits the savory chicken filling and melted Gouda.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Gouda or a cheese-lined dish with stewed chicken, olives, raisins and capers, then bake until the cheese melts.
  3. Spaghetti bolognese. Plum fruit and balanced acidity work with a meaty tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown ground beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and simmer for an hour. Toss with spaghetti and top with parmesan.
  4. Grilled steak. Dark cherry and mocha notes pair well with the char of grilled beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a sirloin with salt, pepper and garlic, grill over high heat to your liking and rest for five minutes before slicing.
  5. Soft cheese board. Velvety texture and gentle tannins flatter creamy cheeses.
    Recipe idea: Arrange brie, camembert and a mild Gouda with crackers, grapes and a few walnuts. Let the cheese come to room temperature first.
