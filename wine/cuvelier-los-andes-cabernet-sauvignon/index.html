About this wine

Cuvelier Los Andes belongs to the Cuvelier family, whose roots in French wine go back to 1804 and who also co-own Château Léoville-Poyferré in Saint-Julien, Bordeaux. The family came to Argentina in 1998, planted its first vines in 1999 and released the first Cuvelier Los Andes wines in 2003. The estate lies in Vista Flores, Tunuyán, in the Uco Valley, at about 1,000 metres, on stony alluvial soils that are farmed organically and biodynamically. This Cabernet Sauvignon shows the grape's classic red pepper and spice character with good concentration and length.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Slow-cooked goat with spice stands up to the firm tannins and dark fruit of this Cabernet.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic, cumin and curry, brown it, add onion, tomato and sweet pepper and simmer slowly for two hours.
  2. Carni stoba. A rich beef stew softens the tannins while the wine's pepper notes lift the sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, sweet pepper, a bay leaf and a splash of wine, cover and simmer until tender.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Fat and char from the grill are the best partner for a structured, tannic red.
    Recipe idea: Salt a thick ribeye, grill on high heat 4 to 5 minutes per side, rest it and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Roast leg of lamb. Lamb and rosemary echo the herbal, peppery side of Cabernet Sauvignon.
    Recipe idea: Stud lamb with garlic and rosemary, rub with olive oil and salt and roast at 180 C until pink inside, then rest before slicing.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. A meaty tomato sauce has enough weight and savour for a full red.
    Recipe idea: Brown minced beef with onion, carrot and garlic, add tomatoes and a little wine, simmer an hour and toss with spaghetti.
