About this wine

Trivento was founded in Mendoza in 1996 and has more than 1,500 hectares of vineyards. Its name refers to three winds that blow across Mendoza: the Polar, the Zonda and the Sudestada. The Reserve Cabernet-Malbec blends Cabernet Sauvignon and Malbec and is aged in oak, which adds vanilla to plum, red fruit and blackberry. Retailers describe it as bold and full-bodied with fleshy, firm tannins. Some recent listings add Syrah to the blend, so the exact recipe can vary from one bottling to the next.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled ribeye. Firm tannins and dark fruit are made for charred, juicy beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  2. Keshi yena. Round, ripe fruit stands up to rich melted cheese and a savoury filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Gouda or Edam with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, capers and raisins, top with cheese and bake 30 minutes.
  3. Stobá di kabritu. A bold red blend matches the strong, spiced flavour of goat.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  4. Beef empanadas. Plum and spice in the wine suit cumin-seasoned beef.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with beef fried with onion, cumin and paprika, fold, seal and bake at 200°C for about 20 minutes.
  5. Barbecue ribs. Vanilla from the oak echoes a smoky, sweet glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, garlic and brown sugar, bake covered at 150°C for 2 hours, then glaze and grill.
