About this wine

Viña Las Perdices is a family winery of the Muñoz López family in Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo, about 40 km from Mendoza city at the foot of the Andes. It farms 96 hectares with 17 grape varieties, including vines more than 40 years old, and its original building follows the Spanish-influenced style of traditional Mendoza wineries. Ala Colorada is a high-end line of the winery, made from Agrelo grapes; an earlier wine in the line aged 15 months in new French and American oak. Tannat comes from southwest France, where it gives deep-coloured, firmly tannic reds, and oak ageing is often used to soften it.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Powerful tannins need a rich, hearty dish like slow-cooked spiced goat.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little Madame Jeanette pepper, then simmer covered for about 2 hours until tender.
  2. Carni stoba. A hearty beef stew softens the firm tannins and matches the dark fruit.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato paste, cumin and stock, simmer for about 1.5 hours and serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Fat and char from the steak tame the tannins of this big red.
    Recipe idea: Season the steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat for about 4 minutes per side, rest for 5 minutes and top with garlic butter.
  4. Barbecue beef short ribs. Smoky, rich ribs stand up to a wine of this depth and structure.
    Recipe idea: Rub short ribs with salt, pepper, paprika and garlic, cook slowly at 150°C for 3 hours, then glaze with barbecue sauce and grill briefly.
  5. Aged Gouda. The salt and fat of a well-aged cheese soften the firm tannins.
    Recipe idea: Serve thin slices of aged Gouda at room temperature with walnuts, dried figs and crackers.
