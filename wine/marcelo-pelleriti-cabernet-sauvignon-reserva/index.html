About this wine

Marcelo Pelleriti is an Argentine winemaker with more than 30 years of work in Argentina and France, where he has helped make the Bordeaux wines of the Péré-Vergé family, including Château La Violette in Pomerol. In 2013 he became the first Latin American winemaker to earn 100 Parker points, for La Violette 2010. He made his first wine in the Uco Valley in 1994 and in 2007 launched his own label to showcase the terroirs where his career began. The Reserve range was the first line of that project and is now sold as Signature; this is its Uco Valley Cabernet Sauvignon.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Firm Cabernet tannins soften against slow braised beef, and blackcurrant fruit suits the tomato and pepper sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, a little vinegar and cumin, cover and simmer 2 hours with potatoes.
  2. Pastechi di carni. Savoury spiced beef and crisp pastry like a structured red; the acidity cuts the fried crust.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of dough with ground beef cooked with onion, raisins, olives and spices, fold, seal with a fork and fry until golden.
  3. Grilled ribeye with garlic butter. Fat and char from the ribeye tame the wine's tannins and bring out its cedar notes.
    Recipe idea: Season ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 to 5 minutes per side, rest and top with butter mashed with garlic and parsley.
  4. Beef lasagna. Rich meat sauce and cheese need a full red with enough acidity to keep each bite fresh.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with beef and tomato ragu, béchamel and grated cheese, then bake 40 minutes until bubbling and browned.
  5. Cheddar cheeseburgers. A juicy burger with sharp cheddar meets the dark fruit and grip of a Uco Cabernet.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef patties, grill 4 minutes per side, melt aged cheddar on top and serve with onions, tomato and pickles.
