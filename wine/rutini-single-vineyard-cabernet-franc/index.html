About this wine

Rutini Wines traces its roots to Felipe Rutini, who founded Bodega La Rural in Mendoza in 1885. Today its winery is in Tupungato in the Uco Valley, with about 180 hectares of vines at 1,000 to 1,200 metres, and Rutini says it pioneered Cabernet Franc in Gualtallary in 1996. This Single Vineyard Gualtallary Cabernet Franc is 100 percent Cabernet Franc from that area, fermented with native yeasts and aged 12 months, half of it in French oak. The producer describes red fruit, cherry jam, mint, vanilla and tobacco with soft, round tannins.

What to eat with it

  1. Rosemary lamb chops. The herbal, minty side of Cabernet Franc is a natural match for lamb and rosemary.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary and olive oil, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and serve with roast potatoes.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Fresh acidity and red fruit balance the rich, spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer 2 hours until tender.
  3. Carni stoba. Firm but fine tannins suit slow-cooked beef with sweet pepper.
    Recipe idea: Simmer beef cubes with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, capers and olives for about 90 minutes until tender.
  4. Grilled pork chops. Bright acidity and cherry fruit lift juicy pork and its herb marinade.
    Recipe idea: Marinate pork chops in garlic, orange juice and oregano for an hour, then grill 5 minutes per side.
  5. Grilled ribeye. A high-altitude red with structure holds up to charred beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
