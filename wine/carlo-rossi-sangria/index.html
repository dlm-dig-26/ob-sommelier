About this wine

Carlo Rossi is one of the wine brands of E. & J. Gallo, the family winery founded in 1933 in Modesto, California, by brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo. The brand takes its name from Charles Rossi, a relative of the Gallo family by marriage, who starred in its advertising in the 1970s, around the time Gallo took the brand nationwide. The Sangria is a light, sweet drink made from red wine with citrus flavours, and is meant to be served well chilled over ice or in a pitcher with sliced fruit.

On the shelf: Larger sizes come in a glass jug with a screw cap and a small finger-ring handle.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L, 3 L, 4 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi. Sweet fruit and cold citrus freshness balance salty, fried pastechi filled with cheese or meat.
    Recipe idea: Roll out a simple dough, fill rounds with Gouda or spiced ground beef, fold into half moons, seal the edges and deep fry until golden.
  2. Keshi yena. The fruity sweetness picks up the raisins and olives in this sweet-and-savoury baked cheese dish.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, raisins, olives and capers, cover with more cheese and bake until melted.
  3. Spicy chicken wings. Sweetness and a cold glass tame the heat of spicy wings.
    Recipe idea: Toss wings with oil, salt, paprika and cayenne, bake at 200 C for 40 minutes, then coat with hot sauce mixed with a little honey.
  4. Garlic shrimp. A light, citrusy sangria fits Spanish-style garlic shrimp served as a snack.
    Recipe idea: Sizzle sliced garlic and chili flakes in olive oil, add shrimp and cook 2 to 3 minutes, then finish with parsley and lemon juice.
  5. Fresh fruit salad. A sweet, fruity wine matches the juice of fresh fruit without tasting sour.
    Recipe idea: Cut mango, pineapple, papaya and strawberries, add a squeeze of lime and a few mint leaves, and chill before serving.
