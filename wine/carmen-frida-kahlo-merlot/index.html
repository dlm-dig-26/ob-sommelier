About this wine

Viña Carmen, founded in 1850, presents itself as Chile's first winery and today belongs to the Santa Rita Estates group, with its home base in Buin in the Maipo Valley. It is also the winery where Carménère was identified in 1994, a grape long thought lost after phylloxera struck Europe. The Frida Kahlo line is Carmen's tribute to the Mexican painter. This Reserva Merlot comes from the Maipo Valley, part of Chile's Central Valley, and is made in a smooth, approachable style with soft tannins and ripe red fruit.

On the shelf: Carmen label carrying the Frida Kahlo name, with Reserva and Merlot marked on the front.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Soft tannins and plum fruit sit well with tender stewed chicken and its savoury tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato, a splash of the wine and stock, and simmer 45 minutes. Serve with rice.
  2. Keshi yena. Medium body and gentle spice suit the rich cheese shell and the mildly spiced chicken filling.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, raisins and capers, top with more cheese and bake until golden.
  3. Carni stoba. Ripe red fruit and a smooth texture match slow-cooked beef without overpowering the sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, tomato, peppers and a little vinegar and sugar, and simmer gently for two hours. Serve with funchi or rice.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms and the wine's plum fruit and light oak bring out the best in each other.
    Recipe idea: Sauté onion and mushrooms, toast arborio rice, add stock a ladle at a time for 18 minutes, then finish with butter and Parmesan.
  5. Herb-roasted pork tenderloin. Lean pork wants a red with gentle tannins, and this Merlot's ripe fruit adds juiciness.
    Recipe idea: Rub the tenderloin with garlic, thyme, salt and pepper, sear it, then roast at 200 °C for 15 to 20 minutes. Rest before slicing.
