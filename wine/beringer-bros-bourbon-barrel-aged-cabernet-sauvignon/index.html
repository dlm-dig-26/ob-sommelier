About this wine

Beringer Bros. is a range from Beringer that pays tribute to brothers Jacob and Frederick Beringer, who left Germany about 150 years ago to found a winery and distillery in Napa Valley. The brothers reused spirit barrels to age wine, and this range revives that idea. The Cabernet Sauvignon is made from California grapes and aged for 60 days in American oak barrels that once held bourbon, which adds toasted vanilla notes and rounds out the tannins. Expect blackberry, dark cherry and vanilla with a smooth finish.

On the shelf: Spirits-style bottle with a label showing a historic photo of the Beringer brothers in front of their winery.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe dark fruit and vanilla oak suit this rich, spiced goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat overnight in lime, garlic and cumin, brown it, then stew 2 hours with onion, tomato, sweet pepper and a little hot pepper.
  2. Carni stoba. Smooth tannins and dark fruit match tender braised beef in a savoury sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks with onion and garlic, add tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and stock, and simmer gently for 2 hours.
  3. Barbecue ribs. Vanilla and caramel notes from the bourbon barrels echo a sweet, smoky barbecue glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with smoked paprika, brown sugar and salt, bake covered 2 hours at 150°C, then glaze with barbecue sauce and grill briefly.
  4. Pan-seared sirloin steak. A full-bodied Cabernet with ripe tannins that stands up to well-browned red meat.
    Recipe idea: Bring the steak to room temperature, season well, sear 3 to 4 minutes per side in a hot pan, rest and slice thinly.
  5. Cheddar and mushroom burger. Toasty oak and dark fruit match a juicy burger with melted cheddar.
    Recipe idea: Grill beef patties, top with aged cheddar and sautéed mushrooms, and serve in toasted buns with onion.
