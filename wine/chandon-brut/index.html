About this wine

Chandon Argentina was founded in 1959 in Agrelo, near Luján de Cuyo in Mendoza, after Robert-Jean de Vogüé set out to find new terroirs, with Renaud Poirier as its first chief winemaker. It is part of the LVMH group. Chandon Brut blends Chardonnay and Pinot Noir grown on the El Yaima and Caicayén estates in the Uco Valley, at 1,000 to 1,200 metres. The base wine is fermented in stainless steel before the second fermentation that creates the bubbles. Fresh and elegant, it shows white peach, lime, jasmine and light brioche.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Cala. Fine bubbles and crisp acidity scrub the palate clean after hot, crunchy bean fritters.
    Recipe idea: Soak and blend black-eyed peas with onion, salt and a little hot pepper, beat until fluffy and fry spoonfuls until golden.
  2. Pastechi. A dry sparkling wine is a natural partner for fried, savoury pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill dough rounds with cheese or spiced ground beef, fold into half moons, seal and fry until crisp.
  3. Ceviche. Lime and white peach notes in the wine echo the citrus and freshness of the dish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice, red onion, hot pepper and cilantro, chill 20 minutes and serve.
  4. Fried chicken. Bubbles and acidity cut through the crunchy, rich coating.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken pieces in buttermilk with garlic and paprika, dredge in seasoned flour and fry until golden and cooked through.
  5. Sushi rolls. Light body and clean freshness suit raw fish, rice and a touch of soy.
    Recipe idea: Spread sushi rice on nori, add strips of fresh fish, cucumber and avocado, roll tightly and slice.
