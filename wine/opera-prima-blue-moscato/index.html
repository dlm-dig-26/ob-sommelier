About this wine

Opera Prima is a brand of J. García Carrión, a Spanish wine company founded in 1890 that makes wine in several Spanish regions, including Rioja and Ribera del Duero, and is also known for its Don Simón label. Opera Prima Blue Moscato is a light, sweet sparkling wine made from Muscat, with about 7 percent alcohol. The bubbles come from the Charmat method: the second fermentation takes place in a large stainless steel tank rather than in each bottle, which keeps the fruity, floral Muscat aromas fresh.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Quesillo. A silky caramel custard and a sweet, fruity sparkler share the same gentle sweetness.
    Recipe idea: Melt sugar to caramel in a mould, blend eggs, condensed milk, milk and vanilla, pour in and bake in a water bath at 170 C for about an hour. Chill well.
  2. Tropical fruit salad. Mango, pineapple and papaya echo the wine's tropical fruit and white flower aromas.
    Recipe idea: Dice mango, pineapple, papaya and melon, add a squeeze of lime and a few mint leaves and serve chilled.
  3. Spicy jerk chicken wings. Sweetness and soft fizz cool down the heat of Scotch bonnet and allspice.
    Recipe idea: Marinate wings in jerk paste of Scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme, garlic and lime, then grill or bake until crisp and charred.
  4. Bolo pretu. A small slice of dark fruit cake is lifted by the wine's bubbles and fresh berry notes.
    Recipe idea: Soak dried fruit in rum for a few days, mix into a batter with butter, brown sugar, eggs, flour and spices and bake slowly until set.
  5. Strawberry shortcake. Berries and cream match the light body and fresh berry aroma of this Moscato.
    Recipe idea: Split sweet biscuits or sponge cake, fill with sliced sugared strawberries and whipped cream and serve straight away.
