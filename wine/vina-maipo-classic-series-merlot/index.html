About this wine

Viña Maipo was founded in 1948 in Chile's Maipo Valley, one of the country's most traditional wine regions, and today it sources grapes from several of Chile's main valleys. The winery is part of the Concha y Toro family of wineries, and the Classic Series is one of its ranges. This Merlot comes from the Central Valley, a large region with warm days and cool nights where Merlot tends to be fruit-forward and plummy. Expect a medium-bodied red with black cherry, a touch of chocolate and vanilla, and medium tannins.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. A smooth, fruity Merlot pairs nicely with the mild cheese and savory chicken filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or slices of Gouda with stewed chicken, olives, raisins, capers and tomato, then bake until the cheese melts.
  2. Pastechi. Easy plum and cherry fruit goes well with crisp pastry filled with spiced beef or cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with seasoned ground beef or Gouda, fold into half-moons, seal the edges and fry until golden.
  3. Grilled lamb chops. Medium tannins and dark cherry fruit suit the savory flavor of lamb from the grill.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper and grill over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes a side.
  4. Pork chops. A soft, medium-bodied red does not overpower lean pork, and its fruit flatters a light glaze.
    Recipe idea: Season pork chops with salt, pepper and paprika, sear in a hot pan for 4 minutes a side and finish with a spoon of honey mustard.
  5. Pasta with tomato and sausage. Fresh acidity and red fruit match a hearty tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown sliced Italian sausage with garlic, add crushed tomatoes and simmer 20 minutes, then toss with penne and grated cheese.
