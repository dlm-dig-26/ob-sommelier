About this wine

Barefoot started in 1965 in Alameda County, California, and has been part of E. & J. Gallo since 2005; Barefoot Cellars is based in Modesto. This Chardonnay is produced and bottled in California. It is smooth and more dry than sweet, with crisp green apple, ripe peach, honey and a touch of vanilla, and a soft finish. It is sold in many sizes, from small 187 ml bottles up to 1.5 litre bottles.

On the shelf: Look for the Barefoot footprint on the label; the label colour changes by grape.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 200 ml, 500 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Round, soft fruit and a hint of honey match the melted cheese and mild spices.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam, or a dish lined with cheese slices, with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives and raisins; cover with cheese and bake until melted.
  2. Grilled red snapper. A smooth, medium-bodied white that suits the firm, sweet flesh of snapper.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole snapper with oil, garlic and herbs, grill 6 to 8 minutes per side and finish with lemon.
  3. Shrimp creole. Peach and apple fruit soften the heat of a tomato and pepper sauce.
    Recipe idea: Cook onion, sweet pepper and celery in oil, add tomato, garlic and hot sauce, simmer 15 minutes and stir in shrimp for the last 4 minutes.
  4. Roast chicken. A classic: buttery roast chicken and a smooth, round Chardonnay.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, lemon and thyme and roast at 200°C for about 1 hour 15 minutes, until the juices run clear.
  5. Chicken Alfredo. Vanilla and a soft texture go well with a creamy parmesan sauce.
    Recipe idea: Cook fettuccine, toss with a sauce of butter, cream and parmesan and top with sliced pan-fried chicken.
