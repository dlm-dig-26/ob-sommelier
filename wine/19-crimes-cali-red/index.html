About this wine

19 Crimes is a wine brand from Treasury Wine Estates, launched in 2012. Its name refers to a list of 19 offences that, in colonial times, could get an English convict transported to Australia, and the brand tells the stories of those rule-breakers. Cali Red, made together with Snoop Dogg, arrived in 2020 as the brand's first California wine. It is a blend of Petite Sirah, Zinfandel and a little Merlot, in a smooth, fruit-forward style that is a touch sweeter than most red wines.

On the shelf: Label shows a portrait of Snoop Dogg; scan it with the Living Wine Labels app and the image moves and tells his story.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Keshi yena. Ripe dark fruit and a hint of sweetness suit the rich cheese and the sweet-savoury raisin filling.
    Recipe idea: Line a dish with Gouda slices, fill with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives, raisins and capers, top with more cheese and bake until golden.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. A full, smooth red with a touch of sweetness tames the cumin and pepper in this goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat with lime, garlic, cumin and pepper; brown it, add onion, tomato and stock and simmer two hours until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. BBQ pork ribs. Jammy fruit and vanilla match a smoky, sweet barbecue glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub ribs with paprika, brown sugar, garlic and salt, bake covered at 150 °C for 2.5 hours, then brush with barbecue sauce and grill until sticky.
  4. Cheeseburger. Soft tannins and dark berry fruit go with juicy beef and melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and melt cheddar on top; serve in toasted buns with onion and pickles.
  5. Chipotle pulled pork tacos. A touch of sweetness calms the chipotle heat while the dark fruit matches the smoky pork.
    Recipe idea: Slow-cook pork shoulder with chipotle, garlic, cumin and orange juice for 6 hours, shred and serve in warm tortillas with onion and cilantro.
