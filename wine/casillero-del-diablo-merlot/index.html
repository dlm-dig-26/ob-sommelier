About this wine

Casillero del Diablo is a range from Concha y Toro, one of Chile's oldest wine companies. The name comes from a legend: Don Melchor de Concha y Toro is said to have spread a rumour that the devil lived in his cellar to keep thieves away from his best wines. This Reserva Merlot comes from Chile's Central Valley, from stony alluvial soils and granite hillsides in a Mediterranean climate cooled by the Andes, and is made with both barrel and stainless steel.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Smooth, low-tannin Merlot sits nicely with chicken in tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, celery and a pinch of cumin, then simmer until tender and saucy. Serve with white rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Plum and soft chocolate notes suit a tender beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Mushroom risotto. Silky texture and red berries match an earthy, creamy rice.
    Recipe idea: Sauté onion and mushrooms, toast arborio rice, add stock a ladle at a time until creamy, then stir in butter and parmesan.
  4. Lasagne. Medium body and soft tannins work with layers of meat sauce and cheese.
    Recipe idea: Layer pasta sheets with beef ragu, béchamel and mozzarella, finish with parmesan and bake at 180C for 40 minutes.
  5. Pork chops. A smooth red with ripe fruit flatters juicy pork.
    Recipe idea: Season pork chops with salt, pepper and thyme, pan-sear 4 minutes a side and finish with a knob of butter and garlic.
