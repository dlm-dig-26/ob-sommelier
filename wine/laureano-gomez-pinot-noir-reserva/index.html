About this wine

Laureano Gomez worked as a viticulturist and then spent more than 20 years as a winemaker at two well-known Argentine wineries before starting his own small family project in 2010. It began in the family home, with tanks in the garage. He buys grapes from growers in the Uco Valley, works with steel tanks and whole berries, and ages his wines in French oak before a rest in bottle. This Pinot Noir Reserva comes from the Uco Valley and shows cherry, strawberry and an earthy, mushroom note.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Galinja stoba. Fresh acidity and red fruit lift chicken stewed with tomato.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, then stew with onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a little soy or Worcestershire sauce for about 45 minutes. Serve with rice.
  2. Keshi yena. A silky red that balances the cheese and gentle spices.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam, or a dish lined with cheese slices, with chicken stewed with onion, tomato, olives and raisins; cover with cheese and bake until melted.
  3. Roast chicken. Cherry fruit and light oak suit herb roast chicken.
    Recipe idea: Rub a whole chicken with butter, garlic, lemon and thyme and roast at 200°C for about 1 hour 15 minutes, until the juices run clear.
  4. Grilled salmon. A medium-light body and gentle spice match rich salmon.
    Recipe idea: Brush salmon fillets with oil, soy sauce and a little honey and grill or pan-fry 4 minutes per side.
  5. Roast pork. Soft tannins and red berries go well with roast pork.
    Recipe idea: Rub a pork loin with garlic, cumin, orange zest and salt and roast at 190°C for about 1 hour, then rest 10 minutes.
