About this wine

Barefoot is a California wine brand based in Modesto. Its Merlot is made and bottled in California; a UK listing shows mostly Merlot with small amounts of Petit Verdot, Petite Sirah and other grapes. The producer calls it rich and lush, with boysenberry, cherry and chocolate. Retailers describe it as smooth and medium-bodied with subtle tannins and a soft, fruity touch, an easy red for casual meals.

Bottle sizes made: 187 ml, 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi with beef. Soft cherry fruit and gentle tannins suit the spiced filling and fried pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with spiced minced beef, raisins and olives, fold into half-moons, seal and deep-fry until golden.
  2. Galinja stoba. A smooth, fruity Merlot works with chicken in a mild tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and a pinch of cumin, and simmer for 45 minutes.
  3. Grilled burgers. Ripe fruit and a hint of chocolate are an easy match for a juicy burger.
    Recipe idea: Shape seasoned minced beef into patties, grill 4 minutes per side and serve on buns with tomato, onion and cheese.
  4. Pasta with meat sauce. A soft red carries tomato and minced beef without fuss.
    Recipe idea: Brown minced beef with onion and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and oregano, simmer 30 minutes and toss with spaghetti.
  5. Barbecue chicken. The slightly sweet fruit of the wine echoes a smoky, sweet glaze.
    Recipe idea: Marinate chicken thighs in smoked paprika, garlic and a little brown sugar, then grill over medium heat for 25 minutes.
