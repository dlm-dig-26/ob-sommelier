About this wine

Lunetta is a Prosecco made by Cavit, a cooperative in Trentino that started out as a consortium of growers. This Prosecco DOC is 100% Glera from the hills of Treviso in the Veneto, between the Piave river and the Prealps. After a first fermentation in stainless steel, the second fermentation takes place in tank (the Charmat method), followed by three months on fine lees. It is light and fruity, with apple and peach, ripe stone fruit and a soft, lasting mousse.

Bottle sizes made: 200 ml, 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Pastechi. Crisp bubbles refresh the palate after salty, fried pastry.
    Recipe idea: Fill rounds of pastry dough with spiced ground beef, cheese or tuna, fold into half moons, seal the edges and deep-fry until golden.
  2. Ceviche. Green apple freshness and a dry finish suit lime-cured fish.
    Recipe idea: Cube fresh white fish, cover with lime juice for 15 minutes, then mix with red onion, cilantro, a little hot pepper and salt.
  3. Keshi yena. Bubbles and acidity lighten rich, melted cheese.
    Recipe idea: Fill a hollowed Edam or Gouda with spiced chicken, olives, raisins and capers, cover with cheese slices and bake at 180°C for 30 to 40 minutes.
  4. Sushi rolls. A light, dry Prosecco fits delicate fish and rice.
    Recipe idea: Roll seasoned sushi rice and nori around cucumber, avocado and crab or salmon, slice and serve with soy sauce and pickled ginger.
  5. Garlic shrimp. White peach and crisp bubbles balance garlic and olive oil.
    Recipe idea: Sauté peeled shrimp in olive oil with plenty of garlic, chili flakes and parsley for 2 to 3 minutes, finish with lemon juice.
