About this wine

Durigutti Family Winemakers is run by two brothers in Las Compuertas, Luján de Cuyo, which the winery calls the coolest part of the area. In 2007 they bought their first 5 hectares of historic Malbec vines on Callejón de la Reta and later grew to 34 hectares, named Finca Victoria after their mother. Their Proyecto Las Compuertas line revives heritage varieties and local traditions. This Cordisco, the grape known as Montepulciano in its native Abruzzo, comes from an experimental plot on the estate and is fermented in concrete eggs with native yeasts. Retail notes describe a fresh, soft red with red cherry, roses and herbs such as thyme.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Lively acidity and cherry fruit cut through the rich tomato and beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes with onion, garlic and sweet pepper, add tomato, cumin and a little vinegar, and simmer until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Galinja stoba. Herbal notes and fresh acidity suit the savory, gently spiced chicken stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and a little soy sauce, and simmer until the meat falls off the bone.
  3. Spaghetti bolognese. An Italian grape at heart, its acidity loves a tomato and meat sauce.
    Recipe idea: Cook ground beef with onion, carrot, celery and garlic, add crushed tomatoes and simmer 45 minutes. Serve over spaghetti with parmesan.
  4. Pizza margherita. Red cherry and bright acidity match tomato, basil and mozzarella.
    Recipe idea: Top pizza dough with crushed tomatoes, mozzarella and a drizzle of olive oil, bake very hot, and finish with fresh basil.
  5. Grilled sausages with peppers. Its herbal, earthy side works well with juicy, smoky sausages.
    Recipe idea: Grill Italian sausages with strips of sweet pepper and onion until charred, and serve in rolls or with crusty bread.
