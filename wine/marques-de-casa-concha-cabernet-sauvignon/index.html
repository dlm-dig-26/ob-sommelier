About this wine

Marqués de Casa Concha is a range from Concha y Toro, which marked its 130th anniversary in 2013. This Cabernet Sauvignon comes from two vineyards in the Maipo Valley: Puente Alto, at about 650 metres, and Pirque, at about 570 metres, on poor, rocky alluvial soils. It is aged for 16 months in French oak barrels and a large 5,000 litre foudre. Winemaker Marcelo Papa makes it in a robust, dry style with cassis, black plum, cedar and chocolate. It is suitable for vegans.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Grilled steak. Firm tannins and cassis are made for charred, juicy beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat 3 to 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. A structured red with the weight to match goat stewed with cumin.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then stew slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and a little chili for about two hours until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  3. Grilled lamb chops. Cedar and herbs in the wine echo rosemary on grilled lamb.
    Recipe idea: Marinate lamb chops in olive oil, garlic and rosemary for an hour, then grill 3 minutes per side over high heat.
  4. Carni stoba. Dark fruit and oak stand up to a deep, slow-cooked beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, garlic and a splash of vinegar, and simmer about two hours until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  5. Aged cheese board. Aged cheese softens the tannins and brings out the fruit.
    Recipe idea: Serve aged Gouda, manchego and cheddar at room temperature with walnuts, dried figs and crusty bread.
