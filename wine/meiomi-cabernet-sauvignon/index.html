About this wine

Meiomi was created by Joe Wagner, whose family runs Caymus in Napa Valley. He developed the brand in 2006 and released the first wines in 2009, starting with Pinot Noir; a Chardonnay followed in 2014. In 2015 Meiomi was sold to Constellation Brands. This Cabernet Sauvignon blends grapes from several California growing regions, including coastal ones, picked at full ripeness to balance richness and freshness. It is a deep ruby, full-bodied red with blackberry, boysenberry and toasty vanilla, a plush texture and a long finish.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Slow-cooked goat stew matches the plush dark fruit and soft, ripe tannins of this Cabernet.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer two hours with onion, tomato, garlic, cumin and a splash of the wine; serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Carni stoba. The velvety texture and vanilla notes sit well with a tender, savory beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, sweet pepper, tomato, capers and olives, cover with stock and simmer until soft.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Fat and char from the grill soften the tannins and bring out the blackberry fruit.
    Recipe idea: Rub with salt, pepper and garlic, grill on high heat 4 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes and slice.
  4. Barbecue pork ribs. Jammy fruit stands up to a smoky, lightly sweet barbecue glaze.
    Recipe idea: Bake the ribs covered at low heat for 2 hours, brush with barbecue sauce and finish on the grill until glazed.
  5. Mushroom cheeseburger. Earthy mushrooms and melted cheese echo the toasty, graham cracker side of the wine.
    Recipe idea: Top a beef patty with sauteed mushrooms and onions, add a slice of cheese and serve on a toasted bun.
