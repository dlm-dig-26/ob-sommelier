About this wine

Gallo Family Vineyards is a label of E. & J. Gallo Winery, the company brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo founded in 1933 in Modesto, California, and which is still owned by the family. This Sauvignon Blanc is made from California grapes and fermented in stainless steel rather than oak, which keeps it light and crisp. Expect honeydew melon, citrus and ripe apple with lively acidity and a clean, light finish. It is an easygoing, fresh white for everyday meals, sold in standard 750 ml bottles and in larger 1.5 liter bottles.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml, 1,5 L. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Ceviche. Bright citrus and crisp acidity echo the lime in the fish and keep each bite fresh.
    Recipe idea: Dice firm white fish, cover with lime juice, add red onion, chili, cilantro and salt; chill 20 minutes until opaque.
  2. Pisca hasa. Fresh citrus notes lift fried fish and balance the crispy coating.
    Recipe idea: Season snapper fillets with lime, garlic and salt, dust in flour and pan fry until golden; serve with pan bati and Creole sauce.
  3. Grilled red snapper. A light, zesty white suits delicate grilled fish without covering its flavor.
    Recipe idea: Score a whole snapper, rub with garlic, lime and herbs, and grill 6 to 8 minutes per side; finish with olive oil.
  4. Goat cheese salad. Tangy cheese and crisp greens match the green apple notes and lively acidity of the wine.
    Recipe idea: Toss mixed greens with apple slices, crumbled goat cheese and toasted nuts; dress with lemon juice, olive oil and a pinch of salt.
  5. Pasta with pesto. Basil and the citrus freshness of the wine balance the richness of olive oil and cheese.
    Recipe idea: Blend basil, garlic, pine nuts, parmesan and olive oil; toss with hot spaghetti and a splash of pasta water.
