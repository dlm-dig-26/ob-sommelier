About this wine

Marcelo Pelleriti Wines is the personal label of Marcelo Pelleriti, a winemaker born in Mendoza who made his first wine in 1994, led the cellar at Bodega Monteviejo and has also worked in Bordeaux. His own vineyards sit in the Uco Valley, with an old plot in La Consulta and a younger one in Altamira. Blend of Terroir brings together fruit from different Uco Valley sites into one red, built to show the region's dark fruit and freshness.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. A full red with firm tannins and dark fruit holds up to rich, spiced goat.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, then simmer slowly with onion, garlic, tomato, sweet pepper, cumin and a little hot pepper until tender. Serve with funchi or rice.
  2. Carni stoba. Dark fruit and structure match a long-simmered beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef chunks, add onion, garlic, tomato paste, bell pepper, a splash of vinegar and stock, then stew for two hours until soft. Serve with rice or funchi.
  3. Grilled ribeye. Firm tannins soften against the fat and char of a good steak.
    Recipe idea: Season a thick ribeye with salt and pepper, grill over high heat for 4 minutes a side, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Lamb chops with rosemary. Violet and spice notes suit herby, rich lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, olive oil and salt, grill or pan-sear 3 minutes a side and rest briefly before serving.
  5. Aged gouda. A structured red pairs well with a nutty, firm aged cheese.
    Recipe idea: Serve slices of aged gouda with dried fruit, nuts and crackers at room temperature.
