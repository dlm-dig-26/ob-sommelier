About this wine

This limited-edition red blend from 19 Crimes was made in partnership with Universal Monsters and is themed on the Bride of Frankenstein. It is made in South Eastern Australia. It is a non-vintage blend, rich and round in style, with sweet fruit aromas, notes of chocolate and a soft, fruity finish. It comes in a standard 750 ml bottle.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Rich, round fruit suits the deep flavours of a slow-cooked beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. A soft, fruity red that smooths out the warm spice of goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat in lime, garlic and pepper, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, cumin and a little Scotch bonnet for 2 to 3 hours.
  3. Barbecue ribs. Sweet fruit aromas pair easily with a sweet and smoky glaze.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, brown sugar and salt, bake wrapped in foil at low heat for 2 hours, then glaze with barbecue sauce on the grill.
  4. Cheeseburger. A round, easygoing red for a juicy burger night.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season with salt and pepper, grill 3 to 4 minutes per side and top with cheddar, onion and tomato.
  5. Chocolate brownies. Chocolate notes in the wine echo a rich brownie that is not too sweet.
    Recipe idea: Melt dark chocolate with butter, stir in sugar, eggs and a little flour, and bake at 175°C for about 25 minutes until just set.
