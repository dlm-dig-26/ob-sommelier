About this wine

Colección Malbec is made by Rutini Wines in Mendoza from 100 percent Malbec grown in the Uco Valley. The wine spends around 12 months in French oak barrels, which adds hints of vanilla and spice to aromas of plum, morello cherry, anise, black pepper and violet. The winemaker describes a violet red colour with bluish tints and silky, friendly tannins, and an importer's notes call it concentrated and balanced with a long finish. In general, Uco Valley Malbec keeps deep colour and fresh fruit thanks to its high vineyards and cool nights.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Ripe plum fruit and smooth tannins wrap around tender spiced goat, and the oak spice echoes the cumin.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin, cloves and a little hot pepper, cover with stock and simmer about 2 hours.
  2. Keshi yena. Soft tannins and dark fruit balance the melted Gouda and the sweet and salty filling.
    Recipe idea: Fill a Gouda lined dish with chicken stewed with tomato, onion, raisins, olives and capers, cover with cheese and bake until golden.
  3. Beef empanadas. A Mendoza classic: juicy spiced beef suits the wine's ripe plum and gentle vanilla notes.
    Recipe idea: Fill pastry rounds with ground beef, onion, paprika, cumin, chopped egg and olives, seal the edges and bake 20 minutes at 200 C.
  4. Rosemary lamb chops. Lamb's richness and the herbs meet the wine's dark fruit and smooth, rounded tannins.
    Recipe idea: Rub lamb chops with garlic, rosemary, olive oil and salt, grill or pan fry 3 minutes per side and rest before serving.
  5. Spaghetti bolognese. Tomato and slow cooked meat sauce match a plush Malbec with balanced acidity.
    Recipe idea: Simmer ground beef with onion, carrot, celery, garlic, tomato and a splash of red wine for 1 hour, then toss with spaghetti and parmesan.
