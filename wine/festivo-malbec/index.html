About this wine

Festivo Malbec comes from Bodega Monteviejo in Vista Flores, Tunuyán, the first winery built in the Clos de los Siete project that Michel Rolland started in the Uco Valley. The estate, created in 1998, was led by Catherine Péré-Vergé of Château Le Gay in Bordeaux and is now run by her son Henry Parent. For this wine the grapes are picked by hand at around 1,000 metres, cold-soaked for four days, fermented in stainless steel tanks and rested briefly on the lees before bottling. It is a ruby Malbec with raspberry, cherry and blueberry, silky tannins, good freshness and 14 percent alcohol.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Soft tannins and juicy red fruit match the slow-cooked beef and its rich sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes with onion, garlic and sweet pepper, add tomato, cumin and a little vinegar, and simmer until tender. Serve with rice or funchi.
  2. Stobá di kabritu. Bright cherry fruit and freshness balance the spices and the depth of the goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown goat pieces, add onion, garlic, tomato, cumin, nutmeg and hot pepper, then simmer gently for about 2 hours until tender.
  3. Grilled steak. A classic match: the fruit and silky tannins suit charred, juicy beef.
    Recipe idea: Season a steak with salt and pepper, grill over high heat to your liking, rest 5 minutes and serve with chimichurri.
  4. Beef burgers. Its fruit and medium body work well with grilled meat and smoky toppings.
    Recipe idea: Shape ground beef into patties, season and grill. Serve in buns with cheese, tomato, onion and lettuce.
  5. Mushroom pizza. Red berry fruit and gentle tannins suit earthy mushrooms and tomato sauce.
    Recipe idea: Spread tomato sauce on pizza dough, top with mozzarella and sliced mushrooms, and bake at a high heat until crisp.
