About this wine

Josh Cellars was founded in 2007 by Joseph Carr, a former sommelier and wine executive, who named it as a tribute to his father Josh. The brand began with a single Cabernet Sauvignon and today draws fruit from across California. In 2021 Wine Enthusiast named Josh Cellars its American Winery of the Year. Legacy is the house red blend: rich dark fruit with a hint of toasted wood and cracked pepper, made to be easy to enjoy with food.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Stobá di kabritu. Dark fruit and a peppery edge echo the cumin and pepper in goat stew.
    Recipe idea: Marinate goat in lime, garlic and pepper, brown it, then simmer with onion, tomato, cumin and a little Scotch bonnet for 2 to 3 hours.
  2. Carni stoba. A dry, balanced red with enough body for a long-simmered beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes, add onion, garlic, sweet pepper, tomato and cumin, pour in a splash of wine and simmer covered for about 2 hours.
  3. Barbecue ribs. Plum and toasted oak sit well with smoky, charred pork.
    Recipe idea: Rub pork ribs with paprika, brown sugar and salt, bake wrapped in foil at low heat for 2 hours, then glaze with barbecue sauce on the grill.
  4. Grilled steak. Medium tannins and dark fruit suit a juicy, well-seasoned grilled steak.
    Recipe idea: Season a ribeye with salt, pepper and garlic, grill over high heat 4 minutes per side and let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
  5. Pepperoni pizza. Fresh acidity handles tomato sauce while the fruit stands up to spicy pepperoni.
    Recipe idea: Spread pizza dough with tomato sauce, top with mozzarella and pepperoni, and bake in a very hot oven for 10 to 12 minutes.
