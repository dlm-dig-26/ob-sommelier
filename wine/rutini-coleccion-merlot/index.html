About this wine

Rutini Wines traces its history to Felipe Rutini, an Italian immigrant who founded the La Rural winery in Coquimbito, Maipú, Mendoza, in 1885. His family planted vineyards in the Uco Valley in 1924, and the winery now works with high-altitude Uco Valley fruit under winemaker Mariano Di Paola. This Merlot comes from the Uco Valley and is aged for 12 months, with about half the wine in French oak barrels. Expect blackberry and other red and black fruit with cocoa and tobacco, and a full, refined palate.

Bottle sizes made: 750 ml. Stores in Aruba may carry only some of them.

What to eat with it

  1. Carni stoba. Velvety tannins and plum fruit sit well with a tender, tomato-rich beef stew.
    Recipe idea: Brown beef cubes with onion and garlic, add tomato, sweet pepper, a little soy sauce and stock, and simmer gently until tender. Serve with rice.
  2. Galinja stoba. A smooth Merlot is gentle enough for stewed chicken yet matches its savoury sauce.
    Recipe idea: Brown chicken pieces seasoned with garlic and lime, then simmer with onion, tomato, peppers and a dash of ketchup until cooked through.
  3. Roast leg of lamb. Dark fruit and tobacco notes flatter the herbs and richness of lamb.
    Recipe idea: Rub a leg of lamb with garlic, rosemary, salt and oil, roast until pink inside and rest before carving. Serve with roast potatoes.
  4. Mushroom risotto. Earthy mushrooms bring out the cocoa and oak of this Merlot.
    Recipe idea: Toast arborio rice in butter with onion, add stock little by little, stir in sauteed mushrooms and finish with parmesan.
  5. Beef fillet with red wine sauce. Its refined, full palate suits a lean fillet with a glossy reduction.
    Recipe idea: Sear a beef fillet and finish in the oven. Deglaze the pan with red wine and stock, reduce, and whisk in a knob of butter.
